Overview

ACME Printing and Packaging PLC recorded revenue of Rs. 378.4 Mn for the six months
ended September 2026, a 27% decline against the previous year. Despite lower revenue, gross
profit improved significantly to Rs. 73.6 Mn from Rs. 18.4 Mn, reflecting a 299% increase.
The Group continued to report an operating loss, although this reduced by 52% to Rs. 40.0 Mn.
Loss after tax also improved considerably, declining from Rs. 165.4 Mn to Rs. 14.9 Mn. The
period included a Rs. 1.425 Bn Rights Issue, which happened in the previuos Financial Year,
and the disposal of the Piliyandala property for Rs. 630 Mn, supporting the Group’s financial
restructuring and operational plans.

Operating Environment

The period was characterised by significant financial and operational restructuring. The
disposal of the Piliyandala land and factory premises to Singer Sri Lanka PLC for Rs. 630 Mn
was completed during the quarter. The Group also continued utilising proceeds from its Rs.
1.425 Bn Rights Issue to settle intercompany loans, reduce bank borrowings, support working
capital and fund factory relocation and modernisation. By 30 September 2026, approximately
Rs. 1.244 Bn of the proceeds had been utilised. These initiatives indicate the Group’s focus on
strengthening its financial position and improving operational efficiency.

Financial Performance

Group revenue declined by 27% to Rs. 378.4 Mn, while gross profit increased by 299% to Rs.
73.6 Mn, supported by a 39% reduction in cost of sales. Administrative and distribution
expenses also declined by 10% and 54%, respectively. Further, other expences include Rs. 59.7
Mn loss on sale of Piliyandala property. Consequently, the operating loss narrowed by 52% to
Rs. 40.0 Mn. Net finance expense reduced significantly by 70% to Rs. 25.6 Mn, contributing
to a 61% reduction in loss before tax. Loss after tax improved by 91% to Rs. 14.9 Mn. Total
assets increased to Rs. 2.298 Bn, while cash and cash equivalents stood at Rs. 791.2 Mn.

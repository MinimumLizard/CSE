                   Period Ended 30th June 2025




Balance Sheet Analysis
TOTAL ASSETS
Total Assets increased by 2% to LKR 19,467 million as of June 30, 2025 compared to LKR 19,028 million in March 31, 2025.
Total Non-Current Assets LKR 18,050 million as of June 2025 and there has been a slight increase of 0.02% with the balance as of March 31, 2024.
Total Current Assets showcased an increase of 44% to LKR 1,416 million compared to LKR 982 million as of March 31, 2025



TOTAL SHAREHOLDERS’ FUNDS (NET ASSETS)
Total Shareholders’ Funds increased by 12% to LKR 10,937 million at the end of June 30, 2025 compared to LKR 9,804 million recorded at the end of
March 31, 2025.



NET ASSET VALUE PER SHARE (NAVPS)
The NAVPS was LKR 35.77 as of end June 2025 compared to LKR 36.19 per share recorded for the financial year ended March 2025.



LOAN TO VALUE (LTV)
The LTV recorded for the quarter ended June 2025 was 20.72%, decreased from the 25.28% recorded for the financial year ending March 2025.




P&L Analysis

REVENUE | EXPENSES | OPERATIONAL PROFIT
Overall consolidated revenue decreased by 7% to LKR 292 million during the quarter ended June 2025 compared to LKR 313 million during the
corresponding quarter of the previous financial year.
Direct expenses demonstrated a decrease of 36% to LKR 191 million for the quarter ended June 2025 compared to LKR 298 million during the comparable
period in the previous year.
The operational profit recorded for the quarter was LKR 11 million compared to the operating loss of LKR 66 million recorded during the quarter
comparable period in the previous year.



PROFIT/LOSS AFTER TAX
The Group recorded a loss of LKR 74 million for the quarter ended June 2025 compared to the loss of LKR 187 million recorded in the corresponding
quarter of the previous year.




EARNINGS PER SHARE (EPS)
The EPS was LKR -0.43 per share for the quarter ended June 2025, compared to LKR -0.95 per share in the corresponding period of the previous year.

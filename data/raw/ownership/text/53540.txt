 MANAGING DIRECTOR’S MESSAGE
Dear Shareholders,

WindForce PLC continued to deliver strong financial and operational performance during the first six months of FY 2026/27, recording
growth in revenue, gross profit and profitability.
Overall Financial Performance 6M - FY 2026/27
•    Consolidated Revenue: LKR 5,610 million – a 20% increase from LKR 4,680 million

•    Gross Profit: LKR 3,212 million - a 12% increase from LKR 2,867 million

•    Profit After Tax (PAT): LKR 1,857 million - a 5% increase from LKR 1,768 million
A revenue loss of LKR 193.57 million was incurred due to curtailment during the six-month period. Following discussions with the relevant
authorities, PUCSL has approved a methodology and compensation framework for curtailed renewable energy and has directed the NSO to
compensate renewable energy generators based on the applicable energy purchase tariff under the respective SPPAs. However, discussions
among the relevant authorities are continuing. Accordingly, the timing and recovery of compensation for curtailment remain uncertain at this
stage.
Segmental Performance 3M - FY 2026/27
•    Solar - LKR 1,658 million - up 23% year-on-year from LKR 1,345 million.

•    Wind - LKR 2,395 million – up 7% year-on-year from LKR 2,231 million.

•    Hydro - LKR 307 million – down 19% year-on-year from LKR 381 million.

Financing Milestone

In September 2026, WindForce PLC successfully received the first tranche of USD 5 million (LKR equivalent) under its USD 18 million
financing facility with the International Finance Corporation (IFC), a member of the World Bank Group, marking a significant financing mile-
stone for the Company and reinforcing its ability to secure long-term international funding to support its renewable energy growth strategy.

Pipeline Projects and Key Milestones:
Rividhanavi 100 MW Solar Power Project - Siyambalanduwa
Financial Closure: The project achieved financial closure in September 2026, securing LKR 22.736 billion and LKR equivalent of USD 33
million in syndicated term loan facilities and bank guarantee facilities through a banking consortium led by Sampath Bank PLC, together with
Commercial Bank of Ceylon PLC, National Development Bank PLC and Nations Trust Bank PLC.
Solar Park Facility: Civil construction activities are progressing as planned. Procurement has advanced, with several key components already
delivered to site and the remaining equipment under order.
Transmission Facility: Construction of the transmission infrastructure is progressing steadily. Foundation works for the 132/33kV transmis-
sion line have been completed, with tower erection in progress and preparations underway for conductor stringing. Civil construction of the
132/33kV grid substation is also advancing.

Battery Energy Storage Systems – 130 MW/520 MWh - 13 locations
In September 2026, WindForce commissioned the 10 MW/40 MWh Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) in Anuradhapura , marking the
first completed facility within the Company’s portfolio of 13 standalone BESS projects totalling 130 MW/520 MWh, secured under Sri Lanka’s
first grid-scale BESS initiative led by the National System Operator (Pvt) Ltd. Development of the remaining 12 projects is progressing,
with completion targeted by December 2026.

Safe Power International (Pvt) Ltd – 10 MW Wind Power Project – Puttalam
Further to the extension granted until May 2027 following the force majeure event caused by Cyclone Ditwah, the Company continues to
progress development activities in line with the revised project schedule.

Following the completion of the plant design and placement of orders for the major equipment, civil construction works have now
commenced at the project site, while procurement, logistics and other project activities continue to progress.


Diya Janani (Pvt) Ltd – 5 MW Floating Solar Project – Dambulu Oya Reservoir
In September 2026, WindForce PLC commissioned the 5 MW Diya Janani Floating Solar Power Project at the Dambulu Oya Reservoir. The
project, one of Sri Lanka’s pioneering private-sector floating solar developments, further strengthens the Company’s diversified renewable
energy portfolio while promoting efficient land utilisation.

     QUARTERLY FINANCIAL REVIEW – 2026/27 Q2
   WindForce PLC (WF) maintains a positive outlook on Sri Lanka’s renewable energy sector, supported by favourable policy
   momentum and continued investment in renewable energy and battery storage projects.


   This quarterly financial review compares the Group’s performance for the three and six months ended 30 September 2026 with
   the corresponding periods of the previous year, thereby accounting for seasonality and improving the comparability of results.
   It also examines significant cash and non-cash items recorded during both periods to better reflect WF’s underlying operating
   performance and free cash flow generation.


   Capacity Mix (MW)

                                                                                 Total capacity of WF is depicted in the graph to
                                                                                 the left, along-with the segmented capacity mix by
                                                                                 wind power, hydro power, solar power and BESS.

                                                                                 Total generating capacity of subsidiaries increased by
                                                                                 10.4% year-on-year to 159.5 MW in 6M of FY 2026/27
                                                                                 contributing to the growth in consolidated revenue, whilst
                                                                                 the total capacity  of the portfolio increased to 267.8 MW.

                                                                                 The net increase of 15 MW (Ie. 10.4% YoY growth) in subsidiaries is
                                                                                 attributable to the commissioning of Diyajanani Floating Solar Power
                                                                                 Plant as of September 2026 with 5 MW capacity and the Battery
                                                                                 Energy Storage System (BESS) in Anuradhapura as of September
                                                                                 2026 the first completed facility within the portfolio of thirteen
                                                                                 standalone BESS projects (130 MW/520 MWh) secured under
                                                                                 Sri Lanka’s first grid-scale BESS initiative.




   Generation Mix (GWh)

                                                                                 Total power generation [in GWh] of subsidiaries reflected in consolidated revenue
                                                                                 of WF is depicted in the graph to the left, along-with the segmented power
                                                                                 generation mix [in 6M of 2026/27] by wind power, hydro power, and solar power.

                                                                                 In 6M of 2026/27, total power generation from subsidiaries increased by 4%
                                                                                 year-on-year, while capacity grew by 10.4% over the same period.
                                                                                 Although capacity additions were recorded in both solar and BESS,
                                                                                 generation growth was primarily driven by solar and improved performance
                                                                                 across the existing portfolio. The Anuradhapura BESS facility was
                                                                                 inaugurated in September 2026; however, as commercial operations
                                                                                 commenced from 1 October 2026, no BESS generation or related
                                                                                 revenue was recorded for the period ended 30 September 2026. Regular
                                                                                 maintenance also supported stable operations across the portfolio.

                                                                                 It is important to note that the increase in generation was achieved amidst
                                                                                 curtailments imposed by the National System Operator (NSO) to maintain grid
                                                                                 stability during weekends, primarily due to periods of low electricity demand.



Tarriff Performance

For the 6M ended in FY 2026/27, the positive impact of higher tariffs from newly commissioned plants marginally outweighed the effects of tariff
revisions in older plants. This outcome was primarily driven by management’s effective and efficient capital allocation within the competitive industry
landscape in which company operates.

Review Summary – 6M of FY 2026/27 Financial Performance

For the six months ended FY 2026/27, WindForce PLC delivered a strong and well-balanced operating and financial performance, driven by increased
power generation from recently commissioned capacity and improved plant availability across the existing portfolio. Higher tariffs from new projects
helped offset tariff revisions applicable to older assets, supporting year-on-year growth in recurring revenue. Supported by improving domestic macro-
economic conditions, the Company’s performance across capacity expansion, energy generation, revenue growth and profitability remained aligned with
its medium-term strategic objectives.

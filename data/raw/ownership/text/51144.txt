  1   Corporate Information
      1.1 General
          InsureMe Insurance Brokers PLC is a public limited company incorporated and Domiciled in Sri
          Lanka under Companies Act 07 of 2007 and the ordinary shares of the Company are listed on the
          Colombo Stock Exchange. The Registered office of the Company is Located at No.30/3, Bagatalle
          Road, Colombo 3, Sri Lanka.
      1.2 Principal Activities and Nature of Operations
          The principal activity of the Company is carrying on business as an insurance broker, offering Life,
          General Insurance and Reinsurance solutions to corporate and individual customers, and mana-
          ging and holding investments in the insuretech industry.
      1.3 Consolidated Financial Statements
          The ﬁnancial statements for the Year ended 31st March 2026, comprise “the company” referring
          to InsureMe Insurance Brokers PLC as the holding company, and ‘’ the Group’’ including its sole
          subsidiary Digital Services Global Pvt Ltd ("Digis"), whose accounts have been consolidated
          therein.
      1.4 Approval of Financial Statements

  5   Events after the reporting period
      All material events after the reporting period have been considered and where appropriate
      adjustments or disclosures have been made in the respective notes to the ﬁnancial statements.

  6   Contingent Liabilities
      The Department of Inland Revenue (IRD) has issued assessments against the Company in respect of
      the Social Security Contribution Levy (SSCL), covering 12 quarterly periods from January 2023 to
      December 2025 (Period Codes 2310 to 2540). These assessments apply SSCL at 2.5% on the
      Company’s insurance brokerage income, together with applicable penalties.
      The total assessed exposure, including SSCL and penalties, amounts to Rs. 33,451,328/-.
      The Company has ﬁled formal appeals against these assessments and maintains the position that it is
      not liable for SSCL, based on the current interpretation of applicable SSCL laws in Sri Lanka.

  7   Finance Cost
      Finance cost on lease liabilities represents the unwinding of the discounting effect recognised in
      accordance with SLFRS 16 Lease standard.

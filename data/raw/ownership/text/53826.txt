Performance Review for the Six Months Ended 30 September 2026
Overview

The Group delivered a strong performance during the six months ended 30 September 2026,
achieving significant growth in revenue and profitability despite a challenging and evolving
operating environment.

During the period under review, the Group continued its positive growth trajectory and
delivered a strong financial performance. Supported by this performance and the Group's
healthy cash generation capabilities, the Board of Directors approved and paid an interim
dividend amounting to Rs. 86 million during September 2026, reaffirming its commitment to
creating consistent shareholder value while preserving the financial strength required to pursue
future growth opportunities.

Consolidated revenue increased by 26% to Rs. 2.68 billion compared to Rs. 2.13 billion in the
corresponding period of the previous year. Profit Before Tax rose substantially to Rs. 372.6
million from Rs. 121.1 million, while Profit After Tax reached Rs. 246.2 million, representing
an increase of 234% over the comparative period. Earnings per share improved to Rs. 1.43
from Rs. 0.61 in the previous year.

The improvement in performance was underpinned by healthy volume growth across the
Group's core businesses, enhanced operational efficiencies, disciplined cost management and
an improved product mix. Gross profit increased by 60% to Rs. 836.8 million, significantly
exceeding revenue growth, resulting in a marked expansion of margins and demonstrating the
effectiveness of the strategic initiatives implemented throughout the Group.

While distribution and administrative expenses increased in line with business expansion, the
Group successfully leveraged its operating scale and manufacturing efficiencies to deliver
superior profitability. Net finance costs remained broadly stable compared to the previous year
despite increased funding requirements and prevailing market interest rates.



Operating Environment

The period under review was characterised by fluctuations in global raw material markets,
ongoing geopolitical developments and supply chain uncertainties that continued to influence
key raw material pricings, freight costs and procurement lead times. In Sri Lanka, businesses
continued to navigate changing market conditions, cost pressures and currency-related
challenges.

Against this backdrop, the Group maintained its focus on operational excellence, customer
service, product innovation and prudent financial management. Strategic procurement
initiatives, expanded stockholdings, manufacturing optimisation programmes and continued
investments in operational capabilities enabled the Group to capitalise on market opportunities
while mitigating external challenges.

Revenue

Group revenue increased by 26% to Rs. 2.68 billion, reflecting improved demand across both
operating segments together with the successful execution of customer-focused growth
initiatives. The performance demonstrates the resilience of the Group's diversified packaging
portfolio and its strong positioning within key end-user sectors.

Gross Profit

Gross profit increased by 60% to Rs. 836.8 million from Rs. 522.5 million in the corresponding
period last year. Gross margin improved from 24.5% to 31.2%, driven by better product mix,
manufacturing efficiencies, waste reduction initiatives and effective cost management.

Profitability

Profit Before Tax increased to Rs. 372.6 million from Rs. 121.1 million during the
corresponding period, representing growth of 208%. Profit After Tax increased to Rs. 246.2
million compared to Rs. 73.7 million in the previous year. The substantial improvement reflects
the benefits of higher volumes, enhanced margins and improved operating leverage across the
Group.



Segmental Performance
Packaging Segment

The Packaging segment remained the Group's largest contributor to revenue and profitability
during the period.

Segment revenue increased by 25% to Rs. 1.98 billion from Rs. 1.58 billion recorded in the
corresponding period of the previous year. Segment Proft Before Taxation improved
significantly to Rs. 243.2 million compared to Rs. 20.8 million achieved in the previous year.

The strong performance was driven by higher customer demand, improved capacity utilisation
and continued operational excellence across manufacturing operations. Against a backdrop of
market disruptions and supply uncertainties, the Company's ability to consistently deliver on
customer requirements enhanced its reputation as a reliable and trusted supplier. As a result,
the business benefited from increased order placements from existing customers and
successfully secured new business opportunities previously serviced by competitors. The
resulting growth, coupled with product mix optimisation, productivity enhancements and
effective cost control measures, contributed to a significant improvement in segment
profitability.


Injection and Blow Moulding Segment

The Injection and Blow Moulding segment continued its positive growth trajectory during the
period under review.

Revenue increased by 24% to Rs. 766.3 million compared to Rs. 618.8 million in the previous
year. Segment Profit Before Taxation increased by 29% to Rs. 129.5 million from Rs. 100.3
million achieved during the comparative period.
The segment's performance was supported by growing demand from beverage, FMCG and
industrial sectors, coupled with ongoing improvements in manufacturing efficiency and
product quality.

Shareholders' equity increased to Rs. 1.80 billion from Rs. 1.64 billion at the beginning of the
financial year, supported by strong earnings generated during the period. Net Asset Value per
share improved to Rs. 10.48 from Rs. 9.55.

The Group continued to maintain adequate liquidity while supporting business growth and
strategic capital investments. Operating cash flows improved significantly compared with the
previous year and provided support for ongoing expansion initiatives.


Capital Investment and Operational Development

During the period, the Group continued to invest in manufacturing assets, productivity
enhancement initiatives and operational improvements aimed at supporting future growth and
strengthening competitiveness. Property, plant and equipment increased to Rs. 1.08 billion
compared with Rs. 1.03 billion at the beginning of the financial year.

The Group continues to focus on strengthening its manufacturing capabilities while enhancing
operational efficiencies, quality standards and customer service excellence. As part of its long-
term growth strategy, the Group continues to invest in research and development focused on
alternative raw materials, innovative material structures and optimised resin combinations.
Management believes that this commitment to innovation will strengthen the Group's
competitive position by enabling the development of cost-effective, high-performance
packaging solutions that meet evolving customer needs while delivering sustainable value to
stakeholders.

The Group remains committed to maintaining the highest standards of quality, food safety and
operational excellence. J. F. Packaging PLC successfully obtained the BRCGS Packaging
Materials Certification during the period. This achievement reinforces the Company's
commitment to internationally recognised quality and food packaging safety standards, while
enhancing its ability to serve both local and international customers operating under stringent
compliance requirements.



Sustainability and People

The Group persists to integrate sustainability principles into its operations by focusing on
resource efficiency, waste minimisation, environmental stewardship and employee
development. Continuous improvement initiatives remain a key component of the Group's
operational strategy, supporting both long-term competitiveness and responsible business
growth.

The Group continued to advance its ESG framework during the period under review through
the adoption of structured sustainability governance practices aligned with SLFRS S1 and
SLFRS S2. Efforts were focused on strengthening sustainability monitoring, data management
and reporting processes in preparation for the inclusion of ESG disclosures in the FY 2026/27
Annual Report, in line with applicable sustainability reporting requirements.

Outlook

Looking ahead, while uncertainties relating to global economic conditions, raw material price
movements and geopolitical developments may continue to present challenges, the Group
remains cautiously optimistic about its growth prospects.

The Board and Management remain focused on strengthening customer relationships,
expanding value-added product offerings, improving manufacturing efficiencies and pursuing
sustainable growth opportunities in both domestic and export markets. Continued investments
in innovation, technology and operational excellence are expected to further enhance
competitiveness and support profitable growth in the coming periods.

The Group is confident that its diversified business portfolio, strong customer base and
disciplined management approach will enable it to continue creating value for shareholders and
other stakeholders.

NOTES TO THE FINANCIAL STATEMENTS


01. Corporate Information
    Colombo Dockyard PLC, is a public limited company incorporated and domiciled in Sri
     Lanka and listed in Colombo Stock Exchange.
     Company Subsidiaries are :
      Dockyard General Engineering Services (Pvt) Ltd - Sri Lanka - 100%
      Dockyard Total Solutions (Pvt) Ltd - Sri Lanka - 100%
      Ceylon Shipping Agency (Pte) Ltd - Singapore - 51%
    The interim financial statements of the Group and the Company for the six months period
    ended 30 September 2026 were authorized for issue by the board of directors on October 16, 2026.


02. Basis Of Preparation
   The condensed consolidated interim financial statements have been prepared in accordance with
   Sri Lanka Accounting Standards - LKAS 34 Interim Financial reporting.


03. Borrowing Cost
    As per LKAS 23 (Borrowing cost ) interest costs amounting to Rs 54.82 Mn
  (2025/04/01 - 2025/09/30 Rs 180.6 Mn) which was related to shipbuilding projects (qualifying assets) were charged
   to the cost of sales.


04. Contingencies, capital and other commitments
   There has been no significant change in the nature of the contingencies and other commitments,
   which were disclosed in the annual Financial Statements for the fifteen months ended March 31, 2026.

05. Post balance sheet events
    There have been no events subsequent to the Balance Sheet date, which require disclosures in
     or adjustment to Financial Statements .

06. Comparative Information
   During the previous financial year, the Company changed its financial year end from 31 December to 31 March
   in order to align its reporting period with the new parent company Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders Limited.
   Accordingly, the current financial year commenced on 1 April 2026, and this financial information represents
  the six month period ended 30 September 2026. In accordance with the applicable financial reporting
  requirements for interim financial reporting, the comparative information presented for the statement of profit or loss,
  statement of changes in equity and statement of cash flows relates to the corresponding Six-month period
  from 1 April 2025 to 30 September 2025.
  Certain comparative figures have been re-classified to conform with the current year's presentation.

07. Cash and Cash Equivalents
  As at 30 September 2026, the Company’s cash and cash equivalents amounted to Rs. 17,003.09 Mn
  (Group: Rs. 17,274.91 Mn). This includes interest earning deposits of Rs. 15,457.47 Mn held under lien as collateral
  for bank guarantees and other credit facilities. The corresponding amounts held under lien were Rs. 2,763.70 Mn
  as at 30 September 2025 and Rs. 4,912.20 Mn as at 31 March 2026.

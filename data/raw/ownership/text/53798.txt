FINANCIAL REVIEW


Cable Solutions PLC delivered a strong second-quarter performance in FY2026/27, with
significant year-on-year growth in Group revenue and profitability. The results were supported by
higher sales and improved gross profit generation, despite increases in operating expenses.
Performance also strengthened compared with the first quarter of the financial year.

Group revenue for the quarter ended 30 September 2026 increased by 38% to LKR 1,205 million,
compared with LKR 873 million in Q2 FY2025/26. Revenue for the first six months reached LKR
2,193 million, an increase of 24% over the corresponding period of the previous year. Quarter-on-
quarter revenue increased by approximately 22%, indicating stronger business activity in Q2.

Quarterly gross profit rose by 40% to LKR 395 million from LKR 281 million. The gross profit
margin improved to 32.8%, compared with 32.2% in the corresponding quarter and 27.9% in Q1
FY2026/27. This recovery in margin reflects a more favourable relationship between sales and
cost of sales during the quarter.
Group operating profit (EBIT) increased by 54% to LKR 233 million, compared with LKR 152
million in Q2 FY2025/26. The improvement was achieved despite year-on-year increases of 29%
in administrative expenses and 34% in distribution expenses. Profit after tax increased by 62% to
LKR 199 million from LKR 123 million, lifting the quarterly net profit margin to 16.5% from
14.1%. For the six months ended 30 September 2026, Group profit after tax amounted to LKR 298
million, up 16% year-on-year.

USD Financial Performance

In the separately presented USD financial statements, Group revenue for Q2 FY2026/27 amounted
to USD 3.64 million, representing 25.8% growth from USD 2.89 million in the corresponding
quarter. Gross profit increased by 28.1% to USD 1.19 million, while operating profit grew by
40.5% to USD 705,267.

Group profit after tax in USD increased by 47.7% to USD 601,137, compared with USD 407,019
a year earlier. For the six-month period, USD revenue reached USD 6.65 million, an increase of
13.4%, while profit after tax amounted to USD 903,276, up 5.6%. The differences between LKR
and USD growth rates reflect the respective currency presentation bases; these USD amounts are
drawn from the separate USD financial results.

Outlook

The second-quarter improvement provides a positive foundation for the remainder of FY2026/27.
Management remains focused on sustaining revenue momentum, protecting gross profit margins,
strengthening operational efficiency and maintaining discipline over administrative, distribution
and finance costs. Continued attention to capacity utilization and cost management, including
within the India operation, will remain important to supporting sustainable Group profitability.

 1.1   Reporting entity – Domicile and legal form
       Cable Solutions PLC (“the Company”) is a limited liability company incorporated and domiciled in Sri
       Lanka. The registered office of the Company is located at Rammuthugala Estate, Rammuthugala,
       Kadawatha, Sri Lanka. The principal place of business is also at the same place

 1.2   Principal activities and nature of operations
       The Company is engaged in manufacturing and exporting of cables and related products. There were no
       significant changes in the nature of the principal activities of the Company during the financial year under
       review. The principal activity of the subsidiary is manufacturing cable assemblies in India.

 1.3   Parent company
       The Company’s immediate parent entity is ACL Cables PLC.

 02    Condensed Interim Financial Statements
       The financial statements for the period ended 30 September 2026, includes “the Company” referring to Cable
       Solutions PLC.

       The Company presents its financial statements primarily in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR). However, as the
       Company’s functional currency is the United States Dollar (USD), the Statement of Comprehensive Income
       and the Statement of Financial Position are also presented in USD for supplementary information.

 2.1   Approval of Financial Statements
       The condensed financial statements of the Company for the period ended 30 September 2026 were
       authorized for issue by the Board of Directors on 13 November 2026.

 2.2   Basis of Preparation
       The condensed interim financial statements of the Company have been prepared in accordance with Sri
       Lanka Accounting Standard - LKAS 34 Interim Financial Reporting . The condensed interim financial
       statements should be read in conjunction with the annual financial statements for the year ended 31 March
       2026.

 03    Accounting Policies
       The accounting policies adopted are consistent with those of the previous financial year.

 04    Contingencies, Capital and Other Commitments
       There were no significant contingencies, capital and other commitments as at the reporting date.

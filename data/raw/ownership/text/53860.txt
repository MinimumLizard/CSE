Interim Financial Statements for the Period Ended 30th September 2026

Analysis of Statement of Financial Position

Total Assets

Total Assets of the Group have decreased by Rs. 177Mn from Rs. 15,159Mn as at 31st March 2026 to Rs.
14,982Mn as at 30th September 2026. Total Non-Current Assets were Rs 8,444Mn and Total Current Assets
were Rs. 6,539Mn as at 30th September 2026.

Equity Attributable to Parent Company’s Shareholders
Equity attributable to Parent Company’s Shareholders has increased by Rs. 232Mn from Rs. 6,511Mn as at
31st March 2026 to Rs. 6,742Mn as at 30th September 2026.

Net Assets Per Share (NAPS)

The NAPS has increased to Rs. 18.00 as at 30th September 2026 compared with Rs. 17.38 per share recorded
as at 31st March 2026.

Analysis on Statement of Profit or Loss and Other Comprehensive Income

Revenue & Expenses
Group Revenue has decreased by 24% to Rs. 3,775Mn for the quarter ended 30th September 2026 when
compared with Rs. 4,974 Mn in the corresponding quarter of the previous year. The composition of the Group
Revenue for the period is 44% from export sales and 56% from local sales.

Total Group Admin & selling expenses have increased by 1% to Rs. 534Mn for the quarter ended 30th
September 2026 compared with Rs. 531Mn in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

Profit / (Loss) After Tax

The Group reported a profit after tax amounting to Rs. 65Mn for the quarter ended 30th September 2026
compared to a profit of Rs. 625Mn reported in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

Earnings / (Loss) Per Share
Loss per share was Rs 0.07 for the quarter ended 30th September 2026, compared with earnings per share of
Rs 3.34 in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

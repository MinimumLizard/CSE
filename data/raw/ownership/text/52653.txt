QUARTER REVIEW


Blue Diamonds Jewellery Worldwide PLC (BDJW) has released its unaudited financial results for the quarter
ended 30 June 2026, being the first quarter of the financial year 2026/2027.

During the quarter under review, the Company recorded revenue of approximately Rs. 10.53 million, compared
with Rs. 23.17 million recorded during the corresponding quarter of the previous year. This represents a decrease
of approximately 54.6%, reflecting the challenging operating environment and lower sales volumes during the
period.


Despite the decline in revenue, the Company recorded a profit before tax of approximately Rs. 6.90 million for the
quarter, compared with Rs. 6.95 million in the corresponding quarter of the previous year. The Company therefore
maintained a broadly stable profit before tax position despite the significant reduction in revenue.


The Company recorded a profit for the period of approximately Rs. 6.90 million for the quarter ended 30 June
2026, compared with approximately Rs. 6.95 million for the corresponding period of 2025.

As at 30 June 2026, the Group's total assets amounted to approximately Rs. 183.84 million, compared with Rs.
162.40 million as at 30 June 2025. Total equity increased to approximately Rs. 80.29 million, compared with Rs.
17.44 million in the corresponding period of the previous year. The increase in equity primarily reflects
movements in accumulated losses and reserves during the period.

Inventory stood at approximately Rs. 68.37 million as at 30 June 2026, compared with Rs. 58.64 million as at 30
June 2025. Interest-bearing loans and borrowings amounted to approximately Rs. 63.88 million, compared with
Rs. 50.30 million in the previous year.

During the first quarter, the Company also reversed an inventory provision of approximately Rs. 12.49 million,
based on the assessment of the recoverability and net realisable value of inventories.

The management continues to focus on improving sales volumes, strengthening gross margins, managing operating
costs and improving working capital efficiency. The Company will continue to explore opportunities in both
existing and potential international markets while maintaining prudent financial and operational controls.


The management remains committed to improving the Company's operational performance and creating
sustainable value for shareholders. The Company will continue to monitor market conditions closely and take
appropriate measures to strengthen its financial position and profitability during the remainder of the financial year.

7.   The Capital Work-in-Progress amounting to Rs. 1,173,147,595/- represents the expenditure incurred for
     the construction of two 5MW Ground-Mounted Solar PV Projects, Dolahena I & II, located in Matara. The
     projects are already connected to the National Grid, and upon completion of the Addendum to the
     Standardized Power Purchase Agreements (SPPAs), which is expected shortly, the balance under
     Capital Work-in-Progress will be transferred to the respective asset categories.

     Subsequent to the reporting date, an additional amount of Rs. 147,895,003/- has been capitalized under
     Capital Work-in-Progress. Furthermore, the Company has an estimated remaining capital expenditure
     commitment of approximately Rs. 445 million for the completion of the ongoing construction work related
     to these projects.


8.   Term Loan Facility – Dolahena I & II Ground-Mounted Solar PV Projects

     The Company entered into a term loan facility agreement with Commercial Bank of Ceylon PLC on 5th
     February 2025 for a total amount of Rs. 1,372,000,000/-, with a repayment period of 84 months, after a
     one-year grace period.




                                                    Page 6
       The interest rate applicable is as follows:

           11% per annum (fixed) for the first five years
           AWPLR + 0.5% per annum for the remaining three years

       During the reporting period, a sum of Rs. 708,872,196/- was disbursed under this facility to finance the
       construction of the Dolahena I & II Ground-Mounted Solar PV Projects. The loan is secured by:


           A floating primary mortgage of Rs. 472,000,000/- over the project land (immovable assets)
           A floating primary mortgage of Rs. 900,000,000/- over the project’s movable assets

       Subsequent to the reporting date, an additional Rs. 143,863,553/- was disbursed under the same facility.

       The first capital repayment, amounting to Rs. 16,334,000/-, is scheduled to commence on 10th July
       2026.




9.     Contingent Liability
       As at 30th September 2025, the Group has contingent liabilities arising from bank guarantees totaling
       Rs. 651,700,000/-, issued in favour of Sri Lanka Customs in relation to the Bonding Warehouse facility
       obtained for the importation of goods for the Dolahena I & II Ground-Mounted Solar PV Projects.


       Additionally, the company has provided two bank guarantees amounting to Rs. 50 million as proposal
       security for the purpose of the CEB tender.


10. Events After the Reporting Date

Review of the Financials

Teejay Group recorded a net profit of LKR 0.4 billion for the quarter ended 30th September 2026, marking a significant 168% increase compared with the corresponding period of the previous year. This strong
quarterly performance enabled the Group to substantially recover from the LKR 0.5 billion loss reported in Q1, narrowing the cumulative loss to a marginal LKR 0.1 billion at the end of the first half. This
improvement reflects the continued progress of the Group's restructuring and transformation initiatives, with a strategic focus on strengthening operational efficiency, enhancing productivity and optimizing the
overall cost base.
The Group delivered revenue of LKR 18.3 billion for the quarter ended 30th September 2026, recording an 18% year-on-year growth, while first-half revenue increased by 9% compared with the corresponding
period last year. This performance underscores the Group's ability to sustain business momentum and respond effectively to evolving customer expectations and market dynamics, despite persistent geopolitical
and economic uncertainties across key export markets. Marketing efforts remain centered on strengthening strategic customer relationships, capitalizing on emerging market opportunities, optimizing the
product mix and diversifying the customer and market portfolio to support sustainable long-term growth.

Gross profit for the quarter reached LKR 1.6 billion, representing a 30% year-on-year increase, supported by 18% revenue growth during the period. This improvement was driven by higher business volumes and
stronger revenue generation, further reinforced by the positive impact of ongoing restructuring and transformation initiatives. These initiatives continue to prioritize operational efficiency, productivity
enhancement, disciplined inventory management and cost optimization, contributing towards improved overall business performance.

The Group continued to demonstrate disciplined cost management, with distribution expenses declining by 9% year on year to LKR 0.13 billion, while administrative expenses decreased by 5% to LKR 0.84 billion
compared with the corresponding quarter of the previous financial year. These reductions reflect the continued benefits of the Group's cost optimization initiatives and its sustained focus on maintaining greater
cost discipline across operations.

Maintaining a strong financial position, the Group continued to demonstrate balance sheet stability and healthy liquidity, supported by a robust cash balance of LKR 11.08 billion. As of 30th September 2026, the
Group's net asset base remained strong at LKR 33.8 billion, equivalent to LKR 46.88 per share. This sustained financial strength and healthy liquidity position reinforce the Group's resilience and its capacity to
meet ongoing business requirements while supporting strategic growth initiatives.
Looking ahead, the Group remains focused on navigating an increasingly complex and evolving global environment, shaped by geopolitical tensions and the ongoing conflict in the Middle East, civil unrest and
political uncertainty across parts of Europe, as well as the evolving political and economic landscape in the United States following the mid-term elections. Against this backdrop, the Group continues to advance
its strategic priorities, with particular emphasis on market diversification, product mix optimization, operational efficiency and cost competitiveness. Underpinned by a strong financial position, a diversified
manufacturing footprint and ongoing transformation initiatives, Teejay Group remains committed to strengthening operational resilience, sustaining profitability and delivering sustainable long-term value to its
stakeholders.

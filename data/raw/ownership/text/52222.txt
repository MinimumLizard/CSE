Performance Review for the Three Months Ended 30th June 2026
Overview

 The Group recorded revenue of Rs. 7.8 Bn for the quarter ended 30 June 2026. Profit Before Tax (PBT)
and Profit After Tax (PAT) amounted to Rs. 234 Mn and Rs. 105 Mn, respectively.
The Group's profitability declined compared
to the corresponding period of the previous
year, reflecting the impact of local and global
economic headwinds. Geopolitical tensions
in the Middle East contributed to heightened
uncertainty, increased cost pressures, and
weaker consumer sentiment, which in turn
affected consumption patterns in Sri Lanka.
Despite these headwinds, the Group
remained focused on prudent cost
management, strengthening its brand
portfolio,     and     driving     operational
efficiencies to sustain long-term growth.
The Consumer segment continued to be the
Group’s largest revenue contributor,
representing 60% of total revenue for the
quarter ended 30 June 2026, followed by the Industrial segment at 20% and the Wellness & Nutrition
segment at 19%.


Operating Environment
During this period, the operating environment remained challenging amid rising inflationary pressures,
exchange rate volatility and tighter monetary conditions.
In response to increasing inflation concerns, the Central Bank of Sri Lanka raised the Overnight Policy
Rate by 100 basis points to 8.75% in May 2026 to support price stability, currency resilience and foreign
exchange reserve management. This resulted in higher market lending rates, increasing borrowing costs
for businesses.
Inflation accelerated during the quarter, driven by higher fuel and transport costs, rising food prices and
the impact of currency depreciation on imports. The Sri Lankan Rupee depreciated by approximately 8%
against the US Dollar rates in December 2025, contributing to increased costs of imported raw materials,
fuel and capital goods, particularly for import-dependent sectors.

Consumer Segment

The Consumer segment remained the Group's principal revenue contributor for the quarter ended 30 June
2026. During the period, the segment recorded a revenue of Rs. 4.7 Bn and a PBT of Rs. 65 Mn.
Home Care & Personal Care:

The Home Care & Personal Care sector accounted for 72% of the segment's total revenue, generating Rs.
3.3 Bn, a modest increase from Rs. 3.2 Bn in the previous year. This performance highlights the sector's
resilience and its continued position as the primary revenue contributor within the Consumer segment,
despite challenging market conditions.
Foods:
During the period, the Foods sector contributed 28% of the segment's total revenue, with revenue declining
by 24% to Rs. 1.3 Bn from Rs. 1.7 Bn in the previous year. This decline was primarily driven by supply
constraints, as Middle East-related shipping disruptions extended import lead times, resulting in stock
shortages during the period under review. The situation has since recovered, with improved product
availability following the implementation of proactive supply chain measures.
The Consumer segment's performance was impacted by the challenging macroeconomic environment,
which required higher working capital investments to ensure business continuity and maintain adequate
inventory levels. Profitability was further affected by increased operating costs, while inflationary
pressures and the rising cost of living continued to influence consumer spending patterns and purchasing
behavior.
In response to these challenges, the Company remains focused on enhancing operational efficiencies,
optimizing processes, and implementing strategic initiatives to sustain sales volumes and strengthen
business resilience.


Industrial Segment

During the period, the Industrial segment recorded revenue of Rs. 1.6 Bn and PBT of Rs. 112 Mn.
Energy & Lighting:
The Energy & Lighting sector offers a comprehensive range of energy and lighting products as well as
integrated solar and battery energy storage systems. The sector contributed 24% to the Industrial segment,
recording revenue of Rs. 380 Mn during the period. Compared to the Rs. 712 Mn revenue recorded in the
previous year, the sector experienced a 47% decline, primarily due to lower turnover from renewable
energy driven by grid connection limitations, which continued to impact market demand and project
execution.

Building Solutions:

The Building Solutions sector offers a comprehensive range of building and steel products, including steel
wire products, steel roofing, wall cladding, and structural building solutions. During the period, the sector
recorded revenue of Rs. 1.2 Bn, contributing 76% to the Industrial segment's total revenue. The sector
recorded a decline compared to the previous year’s revenue of Rs. 1.4 Bn, primarily due to lower GI wire
sales. Performance was impacted by softer demand, heightened competition following cost-driven price
revisions, and an adverse shift in the sales mix towards lower-revenue orders.
The Industrial segment's revenue and PBT declined during the period under review due to prevailing
market conditions; however, the sector maintained its margins through effective cost management, and
operational efficiencies, demonstrating resilience in a challenging business environment.


Wellness and Nutrition Segment
The Wellness and Nutrition segment recorded revenue of Rs. 1.5 Bn for the quarter ended 30 June 2026,
while PBT amounted to Rs. 53 Mn. The segment delivered revenue growth compared to Rs. 1.4 Bn
recorded in the previous year, driven by stronger demand for specialized nutrition and adult care products,
supported by favorable market conditions.

The segment benefited from the strategic initiatives implemented in previous period, including the
introduction of new product variants, portfolio expansion, and price optimization measures. These
initiatives, together with the segment’s continued focus on strengthening market presence and responding
to evolving healthcare needs, supported sustained growth and improved performance.


ESG initiatives

During the first quarter, the Group continued to advance its sustainability journey by progressing strategic
initiatives focused on enhancing operational excellence, resource productivity, and long-term business
resilience. The Group continued to identify and implement improvement opportunities that support
efficient resource utilization, and responsible value creation across its business operations.

These efforts reinforce the Group’s commitment to integrating sustainability considerations into strategic
and operational decision-making, enabling continuous improvement, cost optimization, and enhanced
preparedness to address evolving environmental and regulatory expectations.

Strategic Outlook

Continued global uncertainties are expected to pose challenges to the country’s economic outlook, with
Sri Lanka remaining exposed to external pressures, including inflationary trends, exchange rate volatility,
and elevated import and energy costs. These challenges will impact domestic consumption, business
confidence, and external sector stability, creating a challenging operating environment for businesses.

Against this backdrop, the Group continues to focus on adapting to evolving market trends, capitalizing
on emerging opportunities, and strengthening its operational capabilities. Ongoing initiatives to optimize
operating structures and accelerate digital transformation are expected to enhance organizational agility,
improve productivity, and create long-term value for stakeholders.

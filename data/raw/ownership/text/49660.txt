     The interim Financial Statements have been prepared in accordance with Sri Lanka Accounting Standard for Small and

     Financials Statements for the year ended 31st March 2025 and are in compliance with LKAS 34 – Interim Financial
     Reporting. The presentation and classification of comparative information have been amended where relevant. The
     interim financial statements are not audited.

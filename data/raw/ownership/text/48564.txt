The FMCG segment recorded healthy double-digit volume growth across key categories—value-added dairy, ice cream,
convenience foods, and beverages—driving topline growth of 17.3% during the quarter. The biscuit (confectionery) category
recorded modest volume growth, while culinary food volumes remained flat year-on-year. We remain committed to investing in
quality, innovation, and sourcing, while expanding our distribution capabilities and strengthening our brand portfolio to sustain
this growth trajectory. Sales growth was supported across all major channels, including general trade, modern trade
(supermarkets), and food service.
From April 2025, the Government’s removal of VAT on selected dairy products—specifically liquid milk and yoghurt—further
supported volume growth in these categories. The Company passed on the full benefit of this tax reduction to consumers
through price adjustments. However, sales in the dairy category were sluggish in early April ahead of the National New Year,
due to market uncertainty regarding the VAT removal, which was formally introduced only on April 11. This caused a temporary
holdback in orders by retailers, but we successfully recovered the lost volumes later in the quarter.
In the beverages category, production-related challenges affected supply during the month of May, particularly within the
energy drink portfolio. Despite this, the segment rebounded strongly in June, driven by the strong performance of RIDE energy
drinks, KIST fruit nectars, and the KIST pure fruit juice range. We are also pleased with the positive consumer response to our
newly launched carbonated fruit beverage line. During the quarter, we introduced the new halaal-certified range of
convenience food products and unveiled refreshed packaging for the RIDE energy drink range, both of which have been well
received in the market.

3. Contingent liabilities

   The income tax concession claimed under the Inland Revenue Act, No. 10 of 2006 and Act, No. 24 of 2017, as well as the Value Added Tax
   concession/exemption claimed under the Value Added Tax Act, No 14 of 2002 have been contested by the Department of Inland Revenue.
   The contingent liabilities for the Group in respect of these amount to Rs 690 Mn and Rs 1,471 Mn respectively.
   Having sought professional advice, the Management is confident that the tax concessions are applicable and as such no liabilities would
   arise. Accordingly, no provision has been made in the financial statements.

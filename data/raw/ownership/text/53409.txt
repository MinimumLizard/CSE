    Right Issue of Ordinary Voting Shares.

    Subsequent to the reporting date, the Board of Directors approved a proposal to raise capital by way of a rights issue, subject to
    obtaining the necessary shareholder and regulatory approvals. The proposed issue comprises eight (8) new ordinary voting shares
    for every seventeen (17) existing ordinary voting shares at an issue price of Rs. 39/- per share, with the objective of raising
    approximately Rs. 5 billion. The proceeds will be used to partially settle the Company's existing bank borrowings and overdue inter-
    company trade and other payables (all being interest bearing dues), thereby substantially strengthening the Company's overall

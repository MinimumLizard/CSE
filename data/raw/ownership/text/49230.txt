Chairman & Chief Executive Officer’s Review

Dear Valued Shareholders,

It is with great pride and confidence that I present the consolidated financial performance of Kapruka
Holdings PLC for the quarter ended 30th September 2025—a clear reflection of our resilience, strategic focus,
and commitment to sustainable growth.

Amidst a challenging operating environment, the Group delivered a robust top-line performance, recording
revenue of LKR 416.09 million, a commendable 12% year-on-year growth. Gross profit increased by
19% to LKR 154.52 million, driven by strengthened operational efficiencies, disciplined cost management,
and a more favourable sales mix. These triumphs have catalysed a remarkable 77% enhancement in
operating profits, shrinking our operating loss to a mere LKR 5.76 million from LKR 25.43 million in the
previous year. Furthermore, the Group achieved an impressive 84% in Earnings Before Taxation, reducing
the loss to LKR 3.05 million from LKR 18.55 million in the corresponding period of the prior year. This
significant turnaround underscores the effectiveness of our strategic initiatives and the enduring strength of
our business model.

A key pillar of our strategy is Kapruka Partner Central, our seller platform. Partner Central has enabled us
to onboard a rapidly growing network of third-party sellers and brands, allowing Kapruka to introduce
thousands of new, niche product categories without incurring stock-holding costs. This asset-light model
significantly enhances our scalability and capital efficiency while staying true to one of our primary
objectives: offering a true “world of choices” to our online shoppers.

Through Partner Central, we are deepening our integration with leading brands in Sri Lanka, providing them
with a powerful digital storefront and fulfilment infrastructure, while Kapruka benefits from diversified,
higher-margin, commission-based revenue streams. Over time, this platform-driven model is expected to
reduce earnings volatility, strengthen supplier partnerships, and reinforce Kapruka’s position as the country’s
leading digital commerce ecosystem.

Building on this platform strategy, we are also in the process of launching a “Services Platform” under
Kapruka Partner Central. This will enable customers to book everyday services online – for example, simple
jobs such as home visits to resolve IT issues – with verified service providers. The concept is similar to
platforms such as TaskRabbit in developed markets, adapted to local needs and trust standards. This
initiative will expand Kapruka beyond products into services, unlocking a new addressable market while
leveraging our brand, technology, and customer base.

Another important growth engine is our Kapruka Cross Border initiative, which is delivering very
encouraging results. Under this model, Kapruka acts as an e-distributor for Sri Lankan brands on leading
global marketplaces such as Amazon in the US, Canada, and the UK. We manage the end-to-end presence of
these brands – including listings, compliance, logistics, and customer service – enabling them to access
international demand without having to invest in their own overseas infrastructure. This has evolved into a
strong USD-generating business vertical and has been a meaningful contributor to the financial performance
reported for the quarter.

Looking ahead, we remain firmly committed to innovation, disciplined execution, and sustainable value
creation. With our strengthened brand, the rapid progress of Kapruka Partner Central, the upcoming Services
Platform, the strong traction of Kapruka Cross Border, and growing USD revenues, we believe Kapruka is well-
positioned to enhance shareholder value while scaling our market leadership and expanding our global reach.

Thank you for your continued confidence and support.


Dulith Herath
Chairman & CEO

7.1 Rights issue announcement - Softlogic Finance PLC The Board of Directors of Softlogic Finance PLC, a subsidiary of Softlogic Holdings
PLC, resolved on 18 September 2026 to recommend a Rights Issue of 401,072,162 new Ordinary Voting Shares at a price of Rs. 2.50 per
share, amounting to Rs. 1,002,680,405, on the basis of five (5) new Ordinary Voting Shares for every twelve (12) existing Ordinary Voting
Shares held. The proposed Rights Issue is subject to approval by the shareholders of the Company at an Extraordinary General Meeting
scheduled to be held on 13 November 2026. Upon full subscription, the stated capital of the Company will increase from Rs.
2,326,718,963.64 to Rs. 3,329,399,368.64.

The proceeds from the proposed Rights Issue will be utilized to strengthen the Core Capital (Tier 1) of Softlogic Finance PLC and expand its
lending portfolio, particularly in the vehicle financing segment.

risk-weighted assets. As at 30 September 2026, the Company’s Tier 1 Capital and Total Capital stood at Rs. 1,951 million, against total risk-
weighted assets of Rs. 4,223 million. Accordingly, both the Tier 1 Capital Ratio and Total Capital Ratio stood at 46.20%, remaining well above
the regulatory minimum requirements of 8.5% and 12.5%, respectively.

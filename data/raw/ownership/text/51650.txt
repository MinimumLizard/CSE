11     DISCLOSURE ON IMPLEMENTATION OF NEW LAWS IN INDONESIA FOR PALM OIL PLANTATIONS
       As disclosed in the previous Interim Accounts, Government of Indonesia introduced new regulations, establishing a
       Presidential Task Force for the repossession of lands categorized as forest status and a methodology for calculation
       of administrative fines. Goodhope Asia Holdings Ltd (a subsidiary of the group) is in discussion with the Government
       in finalizing the administrative fine, and has already paid a substantial amount. The Group has also provided for the
       administrative fine in the current quarter as reflected in the other operating expenses of the accounts.

       Further, the Group’s Palm Oil Plantation Segment has fully written-off affected land areas from the non-current assets in
       the 3rd quarter of the financial year.

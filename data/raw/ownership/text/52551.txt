1. The Basis of Preparation
The Interim Financial Statements have been prepared in compliance with Sri Lanka Accounting Standard LKAS 34 – Interim Financial
Reporting. These Interim Financial Statements should be read in conjunction with the Annual Financial Statements for the year ended
31 December 2025. Further, Provisions of the Companies Act No.7 of 2007 have been considered in preparing the Interim Financial
Statements of the Group.

Application of Statement of Alternative Treatment (SoAT)

SLFRS 17 Insurance Contracts become applicable for annual reporting periods commencing on or after 1 January 2026. Due to
practical implementation challenges faced by the insurance industry, CA Sri Lanka has granted permission to insurers to continue
adopting SLFRS 4 Insurance Contracts for the first two interim reporting periods of the initial year of transition, provided that enhanced
disclosures are made regarding the anticipated effects of SLFRS 17 and SLFRS 9. In line with this concession, the Company has
elected to apply SLFRS 4 for the quarter ended 30th June 2026.

In accordance with the Statement of Alternative Treatment (SoAT), the group intends to comply with the following:

•   Quantitative disclosures have not been presented for the current period, as the draft figures prepared under SLFRS 17 and SLFRS
    9 are subject to further review.

•   Comprehensive quantitative disclosures relating to all financial statement line items affected by the adoption of SLFRS 17 and
    SLFRS 9 will be provided in year ended 31 December 2026.

•   Provisional financial information for the third interim reporting period, prepared in accordance with SLFRS 17 and SLFRS 9, will be
    included in the interim financial statements for the quarter ended 30 September 2026.

Impact of SLFRS 17 – Life and General Insurance

With respect to the General Insurance segment, the Group expects to adopt SLFRS 17 which primarily impact on the measurement
basis and presentation of insurance contract liabilities, the recognition and presentation of insurance revenue and insurance service
expenses, together with related disclosures. The Premium Allocation Approach (PAA) is expected to be applied to the majority of
General Insurance contracts that meet the eligibility criteria under SLFRS 17.

For the Life Insurance segment, the adoption of SLFRS 17 is expected to result in changes to the measurement of long‑term insurance
contract liabilities, the introduction of the Contractual Service Margin (CSM) representing unearned profit, and a revised pattern of
profit recognition over the coverage period, together with enhanced disclosure requirements.

The quantitative impact of these changes will be recognized and presented upon the Group’s full adoption of SLFRS 17 in future
reporting periods.

Impact of SLFRS 9 Financial Instruments

SLFRS 9 Financial Instruments replaces LKAS 39. However, in accordance with the amendments to SLFRS 4, the Group has elected
to apply the temporary exemption from SLFRS 9 and has deferred its adoption to align with the initial application of SLFRS 17. SLFRS
9 introduces a revised classification and measurement framework for financial assets based on the business model and contractual
cash flow characteristics, with assets measured at Amortised Cost, Fair Value through Other Comprehensive Income (FVOCI), or Fair
Value through Profit or Loss (FVPL). In addition, SLFRS 9 replaces the incurred loss model with a forward‑looking Expected Credit
Loss (ECL) approach for impairment, requiring the recognition of credit losses based on changes in credit risk over the life of financial
assets.

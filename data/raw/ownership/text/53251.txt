SUMMARY OF KEY OPERATIONAL AND FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS FOR
THE SECOND QUARTER OF THE FINANCIAL YEAR 2026/27 (FY26/27)


Company reported revenue of Rs. 5.4 Bn for the first half of FY26/27, marking a 38% year-on-year
increase from Rs. 3.9 Bn in the corresponding period of the previous financial year. Growth was driven
by strong underlying fundamentals, with average monthly unique driver earners and average monthly
unique consumers reaching all-time highs, despite fuel supply constraints affecting the first quarter.


Activity levels expanded across all product verticals, with total platform movements increasing by 39%
year-on-year in the first half of FY26/27. Gross Transaction Value (GTV), representing the total value of
all facilitated trips and deliveries, mirrored this growth by rising 43% to Rs. 53.8 Bn from Rs. 37.6 Bn in
the corresponding period of the prior year. This momentum translated to total earnings of Rs. 46.7 Bn
generated for independent drivers and merchants on the platform during the period.


Supported by resilient top-line growth, operating leverage and continued cost discipline, the Company
recorded a 44% increase in Operating Profit to Rs. 1.9 Bn for the first half of FY26/27, up from Rs. 1.3
Bn in the corresponding period of the prior year. Net Profit increased 49% year-on-year to Rs. 1.4 Bn,
compared to Rs. 950 Mn in the prior year period, with Earnings Per Share (EPS) rising to Rs. 4.24 from
Rs. 2.85.


Revenue for the second quarter of FY26/27 reached Rs. 2.9 Bn, reflecting 37% year-on-year growth
compared to Rs. 2.1 Bn recorded in the second quarter of the previous financial year, and a sequential
14% quarter-on-quarter expansion over the first quarter, which was partially impacted by the fuel
supply constraints. Demand was underpinned by average monthly unique consumers reaching 2.1 Mn
in the second quarter of FY26/27, representing an increase of 38% year-on-year and 10% quarter-on-
quarter. Performance was further supported by stronger supply-side dynamics, with average monthly
unique driver earners expanding 58% year-on-year.


During the second quarter of FY26/27, the platform facilitated a GTV of Rs. 28.5 Bn, with 87%
representing direct earnings passed through to independent drivers and merchants, underscoring the
platform’s capacity to create sustained income opportunities for independent earners.

Notes To The Financial Statements

1. These Interim Financial Statements have been prepared in accordance with the requirements of Sri Lanka Accounting Standard -LKAS 34 on
'Interim Financial Reporting'.

2. The accounting policies adopted in the preparation of these Interim Condensed Financial Statements are consistent with those followed in the
preparation of the Company’s Annual Financial Statements for the year ended 31 Dec 2025.

3. Stated Capital represents 202,792,332 number of issued & fully paid Ordinary Shares including one Golden Share held by the Treasury which
has special rights and 20,250,660 number of Non voting shares.

4. The above figures are provisional and subject to audit.

5. No circumstances have arisen since the Balance Sheet date, which would require adjustments or disclosures in the Financial statements.


6. The Company has been adopting Sri Lanka Accounting Standards (SLRFS/LKAS) with effect from 1st January 2012 .

7. Finance Cost incorporates a total of Rs. 154,120,508 attributable to FOREX loss, while Finance Income encompasses a total of Rs. 196,389,228
from FOREX gain for the nine months ending in 2026.

8. Starting from the quarter ended 30 September 2025, the Company reviewed its internal reporting structure in line with the requirements of
SLFRS 8 – Operating Segments. As a result, certain previously reported minor segments have been aggregated and presented under “Others,” since
they are no longer considered material for separate disclosure. This change reflects management’s revised assessment of reportable segments and has
been made in accordance with paragraphs 12–19 of SLFRS 8 and LKAS 34 – Interim Financial Reporting. Comparative information has been
reclassified to conform to the current period presentation to ensure comparability. The change in segment presentation has no impact on the total
revenue, profit before tax, or net assets previously reported for any period.

9. No Provision was made for Deferred Tax.

10. As disclosed in the Annual Financial Statements for the year ended 31 December 2025, the comparative figures have been restated to recognise
the deferred tax impact of Rs. 278,759,863 arising from the revaluation surplus on property, plant and equipment, which had not been recognised as
at 31 December 2022 and 2023. Accordingly, the comparative information presented in these interim financial statements has been restated. This
restatement was not disclosed in the Interim Financial Statements for the three-month period ended 31 March 2026, as it was identified and
reflected subsequently in the Annual Financial Statements.

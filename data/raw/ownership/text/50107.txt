Dear Shareholders,
For the Nine Months and Third Quarter Ended 31 December 2025 (Unaudited)

I am pleased to report a significant improvement in the Company’s financial performance for the
nine months ended 31 December 2025, with the Company recording a profit before tax of LKR
7.6 Mn, compared to a loss of LKR 118.6 Mn in the corresponding period of the previous year,
representing a year-on-year improvement of LKR 126.2 Mn.
Cumulative revenue for the nine-month period increased by 18% year-on-year to LKR 1,756.1
Mn. Gross profit more than doubled, increasing by 107% to LKR 246.4 Mn, driven by improved
margins and effective cost of sales management. As a result, the Company reported a positive
operating profit of LKR 80.2 Mn, compared to an operating loss in the prior year. Net finance
expenses declined by 24%, further supporting the return to profitability.

For the third quarter ended 31 December 2025, revenue declined by 19% to LKR 604.2 Mn,
compared to LKR 746.6 Mn in the corresponding quarter of the previous year. Despite the lower
revenue, gross profit increased by 32% to LKR 95.2 Mn, reflecting sustained margin
improvements.

Operating profit for the quarter increased by 33% year-on-year to LKR 32.9 Mn, notwithstanding
higher distribution costs and a 4% increase in administrative expenses. Net finance expenses
increased marginally by 4%. Consequently, the Company recorded a profit before tax of LKR 3.9
Mn for the quarter, compared to a loss of LKR 3.2 Mn in the same quarter of the prior year.

The Board is encouraged by the Company’s ability to deliver improved profitability both on a
cumulative and quarterly basis. Going forward, continued focus will be placed on strengthening
revenue performance, sustaining margin improvements, and maintaining disciplined cost
management to support long-term value creation.

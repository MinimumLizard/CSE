13    Financial Performance
     The Palms, Beruwala,(Beruwala Resorts PLC) began an extensive refurbishment and upgrade programme in May 2026 to elevate the property from a 3-
     star to a 5-star hotel. The programme required the hotel to close completely for more than four months, until August 2026.

     the hotel retained its workforce throughout the closure, with employees undergoing comprehensive training and development to align service delivery
     and operational standards with the requirements of a 5-star establishment.

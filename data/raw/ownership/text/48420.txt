      the Companies Act, No 07 of 2007 on 13 December 2007 under the new Number PQ 107. The Registered office of the company is situated at 4th Floor,
      Unity Plaza Building, Colombo 04.

(2)   Basis of Preperation and Accounting Policies

       The interim financial statements of the Company have been prepared in accordance with Sri Lanka Accounting Standard - LKAS 34 – Interim Financial
      Reporting. This Interim Financial Statement should be read in conjunction with the Annual Financial Statements for the year ended 31 March 2024
      including the changes to accounting policies as a result of adapting Sri Lanka Financial Reporting Standards, (SLFRS/LKAS).

      The Company has followed the accounting policies and methods of computation as disclosed in the Annual Financial Statements for the year ended 31
      March 2024 in the preparation of the interim accounts for the quarter.

(3)   Disclosures

(3.1) The figures are provisional and subject to audit.

(3.2) There have been no material events since the Balance Sheet date which require separate disclosure in the provisional financial statements.

(3.3) Provisions have been made for all possible expenses as at 30 June 2025.

(3.4) Renting of investment property is considered as one operating segment and accordingly this Interim Financial report do not provide information by
      segment.

(3.5) There are no significant changes in the nature of the contingent liabilities disclosed in the Audited Financial Statements for the year ended 31st March
      2024.

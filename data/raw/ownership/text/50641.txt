QUARTER REVIEW

Blue Diamonds Jewellery Worldwide PLC has released its unaudited Interim Financial Statements for the
quarter ended 31 December 2025.

The Company recorded revenue of Rs. 4.9 million for the quarter under review, compared to Rs. 8.4
million in the corresponding quarter of the previous financial year. The decrease in revenue was primarily
due to lower sales volumes during the period.


Gross profit for the quarter amounted to Rs. 0.3 million, compared to Rs. 4.0 million reported in the
corresponding period of the previous year. The reduction was mainly attributable to the decline in
revenue and margin compression.


Administrative expenses decreased to Rs. 5.8 million from Rs. 9.4 million recorded in the corresponding
quarter of the previous year, reflecting continued cost rationalization measures undertaken by the
Company.


The loss before tax for the quarter was Rs. 6.3 million compared to Rs. 6.5 million in the corresponding
quarter of the previous year.

For the nine months ended 31 December 2025, the Company recorded revenue of Rs. 29.7 million
compared to Rs. 23.7 million in the previous financial year. Gross profit improved to Rs. 16.1 million
from Rs. 6.5 million in the corresponding period of the previous year. The loss before tax for the nine-
month period reduced to Rs. 12.3 million compared to Rs. 24.7 million in the corresponding period of the
previous year.

The Company continues to focus on prudent cost management and operational efficiency while
navigating prevailing economic conditions.

 5   TRANSITION TO CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS


     Blue Diamonds Jewellery Worldwide PLC, incorporated in 1991, had historically prepared its financial statements on a standalone basis. On December 6, 2023, the Company
     incorporated a subsidiary, NGTC Gem & Jewellery (Pvt) Ltd. Commencing from the financial year 2024, Blue Diamonds Jewellery Worldwide PLC transitioned to preparing
     consolidated financial statements, incorporating the financial results of its subsidiary in accordance with applicable financial reporting standards.
     During the course of the statutory audit for the financial year 2023/24, the Company’s external auditors observed that NGTC Gem & Jewellery (Pvt) Ltd qualified as a subsidiary
     and, therefore, should have been consolidated. However, due to an oversight, the Company did not publish consolidated interim financial statements for the 31st December
     2023,31st March 2024 and 30th June 2024.

     From the second quarter (30th September 2024) of the financial year 2024/25 onwards, the Company has commenced the publication of consolidated interim financial statements,
     in compliance with [Full SLFRS and International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS)] the applicable financial reporting requirements and best practices.

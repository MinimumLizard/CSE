10. CERTIFIED EMISSION REDUCTION (CER) UNITS
   As at 31st March 2026, the Group holds 60,527 units of Certified Emission Reductions (CERs), previously recognised at a net
   realisable value of USD 1.00 per unit. These units were generated from the Group’s renewable energy projects, and validation
   and verification activities have progressed during the year.

   An impairment assessment was performed as at the reporting date. In the absence of an active market for this vintage of CERs
   over the past five years, and due to uncertainty in realisability, Management has applied judgement in assessing the recoverable
   amount.

11. IMPACT OF DITWA CYCLONE ON LOTUS HYDRO POWER PLC OPERATIONS
 Lotus Hydro Power PLC Group was heavily affected by the “Ditwa” Cyclone in November 2025. Three power plants—Delta,
 Sanquhar, and Stellenberg—sustained significant damage due to the cyclone.

 The Stellenberg plant resumed operations on 12th December 2025 after completing the necessary repair works. However,
 the damage to the Delta and Sanquhar plants was substantial. Repair works at the Sanquhar plant were carried out, and the
 company recommenced its commercial operations on 01st February 2026.

 Repair works at the Delta plant are currently ongoing, and the company expects to complete the repairs by June 2026. Following
 the completion of the repair works, the company anticipates recommencing operations at the Delta plant.

 The company has formally informed the relevant authorities of the damages sustained by the affected power plants as a result
 of the Ditwa Cyclone and is currently following up on any relief or assistance that may be available.

Financial Review


The first quarter of 2026 marked a decisive step forward in the
Company’s growth journey. Profit after tax more than doubled to
Rs. 59.2 million, reflecting the strength of the core business
model and improved operating momentum. This enhanced
profitability was underpinned by steady balance sheet
expansion, with total assets rising by 23% to Rs. 15.1 billion.
Lending activity remained robust throughout the quarter, as
loans and advances grew by 24% to Rs. 13.8 billion,
demonstrating sustained demand and disciplined portfolio
growth.


During the three months ended 31 March 2026, the Company delivered a markedly improved financial performance
compared to the corresponding period in 2025, reflecting strengthened income generation and enhanced operating leverage.
Gross income increased by 69% to Rs. 601.8 million, driven primarily by a substantial increase in interest income, which
grew by 68% to Rs. 510.4 million. This growth was underpinned by expansion in the lending portfolio.

While interest expenses increased by 126%, reflecting higher funding volumes compared to the 1st quarter 2025, the
Company sustained a healthy margin outcome, resulting in net interest income increasing by 48% to Rs. 332.0 million. The
ability to maintain strong net interest performance underscores disciplined pricing strategies and effective balance sheet
management.

Other operating income recorded a robust increase of 76% to Rs. 91.5 million, contributing to a 53% growth in total operating
income, which reached Rs. 423.5 million for the quarter. Credit risk performance remained stable, with credit loss reversals
increasing marginally by 13%, reflecting prudent portfolio management and stable asset quality.

Operating expenses increased in line with business growth, particularly personnel costs, which rose by 57% to support
expanded operational capacity and strategic growth initiatives. Administrative expenses increased modestly by 19%,
remaining well contained relative to income growth. Distribution costs increased from Rs. 8 Mn in Q1 2025 to Rs. 15 Mn in
Q1 2026, primarily due to expanded revenue generating channels. As a result, profit before taxation increased by 108% to
Rs. 83.6 million, while profit for the period rose by 120% to Rs. 59.2 million. This strong bottom-line growth highlights the
successful translation of revenue expansion into sustainable profitability.

Overall, the performance for the quarter reflects a well-balanced growth trajectory, characterized by expanding income
streams, controlled cost dynamics, stable credit outcomes, and significantly enhanced shareholder returns. These results
reinforce the Company’s strategic focus on sustainable growth, disciplined risk management, and long-term value creation.

The Company’s capital adequacy position remained strong and resilient as at 31 March 2026, reflecting prudent capital management amid

total capital base strengthened to Rs. 3.85 Bn. During the year, the expansion of the financing portfolio led to a corresponding increase in
risk-weighted assets, absorbing capital into productive use and supporting business growth. Consequently, the Tier 1 Capital Adequacy Ratio
and the Total Capital Adequacy Ratio stood at 20.19% and 20.31% respectively, comfortably exceeding the regulatory minimum requirements
of 12.5%. This demonstrates that the preserving balance sheet soundness while enabling expansion. Furthermore, the Capital Funds to
Deposit Liabilities Ratio remained exceptionally high at 136%, underscoring a strong capital buffer and the Company’s capacity to meet
obligations while sustaining future growth within a sound regulatory framework.

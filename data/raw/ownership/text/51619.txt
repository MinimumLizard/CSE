Interim Financial Statements for the Period Ended 31st March 2026

Analysis of Statement of Financial Position

Total Assets

Total Assets of the Group has increased by Rs. 1,780Mn from Rs. 12,476 Mn as at 31st March 2025 to Rs.
14,256 Mn as at 31st March 2026. Total Non-Current Assets were Rs 6,835Mn and Total Current Assets were
Rs. 7,422Mn as at 31st March 2026.

Equity Attributable to Parent Company’s Shareholders
Equity attributable to Parent Company’s Shareholders has increased by Rs. 1,042Mn from Rs. 5,497 Mn as at
31st March 2025 to Rs. 6,539 Mn as at 31st March 2026.

Net Assets Per Share (NAPS)

The NAPS has decreased to Rs. 17.45 as at 31st March 2026 compared with Rs. 29.35 per share recorded as at
31st March 2025. During the quarter a share split was carried out at the rate of two shares for every one share
held.

Analysis on Statement of Profit or Loss and Other Comprehensive Income

Revenue & Expenses
Group Revenue has decreased by 4 % to Rs. 3,785Mn for the quarter ended 31st March 2026 when compared
with Rs. 3,960 Mn in the corresponding quarter of the previous year. The composition of the Group Revenue
for the quarter is 43 % from exports sales and 57% from local sales.

Total group admin & selling expenses has increased by 7% to Rs. 589 Mn for the quarter ended 31st March
2026 compared with Rs. 549 Mn in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

Profit / (Loss) After Tax

The Group reported a loss amounting to Rs. 73Mn for the quarter ended 31st March 2026 compared to a loss
of Rs 62 Mn reported in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

Earnings / (Loss) Per Share
Earnings loss per Share was Rs 0.66 for the quarter ended 31st March 2026, compared to Loss per Share of
Rs 0.33 in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

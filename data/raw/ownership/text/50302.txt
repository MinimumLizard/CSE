6.   Financial Impact of Commissioning of Ground-Mounted Solar PV Project:

        During the period, two 5 MW ground-mounted Solar PV projects, namely Dolahena I and Dolahena
         II located in Matara, were connected to the National Grid on 10 September 2025. Accordingly, the
         Capital Work-in-Progress amounting to Rs. 1,747,596,108/- was capitalised under Property, Plant
         and Equipment with effect from the date the assets were available for their intended use.


        To finance the above investment, the Company obtained a term loan facility of Rs. 1,372,000,000/-
         repayable over a period of 84 months following a one-year grace period. The facility is secured by
         a floating primary mortgage of Rs. 472,000,000/- over the project land and Rs. 900,000,000/- over
         the project’s movable assets.


        Following the commencement of commercial operations, the solar projects contributed revenue of
         Rs. 138.9 million to the Company during the period.


        In addition, a deferred tax reversal gain of Rs. 441.6 million, arising from enhanced capital allowance
         benefits recognised upon capitalisation of the Solar PV project as an expansion under the
         Company’s BOI Agreement, has been recognised in the Statement of Profit or Loss. Accordingly, a
         deferred tax asset of Rs. 272.4 million has been recognised in the Statement of Financial Position.


7.   Contingent Liability
     As at 31st December 2025, the Group has contingent liabilities arising from bank guarantees totaling Rs.
     651,700,000/-, issued in favour of Sri Lanka Customs in relation to the Bonding Warehouse facility
     obtained for the importation of goods for the Dolahena I & II Ground-Mounted Solar PV Projects.


     Additionally, the company has provided two bank guarantees amounting to Rs. 50 million as proposal
     security for the purpose of the CEB tender.

2.1 Interim Reporting Relief – SLFRS 17 and SLFRS 9
SLFRS 17 – Insurance Contracts is effective for annual reporting periods beginning on or after 1 January 2026. Because of the
industry's implementation challenges, CA Sri Lanka allowed insurers to continue applying SLFRS 4 for the first two interim
quarters of 2026. It later extended this relief to the third quarter, provided the expected impact of SLFRS 17 and SLFRS 9 is
disclosed.
The Group and the Company have applied this relief for the quarter ended 30 September 2026. Accordingly:

•    insurance contracts continue to be recognised, measured and presented under SLFRS 4; and

•    financial instruments continue to be accounted for under LKAS 39 – Financial Instruments: Recognition and
     Measurement.

CA Sri Lanka has not yet decided the reporting basis for the fourth quarter or for the annual financial statements for the year
ending 31st December 2026.

4 THE ACCOUNTING POLICY FOR CONSOLIDATION OF SUBSIDIARY
Subsidiaries are all entities (including structured entities) over which the group has control. The group controls an entity when the
group is exposed to, or has rights to, variable returns from its involvement with the entity and has the ability to affect those returns
through its power over the entity. Subsidiaries are fully consolidated from the date on which control is transferred to the group.
They are deconsolidated from the date that control ceases.

Inter-company transactions balances and unrealised gains /losses on transactions between group companies are eliminated.
Amounts reported by subsidiary companies are adjusted to conform with the Group’s accounting policies where necessary.

13 BASIC AND DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE (EPS)
Basic Earnings Per Share is calculated by dividing the profit after tax for the period attributable to ordinary shareholders by the
weighted average number of ordinary shares outstanding during the period. Diluted EPS is determined by adjusting the profit
or loss attributable to ordinary shareholders and the weighted average number of ordinary shares outstanding for the effects of
all dilutive potential ordinary shares. There were no potential dilutive ordinary shares outstanding at any time during the period.
Therefore, Diluted Earnings Per Share is same as Basic Earnings Per Share.

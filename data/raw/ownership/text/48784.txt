Chairman’s Review
For the First Quarter Ended 30.06.2025

Dear Shareholders,

I am pleased to present the financial performance of the Group for the first quarter of the financial
year.

The first quarter results demonstrate the positive impact of strategic measures implemented to
strengthen our core business operations. This has resulted in a revenue of Rs. 590.1 million for Q1,
reflecting a 14% growth compared to Rs. 517.3 million in the corresponding period of last year.
This growth was primarily driven by increased sales volumes and favorable market conditions in
key export markets.

The Gross profit increased from Rs.29mn to Rs.72mn (growth of 144%) supported by both revenue
growth and GP margin improvement. As a result of reduced direct costs, GP margin grew from 6%
to 12%.

The Company reported a Net Profit After Tax of Rs. 2.3 million for the quarter, compared to a
loss of Rs. 52.7 million in Q1 last year supported by improved operational profit and finance cost
reduction (51%).

The Company will make every effort to sustain its growth through operational excellence, prudent
financial management, market diversification and increased sales.

On behalf of the Board, I extend my appreciation to our management team and employees for their
dedication, to our shareholders for their continued trust, and to our customers and partners for their
ongoing support.

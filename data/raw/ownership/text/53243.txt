                                                                   EXPLANATORY NOTES


01. Basis of Preparation
The Interim Financial Statements have been prepared and presented in accordance with Sri Lanka Accounting Standards (SLFRSs and LKASs) laid down by the
Institute of Chartered Accountants of Sri Lanka (CA Sri Lanka) and are in compliance with the requirements of the Companies Act No. 07 of 2007. In addition,
these Interim Financial Statements comply with the requirements of the Finance Business Act No. 42 of 2011, the Listing Rules of the Colombo Stock Exchange
and the guidelines issued by the Central Bank of Sri Lanka (CBSL).

02. Interim Financial Reporting
These Interim Financial Statements have been prepared in compliance with the requirements of Sri Lanka Accounting Standard – LKAS 34 Interim Financial
Reporting.

03. Change in Accounting Policy
Except for the change described below, the accounting policies adopted in preparing these Interim Financial Statements are consistent with those followed in the
Annual Audited Financial Statements for the year ended 31 March 2026.

During the current financial period, the Group and the Company changed the accounting policy for the subsequent measurement of freehold land and buildings
from the cost model to the revaluation model under Sri Lanka Accounting Standard – LKAS 16 Property, Plant and Equipment as Management considers that the
revaluation model provides reliable and more relevant information on their current values.

Accordingly, freehold land and buildings were revalued based on valuations resulting in an increase in Property, Plant and Equipment of Rs. 5,808 Mn and after
recognising the related deferred tax liability of Rs. 1,742 Mn, a net increase in the revaluation reserve of Rs. 4,066 Mn.

In accordance with Sri Lanka Accounting Standard – LKAS 8 Accounting Policies, Changes in Accounting Estimates and Errors, the initial application of the
revaluation model has been accounted for as a revaluation under LKAS 16 in the current period rather than as a retrospective change in accounting policy.
Accordingly, comparative information has not been restated.

04. Group Composition
The Consolidated Financial Statements comprise LB Finance PLC (Parent Company), LB Microfinance Myanmar Company Limited (Subsidiary) and
LB Finance Philippines Inc. (Subsidiary). Associated Motor Finance Company PLC, which was a subsidiary of the Company as at 31 March 2026, was
amalgamated with LB Finance PLC with effect from 31 July 2026. The Company’s parent undertaking and ultimate parent undertaking is Vallibel One PLC,
which is incorporated in Sri Lanka. The Company’s ultimate controlling party is Mr. Dhammika Perera.

05. Amalgamation of Associated Motor Finance Company PLC with LB Finance PLC
Associated Motor Finance Company PLC (AMF), a subsidiary of LB Finance PLC was amalgamated with and into LB Finance PLC with effect from 31 July
2026, in accordance with the provisions of the Companies Act No. 07 of 2007 and applicable regulatory requirements. Following the amalgamation,
LB Finance PLC continues as the surviving legal entity and the operations, assets and liabilities of AMF have been incorporated into those of the Company.

The amalgamation has been accounted for using merger accounting, whereby the assets and liabilities of AMF have been recognised at their existing carrying
amounts. The goodwill of Rs. 952 Mn previously recognised in the Consolidated Financial Statements in relation to the acquisition of AMF has been recognised
in the Company’s Financial Statements upon amalgamation. The goodwill will continue to be subject to impairment testing in accordance with
Sri Lanka Accounting Standard – LKAS 36 Impairment of Assets.

The results of AMF have been incorporated into the financial results of the Company from the effective date of the amalgamation.

06. Incorporation of LB Finance Philippines Inc.
During the period, the Company completed the incorporation of LB Finance Philippines Inc., a fully owned subsidiary in the Republic of the Philippines as part
of the Group’s expansion into the Philippine financial services market. The Certificate of Incorporation and Authority to operate as a Finance Company, dated 01
September 2026, was received by the Company on 17 September 2026.

Accordingly, the USD 650,000 previously classified as Advance Investment in Philippines in connection with the proposed investment has been reclassified to
Investment in Subsidiary upon incorporation. LB Finance Philippines Inc. has been included as a subsidiary of the Group from the date control was established.

07. Events after the Reporting Period
There have been no material events occurring after the reporting date that require adjustments to or disclosure in the Financial Statements.

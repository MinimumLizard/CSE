1.   Principal Activities and Nature of Operations

     The Company owns and operates Jetwing Lighthouse and Jetwing Kurulubedda which are targeted at the up-
     market leisure travelers.
     The Fully owned subsidiary of the Company, Unawatuna Properties (Pvt) Ltd (Incorporated in Sri Lanka) which
     operates Hotel J Unawatuna commenced commercial operation during the month of December 2017.
     On 8th April 2025, the Company acquired 100% of the voting shares of Dream Beach Resort (Pvt) Ltd an unlisted
     company (Incorporated in Sri Lanka) which owns a Hotel operating as Mask & Tide Ambalangoda.

2.   Basis of Preparation

     The interim financial statements have been prepared in accordance with the Sri Lanka Accounting Standard
     (LKAS) 34 - Interim financial reporting.

3.   Approval of Financial Statements

     The interim financial statements of the Group and the Company for the three months ended 30 th June 2026,
     were authorized for issue by the Board of Directors on 4th August 2026.

4.   Changes in Accounting Policies

     These interim financial statements have been prepared in accordance with the accounting policies set out in the
     Annual Report for the year ended 31.03.2026.

5.   Fair value measurement and related fair value disclosures

     different from the transaction prices at the date of initial recognition. There were no transfers between Level 1
     and Level 2 and no transfers into or out of Level 3 categories as per the fair value hierarchy, during the period.
     The fair value changes on financial instruments in Level 3 category were properly recorded in the statement of
     other comprehensive income and there were no purchases and/or disposals during the period. Fair valuation

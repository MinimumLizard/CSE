Performance overview
Senkadagala Finance PLC delivered a strong performance for the nine months ended 31 December 2025,
surpassing the Rs. 50 Bn milestone in total assets, reflecting a 26% increase compared to March 2025. Growth in
net operating income moderated to 6%, primarily due to the unusually high FVTPL gains recognised in the quarter
ended 31 December 2024, which elevated the comparative base. Asset quality improved, with the gross Stage 3
loans ratio declining to 6.45%. Provisioning remained prudent, with Stage 3 impairment coverage at 53.45% and
total impairment coverage at 7.94% as at 31 December 2025, compared to 52.50% and 9.00%, respectively, as at
31 December 2024. The Company’s strategy of maintaining robust provisioning buffers, together with the 28%
growth in loans and advances during the nine-month period ended 31 December 2025, resulted in higher
impairment charges for the period. While this strengthened the overall resilience of the balance sheet, it moderated
profit growth for the period.

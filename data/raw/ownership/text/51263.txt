7.   Financial Impact of Commissioning of Ground-Mounted Solar PV Project:

        During the period, two 5 MW ground-mounted Solar PV projects, namely Dolahena I and Dolahena
         II located in Matara (with a total capacity of 10 MW), were connected to the National Grid on 10
         September 2025. Accordingly, an amount of Rs. 1,174,775,371/- was capitalised from Capital Work-
         in-Progress, while capital expenditure amounting to Rs. 558,315,268/- was directly capitalised under
         Property, Plant and Equipment with effect from the date the assets were available for their intended
         use.


        To finance the above investment, the Company obtained a term loan facility of Rs. 1,372,000,000/-
         from Commercial Bank of Ceylon PLC, repayable over a period of 84 months following a one-year
         grace period. The facility is secured by a floating primary mortgage of Rs. 472,000,000/- over the
         project land and Rs. 900,000,000/- over the project’s movable assets.


        Following the commencement of commercial operations, the solar projects contributed revenue of
         Rs.264.1 million to the Company during the period.


        In addition, a deferred tax reversal gain of Rs. 418.6 million, arising from enhanced capital allowance
         benefits recognised upon capitalisation of the Solar PV project as an expansion under the
         Company’s BOI Agreement, has been recognised in the Statement of Profit or Loss. Accordingly, a
         deferred tax asset of Rs. 250.8 million has been recognised in the Statement of Financial Position.

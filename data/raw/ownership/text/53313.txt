Asia Asset Finance PLC

                      Quarterly financial review
                          Quarter and six months ended 30 September 2026

Asia Asset Finance PLC (AAF) reported a 110% year-on-year increase in profit after tax
to Rs. 838.5 million for the six months ended 30 September 2026. Profit before tax rose
140% to Rs. 1,622.5 million, while second-quarter profit after tax increased 87% to Rs.
409.1 million. Growth was led by the gold loan business and improved operating
efficiency.

Profit after tax




Performance and outlook
For the six months, interest income grew 75% to Rs. 6,859.4 million and net interest
income rose 92% to Rs. 3,783.2 million. The annualised net interest margin widened to
12.9% from 11.0%, while the cost-to-income ratio improved to 38.9% from 49.2%.
Annualised return on equity increased to 28.5% from 20.1%.
Total assets increased 24% since 31 March 2026 to Rs. 66.63 billion, while net loans and
advances grew 10% to Rs. 51.88 billion. Gold loans accounted for approximately 73% of
total lending. With women representing 56.6% of active customers, AAF continues to
support financial inclusion and women’s economic independence through access to
secured credit.
Asset quality improved year on year, with the gross non-performing ratio declining to
7.6% from 9.4% and the net ratio to 4.5% from 4.9%. Impairment charges rose 36% to
Rs. 365.7 million in line with portfolio growth, while provision coverage stood at 42.6%.
Customer deposits grew 37% over the six months to Rs. 35.51 billion. Shareholders’
funds increased 47% to Rs. 7.01 billion, supported by the Rs. 1.50 billion rights issue and
retained profits. Total capital adequacy stood at 18.2%, and liquid assets were 2.98
times the required minimum. Muthoot Finance Limited held 78.4% of ordinary shares.
AAF will continue to grow its secured gold loan and leasing portfolios, with a focus on
asset quality, cost efficiency and prudent liquidity management.

 An operating segment is a component of the Company that engages in business activities from which it may earn revenues and incur expenses, including revenues and expenses that relate to transactions with any of the company’s other components. All operating segments’ operating results are reviewed
 regularly by the Senior Management to make decisions about resources to be allocated to the segment and to assess its performance, and for which discrete financial information is available.



 Segment results that are reported to the Senior Management Personnel and the Board of Directors include items directly attributable to a segment as well as those that can be allocated on a reasonable basis.

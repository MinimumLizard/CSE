FINANCIAL REVIEW



Cable Solutions PLC delivered a resilient topline performance during 3QFY26, supported by
steady export demand and the continued scaling of recently onboarded customers, despite a more
challenging cost environment driven by elevated raw material prices.

Group revenue for 3QFY26 amounted to LKR 841mn, recording a 6% YoY increase compared to
LKR 793mn in the corresponding period last year. Growth was primarily driven by export and
deemed export customers, supported by healthy order inflows from new clients. For the nine-
month period ended December 2025, Group revenue reached LKR 2.6bn, representing a 9% YoY
increase and reflecting sustained demand momentum across core markets.

From a geographic perspective, the India operation continued to increase its revenue contribution
at the topline level, reflecting the gradual ramp-up of operations.

Despite higher revenues, profitability was impacted as costs increased at a faster pace, mainly
due to elevated raw material prices, particularly copper. While pricing pass-through mechanisms
are in place, timing lags and previously committed orders constrained the Company’s ability to
fully offset these cost increases during the quarter. Consequently, gross profit declined
marginally to LKR 269mn, while gross margin compressed to 31.9% from 34.1% in 3QFY25.

At the operating level, Group EBIT declined to LKR 129mn, reflecting a 28% YoY decrease.
Administrative expenses recorded a significant YoY increase, largely due to a distortion arising
from a prior-year write-back of approximately LKR 25mn relating to the India operation recorded
in 3QFY25, which had reduced Group administrative expenses in that period. The absence of this
write-back in 3QFY26, together with additional overheads arising from senior management hires
and operational scale-up initiatives, resulted in higher reported administrative costs. At the
company level, administration expenses increased by 18% YoY during 3QFY26. The compression
in gross margins, coupled with continued investments in the operating base, weighed on overall
profitability.

Group net profit for 3QFY26 stood at LKR 105mn, reflecting a 29% YoY decline and translating to
a net profit margin of 12.4%. The decline in net profit was partially mitigated by a reduction in
net finance costs during the period.

For the nine-month period, the India operation reported a loss of LKR 16mn, compared to a profit
of LKR 30mn in the corresponding period last year. The YoY decline was largely attributable to
cost write-backs relating to the prior year and a relatively large one-off sale recorded in 9MFY25.
In contrast, no such sales were recorded in 9MFY26, while additional costs were incurred as

The Group’s net debt-to-equity ratio improved to 10.7% as at the end of 3QFY26, reflecting

From a macroeconomic perspective, copper prices have increased significantly in recent months

to reflect higher input costs. Demand trends remain supportive, particularly in renewable energy,
electric mobility, and infrastructure-related segments, while uncertainty surrounding U.S. trade

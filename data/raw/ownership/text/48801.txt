Interim Financial Statements for the Period Ended 30th June 2025

Analysis of Statement of Financial Position

Total Assets

Total Assets of the Group have increased by Rs. 591Mn from Rs. 12,019 Mn as at 31st March 2025 to Rs. 12,610
Mn as at 30th June 2025. Total Non-Current Assets were Rs 6,456 Mn and Total Current Assets were Rs. 6,154
Mn as at 30th June 2025.

Equity Attributable to Owners of the Company
Equity attributable to Parent Company’s Shareholders has increased by Rs. 277 Mn from Rs. 5,158 Mn as at 31st
March 2025 to Rs. 5,435 Mn as at 30th June 2025.

Net Assets Per Share (NAPS)

The NAPS have increased to Rs. 29.01 as at 30th June 2025 compared with Rs. 27.54 per share recorded as at 31st
March 2025.

Analysis on Statement of Profit or Loss and Other Comprehensive Income

Revenue & Expenses
Group Revenue has increased by 20 % to Rs. 3,798 Mn for the quarter ended 30th June 2025 when compared with
Rs. 3,147 Mn in the corresponding quarter of the previous year. The composition of the Group Revenue for the
quarter is 49 % from exports sales and 51% from local sales.

Total group admin & selling expenses has decreased by 0.1% to Rs. 460 Mn for the quarter ended 30th June 2025
compared with Rs. 461 Mn in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

Profit / (Loss) After Tax

The Group reported a profit amounting to Rs. 80Mn for the quarter ended 30th June 2025 compared to loss of Rs
86 Mn reported in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

Earnings / (Loss) Per Share
Earnings per Share was Rs 0.43 for the quarter ended 30th June 2025, compared to a Loss per Share of Rs. 0.46 in
the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

   7    Wage Increase and related retirement benefit obligations
        The Hon. Minister of Finance, in the Budget Speech for the year 2026 presented to Parliament on 7 November 2025, proposed an increase in the estate workers’ daily
        consolidated wage from Rs. 1,350 to Rs. 1,550, together with an additional daily attendance allowance of Rs. 200 paid by the Government, with effect from 1 January
        2026.

        Subsequently, Regional Plantation Companies (RPCs) reached an agreement / Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with the Ministry of Plantation and Community
        Infrastructure (MPCI), the Ministry of Labour (MoL), the Employers’ Federation of Ceylon (EFC), and the Planters’ Association of Ceylon (PA) to legalise the wage increase
        and the daily attendance allowance with effect from January 2026.

        Consequently, the impact arising from the increase in the estate workers’ wage has been incorporated into the retirement benefit obligation based on the actuarial
        valuation carried out as at the reporting date.

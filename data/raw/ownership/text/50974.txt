QUARTERLY REVIEW for the first quarter ended 31 March 2026
The Group’s revenue for the first quarter of 2026 was Rs. 8,061.2 Mn, compared to Rs. 7,717.3 Mn
during the same quarter of 2025, representing an increase of Rs. 343.9 Mn or 4%. The Company's
revenue for the first quarter of 2026 was Rs. 7,385.8 Mn, compared to Rs. 7,434.2 Mn during the same
quarter of 2025, representing a decrease of Rs. 48.4 Mn or 0.6%.


The Group reported a profit of Rs. 1,152.0 Mn for the first quarter of 2026, compared to a profit of
Rs. 960.3 Mn during the same quarter of 2025, representing an increase of Rs 191.7 Mn or 20%. The
Company reported a profit of Rs. 1,210.9 Mn for the first quarter of 2026, compared to a profit of
Rs. 932.5 Mn during the same quarter of 2025, representing an increase of Rs. 278.4 Mn or 30%.


The Group reported a marginal increase in revenue during the quarter under review, primarily driven
by higher contributions from the chicken and Broiler Day Old Chicks (DOCs) segments, due to
improved demand during the festive season. This growth was strengthened by a shift in consumer
preference towards value-added processed chicken products, alongside higher average selling prices
and increased volumes. However, revenue from the feed segment declined marginally due to the
transfer of savings from lower import costs in the form of increased discounts to customers. Group
profitability improved compared to the corresponding quarter of the previous year, mainly driven by
higher average selling prices for chicken meat and Broiler DOCs.


The Company and the Group have adhered to the Sri Lanka Financial Reporting Standards in the
preparation of the interim financial statements for the period ended 31 March 2026.


There have been no other events subsequent to the interim period which require disclosure in the
interim financial statements.

NOTES TO THE INTERIM FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
1. CORPORATE INFORMATION
  Ceylon Grain Elevators PLC (the “Company”) is a “Quoted Public Company” with limited liability,
  incorporated and domiciled in Sri Lanka. The address of the Company’s registered office is No.15, Rock
  House Lane, Colombo 15, Sri Lanka.
  The principal business of the Group are manufacturing and selling of poultry feed and other animal feed,
  importing and selling of poultry equipment, drugs and vaccines, operating of poultry breeder farms, raising
  grandparent and parent stock and hatcheries, hatching and selling of day old chicks, operation of
  commercial farms, poultry processing and distribution and provision of warehouse facilities.
  The Company was listed in the Colombo Stock Exchange under Manufacturing Sector. Prima Limited,
  Singapore, holds 45.45% of the issued share capital of the Company.

2. APPROVAL OF FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
  The interim financial statements of the Company and the Group for the three months ended 31 March
  2026 are unaudited and authorised for issue by the Board of Directors on 30 April 2026.
3. BASIS OF PREPARATION
  The interim financial statements of the Company and the Group have been prepared in compliance with
  Sri Lanka Accounting Standard (LKAS) 34 - Interim Financial Reporting.
  The interim financial statements have been prepared on a historical cost basis except for defined benefit
  obligation and biological assets.
4. ACCOUNTING POLICIES
  The accounting policies adopted are consistent with those of the previous financial year.

5. CONTINGENT LIABILITIES
  There has not been any significant change in the nature of the contingent liabilities, which were disclosed
  in the Annual Report for the year ended 31 December 2024.

6. EVENTS AFTER THE REPORTING PERIOD
  Subsequent to 31 March 2026, there are no events, which require adjustments to or disclosure in the
  financial statements.


7. COMPARATIVE INFORMATION
  The presentation and classification of the financial statements of the previous period have been adjusted
  where relevant, for better presentation and to be comparable with current reporting.

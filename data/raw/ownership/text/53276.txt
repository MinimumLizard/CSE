FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
     30TH SEPTEMBER 2026
Bairaha Farms Plc




I am pleased to report that the Group achieved a cumulative net profit of Rs. 456 million for the six-month
period, reflecting a commendable 13% increase compared to Rs. 403 million recorded in the
corresponding period of the previous financial year. Profit before tax (with Share of loss from Joint
Ventures) for the six-month period rose by 52% to Rs. 681 Mn, from Rs. 448 Mn in the corresponding
period of last year. This improvement reflects strong and sustained demand prevailing for both Chicken
and Broiler chicks.

It may be apparent that the growth in net profit is lower than the growth in profit before tax. In the
previous year, the tax charge was reduced by the utilisation of tax losses brought forward from prior years,
whereas the entire profit for the current period is subject to income tax. The net profit for the period
therefore reflects the Group’s performance after absorbing the entirety of tax charge.

The share of loss from the joint ventures was Rs. 59 Mn for the six-month period, compared to a loss of
Rs. 12 Mn in the corresponding period of last year. This was mainly due to increased prices of feed raw
material and increased special commodity levy on corn from Rs 25/kg to Rs 50/Kg. As only 20% of
approved quantity of corn was permitted to be imported during the period, approval was instead granted
for the use of local rice as an alternative, which resulted in higher input costs. As feed represents a
significant share of the cost of poultry production, we trust that a stable and predictable raw material
import policy framework will support the competitiveness of the feed industry and the wider poultry
sector.

The company continues to focus on enhancing operational efficiency and maintaining product quality,
while proactively managing supply chain challenges. We remain confident that our strategic initiatives
and experience in the industry will enable us to sustain growth momentum and continue to deliver value
to our shareholders.

Performance Review of Kotagala Plantations PLC (KPPLC)- Financial Year
2025/2026

KPPLC has achieved a profit before tax of Rs. 52.2 Mn, recording a decrease of 31%
compared to last year. This decline is primarily due to the impact of the wage increase
effected on September 10, 2024 and reduction in tea prices.

The Group recorded a profit before tax of Rs.47.8 Mn, which included results of Rubber
& Allied Products (Colombo) Limited a wholly owned subsidiary.

Production increased by 0.22 Mn kgs for tea and 0.38 Mn kgs for oil palm, while rubber
production decreased by 0.03 Mn kgs due to adverse weather conditions. However, the
company’s revenue increased by 20% to Rs. 1.3 Bn, driven by the increase in production
and higher prices for rubber and oil palm.

The tea sector contributed a gross profit of Rs. 127.3 Mn, compared to Rs. 227.6 Mn.
recorded in the previous year. The gross loss in the rubber sector has increased by 37%
to Rs. 77.6 Mn compared to the previous period. In contrast, the gross profit of the oil
palm segment has increased to Rs. 84.6 Mn, from Rs. 35.3 Mn last year. However, the
overall gross profit stood at Rs. 134.2 Mn, reflecting a decrease of 35% compared to last
year.

With the steady performance of the Company, net assets per share has increased to
Rs.7.15 from Rs.7.00 recorded as of 31st March 2025.

Future Outlook

The company is focused in maintaining a positive bottom-line performance despite the
challenges faced by the industry.

To mitigate the recent increase in wages and the dearth of workers, the company has already
commenced mechanization including harvesting and field operations along with automation
in the processing Centers.

KPPLC is also committed to the development of Human Capital in terms of training &
development, providing opportunities to enhance worker earnings including
compliance with Social Capital together with Regulatory compliances & Corporate
Governance.

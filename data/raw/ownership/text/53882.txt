QUARTER REVIEW

Blue Diamonds Jewellery Worldwide PLC
Quarter Ended 30 September 2026


Blue Diamonds Jewellery Worldwide PLC presents its unaudited financial results for the quarter and six-month period
ended 30 September 2026.


For the quarter ended 30 September 2026, Group revenue increased to Rs. 25.6 million compared with Rs. 1.61 million in
the corresponding quarter of the previous year. Gross profit improved to Rs. 17.8 million from a gross loss of Rs. 1.04
million. However, the Group recorded a profit of Rs. 2.0 million compared with Rs. 13.02 million loss in the previous
year, mainly due to operating and finance expenses.

For the six-month period, Group revenue amounted to Rs. 36.1 million, compared with Rs. 24.78 million in the
corresponding period of 2025. The Group recorded a loss of Rs. 3.5 million compared with Rs. 6.07 million in the
previous year.

As at 30 September 2026, the Group's total assets amounted to approximately Rs. 206.6 million, while total equity stood at
Rs. 36.1 million. Current liabilities amounted to approximately Rs. 167.22 million, and cash and cash equivalents stood at
approximately Rs. 1.03 million.


Management will focus on improving sales, maintaining gross margins, controlling operating expenses and strengthening
the Group's liquidity and working capital position. The Board will continue to monitor the Group's financial performance
and ability to meet its obligations.

             On 2nd June 2025, Ambeon Capital PLC made an initial investment of LKR 600 million in the Project Holding Company (which will be utilized in Sri

             Exchange (AIM: CMET). The Project will be carried by Capital Metals through two companies in Sri Lanka that hold exploration and industrial

             Lanka, Redgate Lanka (Private) Limited (“Project Holding Company”).

Note 01
During the period, the Company identified a net gain of Rs. 107,322,423, relating to periods up to 31 December 2024, arising from adjustments to Other
non-financial assets, Trade and other payables and Income tax payable.

As this amount does not relate to the financial performance of the current period, it has been adjusted to the opening balance of retained profit as at 31
December 2024.

The effect of the adjustment on the Statement of Financial Position as at 31 December 2024 is as follows:
                                                                  Rs.

Increase in Income tax payable                                 (1,101,358)

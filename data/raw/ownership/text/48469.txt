Fair values are determined according to the following hierarchy:

Level 2 – valuation technique using observable inputs: financial instruments with quoted prices for similar instruments in active markets or quoted prices for identical or similar
instruments in inactive markets and financial instruments are valued using models where all significant inputs are observable.

Level 3 – valuation technique with significant unobservable inputs: This category includes all instruments valued using valuation techniques where

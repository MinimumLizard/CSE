Performance Review of Kotagala Plantations PLC (KPPLC)- Financial Year
2025/2026

KPPLC has recorded a profit before tax of Rs.492.6 Mn. for the financial year of 2025/26
which is a decrease of 19% when compared with the corresponding period of the last
year. This decline is primarily due to the impact of the wage increases effected on
September 10, 2024 (35% increase), 1st January 2026 (15% increase) and decline in tea
prices, compared to last year.

The Group recorded a profit before tax of Rs.367.7 Mn, which includes results of Rubber
& Allied Products (Colombo) Limited a wholly owned subsidiary.

The tea sector contributed a gross profit of Rs. 540 Mn, compared to Rs. 758 Mn. recorded
in the previous year. The gross loss in the rubber sector has increased to Rs.85 Mn from
Rs.57 Mn in last year. However, the gross profit of the oil palm segment has increased to
Rs.301 Mn, from Rs.202 Mn last year. The overall gross profit stood at Rs.757 Mn,
reflecting a decrease of 16% compared to last year.

With the steady performance of the Company, net assets per share has increased to
Rs.8.11 from Rs.7.00 recorded as of 31st March 2025.

Future Outlook

The company is focused in maintaining a positive bottom-line performance despite the
challenges faced by the industry.

To mitigate the recent increase in wages and the dearth of workers, the company has already
commenced mechanization including harvesting and field operations along with automation
in the processing Centers.

KPPLC is also committed to the development of Human Capital in terms of training &
development, providing opportunities to enhance worker earnings including compliance
with Social Capital together with Regulatory compliances & Corporate Governance.

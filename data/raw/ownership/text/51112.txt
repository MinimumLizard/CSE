6         Notes to the condensed interim financial statements

    6.1   Condensed Interim financial statements


          The Statement of Financial Position as at 31st March 2026, Statement of Profit or Loss, Statement of Other Comprehensive Income,
          Statement of Cash Flows and Statement of Changes in Equity of the Company for the period ended there of are drawn up from Unaudited
          financial statements of the Company and provide information as required by the Colombo Stock Exchange and LKAS 34. Further,
          provisions of the Companies Act No.7 of 2007 has been considered in preparing the said financial statements of the Company.

          The presentation and classification of the financial statements of the previous period have been amended, where relevant, for better
          presentation and to be comparable with those of the current period. The condensed interim financial statements are presented in Sri Lankan
          Rupees (LKR) and all values are rounded to the nearest thousand except when otherwise indicated.

    6.2   Basis of preparation

          The same Accounting Policies and methods of computation as mentioned in the audited financial statements for the year ended 31
          December 2025 have been adopted in the preparation of these condensed financial statements.

          The Company has adopted Amendments to SLFRS 4 in the preparation of financial statements. Application of SLFRS 9 is not considered,
          considering the temporary exemption permitted under Para 20B of SLFRS 4 as the Company's activities are predominantly connected with
          insurance.

    6.3   Other income / (expenses) includes foreign exchange gains / (losses) and rent income.

    6.4   All expenses related to management expenditure is fully provided for in the financial statements.

    6.5   Contingencies, capital and other commitments
          There are no significant changes in the nature of the contingent liabilities that were disclosed in the Financial Statements for the year ended
          31 December 2025.

    6.6   Event after the reporting period
          No circumstances have arisen since the reporting date, which would require adjustments to or disclosure in the financial statements.

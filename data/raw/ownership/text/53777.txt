 2.3 Temporary Exemption from SLFRS 9
     An insurer that meets the criteria in paragraph 20B of SLFRS 4 (amended) provides a temporary exemption that permits, but does not require, the insurer to apply
     LKAS 39 – Financial Instruments: Recognition and Measurement rather than SLFRS 9 for annual periods beginning before 1st January 2023.

    An insurer may apply the temporary exemption from SLFRS 9 if, and only if:
    • It has not previously applied any version of SLFRS 9, other than only the requirements for the presentation of gains and losses of financial liabilities designated at
    fair value through profit or loss and;
    • its activities are predominantly connected with insurance, at its annual reporting date that immediately precedes 1st April 2016, or at a subsequent annual
    reporting date.

    Taking into account the above criteria, Co-operative Insurance Company PLC and Coop Life Insurance Limited being predominantly connected with insurance
    activities; both Companies may continue to apply LKAS 39 – Financial Instruments: Recognition and Measurement rather than SLFRS 9 for annual periods
    beginning before 1st January 2026.

     (A) Value Added Tax on Financial services and Corporate Income Tax assessments issued by the Commissioner General of Inland Revenue
     The Company received Notices of Assessment from the Inland Revenue Department in respect of Value Added Tax (VAT) on Financial Services for the taxable periods from 01 January
     2018 to 31 December 2018, 01 January 2021 to 31 December 2021, and 01 January 2022 to 31 December 2022. These assessments assert that the Company is liable for VAT on financial
     services.The Company has formally appealed against these assessments on the basis that it operates in the insurance business and, therefore, is not liable for VAT on financial services. The

     assessments and have likewise lodged appeals.The total tax exposure amounted to Rs. 151.86 million, comprising:VAT on Financial Services: Rs. 81.66 million,The remaining balance of
     Rs. 70.14 million relates to disputed VAT and Social Security Contribution Levy (SSCL) liabilities. The Company has submitted valid appeals in respect of these assessments, and the
     matters remain under review.Accordingly, no provision has been made in the financial statements for the disputed taxes and any related penalties.

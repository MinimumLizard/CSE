Dear Shareholders,
On behalf of the Board of Directors, I am pleased to present the financial results of the
Company for the first quarter ended 30th June 2026.
The Company recorded a revenue of Rs. 311.7 million for the quarter under review
compared to Rs. 590.1 million in the corresponding period of the previous year,
representing a 47% decline. The principal factor contributing to this decrease was the
disruption in export demand resulting from the geopolitical tensions and conflict in the
Gulf region, one of the Company's key export markets. The uncertainty arising from the
situation affected customer demand, delayed shipments, and impacted purchasing patterns
during the period, resulting in a significant reduction in export volumes.
The 50% reduction in cost of sales is mainly due to reduction in Tea price and cost control
initiatives by the Company. Consequently, gross profit amounted to Rs. 50.1 million,
compared to Rs. 72.0 million in the corresponding period last year. Distribution expenses
also reduced in line with the lower level of business activity, while administrative expenses
increased marginally due to inflationary cost pressures.
Net finance expenses increased by 55% to Rs. 28.0 million, reflecting higher borrowing
costs during the period. As a result, the Company reported a loss before tax and net loss of
Rs. 32.5 million, compared to a profit of Rs. 2.3 million in the corresponding quarter of the
previous year.
While the external environment remains challenging, the Board and management continue
to focus on cost optimization, operational efficiency, prudent working capital management,
and strengthening the Company's presence in both existing and new export markets. We
remain optimistic that, with the gradual stabilization of regional markets and the continued
execution of our strategic initiatives, the Company is well positioned to improve its
performance in the coming quarters.
On behalf of the Board, I extend my sincere appreciation to our shareholders, customers,
business partners, and employees for their continued trust and support.

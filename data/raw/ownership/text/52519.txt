07.PREFERENCE SHARE AGREEMENT

The accounting implications arising from the Preference Share Agreement entered into by Lanka Energy International
(Private) Limited (“LEI”), a subsidiary of the Company, in 2019 have been incorporated into the financial statements as
an equity instrument by allocating the economic benefits of the preference shares to NCI for the year ended 31 March
2026. Accordingly, the resulting impact will be accounted for as a prior-year adjustment, and the comparative financial
information will be restated and disclosed in the notes to the audited financial statements for the year ended 31 March
2026. To align with this restatement, the comparative figures presented in these interim financial statements have
been adjusted.

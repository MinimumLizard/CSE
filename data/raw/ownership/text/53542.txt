Chairman & Chief Executive Officer’s Review

Dear Shareholders,

I am pleased to share Kapruka Holdings PLC's results for the second quarter and first half ended 30 September 2026.
This is our third consecutive profitable quarter. For the first six months, we turned last year's loss of LKR 10.45 million
into a profit of LKR 35.64 million. Revenue for the half grew 18% and passed LKR 1 billion, and we did it with less
inventory, less debt and more cash than six months ago.

Second Quarter Results

Group revenue grew 15% to LKR 479.51 million, compared with LKR 416.10 million in the same quarter last year.
Gross profit grew faster, up 25% to LKR 193.75 million. Gross margin rose to 40.4%, from 37.1% a year ago. These
revenue numbers understate how much our business has grown. In past years, we sold more of our own goods and
booked the full sale price as revenue. Today, more orders come through our marketplace, where we book only our
commission. A marketplace order adds less to revenue, but more to profit, because we carry no stock and pay no
purchase cost. This is why gross profit is growing faster than revenue.

Our operating costs grew 10%, slower than revenue. As a result, we made an operating profit of LKR 5.72 million,
against a loss of LKR 5.77 million last year. Profit before tax was LKR 16.81 million (last year: a loss of LKR 3.05
million). Profit after tax was LKR 16.40 million (last year: a loss of LKR 4.89 million). Revenue was lower than in the
first quarter, as expected. The first quarter includes Sinhala and Tamil New Year and Mother's Day, our busiest gifting
season of the half.


First Half Results

For the six months, revenue grew 18% to LKR 1,020.99 million, compared with LKR 864.62 million last year. Gross
profit grew 28% to LKR 417.99 million, and gross margin improved by 3.3 percentage points to 40.9%. Operating
profit was LKR 14.29 million, against a loss of LKR 8.10 million last year. Profit before tax was LKR 41.71 million (last
year: a loss of LKR 4.66 million). Profit after tax was LKR 35.64 million (last year: a loss of LKR 10.45 million), an
improvement of LKR 46.09 million.

One area we are watching closely: over the half, administrative and selling costs grew a little faster than revenue
(about 20% against 18%). In the second quarter, costs grew slower than revenue, and we intend to keep it that way.


A Stronger Balance Sheet

Compared with 31 March 2026, we now hold more cash, owe less and carry less stock. Cash and cash equivalents rose
225% to LKR 151.86 million, from LKR 46.73 million. Borrowings, excluding overdrafts, fell 34% to LKR 172.85
million, from LKR 261.79 million. Total equity rose 4% to LKR 861.30 million, and net asset value per share rose 4% to
LKR 5.25, from LKR 5.04. Inventory fell 23% to LKR 133.79 million, from LKR 172.97 million, even as revenue grew.
That is the marketplace model working: more of what we sell is held by our partners, not by us.



Our Growth Platforms

Kapruka Partner Central lets other sellers reach our customers and use our delivery and payment systems. We earn
on each sale without buying the stock. This is a key reason our margins are rising while our inventory is falling.


Kapruka Services Platform takes us beyond physical products into services that customers can book online. It builds
on the same customers and brand we have built over more than two decades, with little extra capital.

Kapruka Cross Border sells Sri Lankan brands on Amazon US, Amazon UK, Amazon Canada and other global
marketplaces. It earns US dollars, which gives the Group a natural hedge and a market far larger than Sri Lanka.

Charitha Factory: Turning Our AI Experience into a Business

Over the past two years, we have built and run AI agents across our own business, in customer service, product
discovery, content and back-office work. Sri Lankan companies now want the same, and demand for AI agents is
growing fast.

To meet that demand, we have set up Charitha Factory, a business-to-business AI solutions company that designs and
deploys AI agents for Sri Lankan businesses. It operates under Kapruka Tech Root (Pvt) Ltd, a fully owned subsidiary of
Kapruka Holdings PLC. We expect it to start contributing to results from the next quarter.

Inside Kapruka, AI continues to lower the cost of running the business. We remain on track to automate 40% of back-
office work in the coming quarters, so we can handle more orders without adding staff at the same pace.

Looking Ahead

For the second half of the year, our priorities are simple:
• Stay profitable every quarter.
• Keep costs growing slower than revenue.
• Keep improving cash, debt and inventory levels.
• Grow Partner Central, Cross Border and Charitha Factory.

Three profitable quarters in a row show that the changes of the past two years are working. Our job now is to keep this
going and turn it into lasting value for shareholders.

On behalf of the Board and the management team, thank you for your continued trust.

Stable Financial Performance in Q2 2026

SDB bank maintained a profitable performance during Q2 2026, recording a Profit After Tax (PAT) of Rs. 106
Mn. This performance was achieved despite the concessions granted to tenor extended portfolio Customer
and associated impairment provisions, The Bank's continued focus on disciplined financial management,
prudent risk governance, and strategic business expansion reinforces its ability to navigate evolving
market conditions while pursuing sustainable growth. Growth in customer advances across agriculture,
SMEs, cooperatives, and retail banking reflects continued customer trust and supports the Bank's long-term
objectives. Ongoing initiatives to improve operational efficiency, strengthen asset quality, and diversify
revenue streams are expected to further enhance stakeholder value and support future growth
opportunities. Notably, all capital adequacy ratios remain comfortably above the regulatory minimum
requirements, reinforcing the Bank’s strong capital position.

The Bank’s strengthened credit discipline, coupled with proactive portfolio management, demonstrates its
balanced approach to managing risk and pursuing growth opportunities. Continued focus on building a
stable, low-cost deposit base and enhancing funding efficiency has further reinforced the Bank’s financial
stability. Anchored in its purpose-driven approach, SDB Bank remains committed to delivering sustainable
growth while contributing to inclusive and forward-looking economic development.

Sustainability Commitments

During Q2 2026, SDB bank continued to strengthen its commitment to sustainable and inclusive growth
through responsible banking practices and financial inclusion initiatives especially through the Rural
Upliftment Program of the Bank. By supporting key sectors such as agriculture, SMEs, cooperatives, and
retail banking, the Bank remains focused on creating positive economic impact and delivering sustainable
progress for the communities it serves.

network of 94 branches island-wide, the Bank provides a comprehensive range of financial services to its
Retail, SME, Co-operative, and Business Banking clients across the country. Environmental, Social, and

propel Sri Lanka to new heights.

Statutory Reserve Fund
Every Licensed Specialised Bank has to make a Provision not less than 5% out of profit after tax to the Statutory Reserve Fund. Such provision should be made annually as stipulated by the Banking Act No. 30 of 1988 as amended by Banking (Amendment) Act No. 24 of 2024 until
the said Reserve Fund is equal to 50% of the Equity Capital of the Bank. Thereafter, the Bank has to make a provision not less than 2% out of profit after tax to the Statutory Reserve Fund until the said Fund is equal to the Equity Capital of the Bank.



General Reserve
The General Reserve is created after provisioning for a Statutory Reserve Fund and Interim Dividend payments for the respective shareholders, this reserve will be used by the Bank for the future capitalisation purposes of the Bank.



Fair value through OCI / Available For Sale Reserve / Revaluation Reserve
These reserves consist of fair value adjustments relevant to investment portfolios and land and buiding.

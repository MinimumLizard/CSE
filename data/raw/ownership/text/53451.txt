CEO MESSAGE
DEAR SHAREHOLDERS,
It is a great pleasure that I share with you the interim ﬁnancial statements of Colombo Land and Development Company PLC
for the quarter ended 30th September 2026. Overall, the Group recorded a Revenue of Rs.416 Mn (YoY 23%), a Gross Proﬁt of
Rs.314 mn (YoY 31%) and a proﬁt before tax of Rs 114 Mn (YoY 34%) for the 6 months ended 30th September 2026.


RENTAL INCOME
Overall rental income increased by 42% compared to the corresponding quarter. The occupancy during this period stood at
97% compared to 96% in 2025.


CAR PARK INCOME
The revenue from Car Parks during this period increased by 11% when compared to the corresponding quarter. The average
monthly revenue of the Gas Land car park decreased by 9%, Liberty Plaza increased by 23% and Peoples Park remained ﬂat
during the quarter under review.


FINANCE EXPENSES
Finance costs increased by 59% compared to the corresponding period of the previous year, mainly due to a one-off interest
commitment and an increase in lending rates during the quarter under review.


FUTURE OUTLOOK

The Company remains committed to driving top-line growth through proactive property management, sustained high
occupancy rates, and strategic rental rate reviews. To mitigate the ongoing pressure of rising ﬁnance costs, we will prioritize
cost optimization, disciplined cash ﬂow management, and prudent capital planning to safeguard proﬁtability and long-term
ﬁnancial resilience. Looking ahead, the convergence of the upcoming festive and tourism seasons is expected to stimulate
retail activity, driving higher mall footfall and increased car park utilization.

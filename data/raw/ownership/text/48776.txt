 9    Reclassification of the Investment in Hatton National Bank PLC (HNB)
      In the preceding quarter, the Group’s investment in HNB was classified as a non-current asset held for sale in accordance with SLFRS 5. During the period, the Group disposed 9.99% of HNB’s
      voting shares and 33% of its non-voting shares. As a result of this disposal, the Group ceased to have significant influence over HNB. Accordingly, the remaining investment, consisting solely of
      non-voting shares, has been reclassified as a financial asset measured at fair value through profit or loss (FVTPL) in accordance with SLFRS 9.

Performance Review for the Twelve Months Ended 31 March 2026

Laxapana PLC

Overview

Laxapana PLC delivered a strong financial and operational performance for the twelve months
ended 31 March 2026 (FY2025/26), demonstrating resilience amidst a competitive market
environment.

The Group reported consolidated revenue of Rs. 3.87 billion, representing a 15% growth compared
to the previous financial year. Profit Before Tax (PBT) increased significantly to Rs. 519 million,
while Profit After Tax (PAT) rose to Rs. 321 million, reflecting year-on-year growth of 104% and
92%, respectively.

The improved performance was primarily driven by increased sales volumes across key product
categories, disciplined cost management initiatives, and enhanced operational efficiencies.
Continued focus on brand-building, dealer engagement, and distribution expansion also
contributed positively towards the Group’s overall performance during the year.

A second interim dividend of Rs. 0.75 per share was paid on 23 March 2026 in respect of the
financial year ended 31 March 2026.

Operating Environment

During FY2025/26, Sri Lanka’s macroeconomic environment continued to demonstrate gradual
stabilization and recovery following the economic challenges experienced in prior years.
Improvements in inflation, relative currency stability, and increased economic activity contributed
positively toward consumer and business sentiment during the year.

However, market conditions remained competitive across the Group’s operating sectors,
particularly within the electrical products industry, where pricing pressures and the availability of
lower-cost alternatives continued to challenge market participants.

In addition, adverse weather conditions resulting from Cyclone Ditwah during late 2025 caused
temporary operational and market disruptions in certain regions. Despite these challenges, the
Group maintained operational continuity and continued to strengthen its market presence through
proactive business strategies and disciplined execution.

During the latter part of the financial year, escalating geopolitical tensions arising from the conflict
in the middle east has resulted in supply chain disruptions, increased freight and energy costs, and
volatility in commodity prices. These developments have also contributed to renewed depreciation
pressure on the Sri Lankan Rupee and a gradual increase in domestic inflationary conditions
including energy prices, leading to higher import and operating costs. The Group continues to
closely monitor these developments and remains focused on proactive supply chain management,
disciplined cost control, and operational efficiency initiatives to mitigate potential business
impacts.

Segment Performance

Energy and Lighting

The Energy and Lighting product segments continued to operate in a highly competitive market
environment during FY2025/26. Nevertheless, the Group successfully maintained sales
momentum through targeted promotional campaigns, strengthened dealer engagement, and
sustained brand-building initiatives.
Demand for LED lighting products continued to improve, supported by increasing consumer
preference for energy-efficient lighting solutions. The battery segment also recorded stable growth
through effective channel management, expanded retail penetration, and consistent product
availability across key markets.

Building Solutions

Sterling Steels (Private) Limited (SSL), a subsidiary of Laxapana PLC, delivered steady
operational performance during the financial year. Demand for purlin and roofing products
remained stable, supported by gradual recovery in construction-related activities.

Operational efficiencies, disciplined production planning, and prudent cost management initiatives
contributed positively towards maintaining margins and profitability within the segment.

Cost Management and Controls

The Group continued to prioritize disciplined cost management and operational efficiency
initiatives throughout FY2025/26. Administrative and operational expenses were carefully
monitored, while efforts to optimize resource utilization and strengthen internal processes
remained ongoing.

The Management also continued to enhance internal controls, reporting systems, and operational
monitoring mechanisms to facilitate timely decision-making and support long-term business
sustainability.

ESG and Governance

During the year, the Group continued to strengthen its Environmental, Social, and Governance
(ESG) initiatives, focusing on sustainability, regulatory compliance, and responsible corporate
practices.

The Group remained committed to improving energy efficiency, waste management practices, and
governance standards as part of its long-term sustainability agenda. The achievement of
GREENSL® certification further reinforced the Group’s commitment to environmentally
responsible operations and sustainable product innovation.

Outlook

Looking ahead to FY2026/27, management remains cautiously optimistic regarding future
business prospects. While competitive pressures, cost volatility, and external economic
uncertainties are expected to persist, the Group remains well-positioned to capitalize on emerging
opportunities through its established brand portfolio, strong distribution network, and disciplined
operational approach.

Strategic priorities will continue to focus on operational excellence, product innovation and
sustainable value creation for all stakeholders.

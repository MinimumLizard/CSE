   REVIEW OF OPERATIONS (DRAFT)

   First Capital Holdings PLC (the Group) recorded a Profit after Tax of Rs. 2.15Bn for the quarter ended 30 June
   2025 compared to Rs. 582Mn in the corresponding period of the previous year.

   The Group’s Net Trading Income before Operating Expenses for the 1st quarter of 2025/26 was Rs. 3.40Bn
   compared to Rs. 1.12Bn in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

   During the quarter, a moderate decline in interest rates was witnessed following a reduction in monetary policy
   rate which resulted in creating a favourable market position for debt securities as well as equity securities.
   Accordingly, the Group’s Primary Dealer division as well as the Corporate Dealing Securities division
   significantly contributed to the Group’s net trading income for the period under review.

   The Primary Dealer division reported a Profit after Tax of Rs. 1.55Bn for the quarter ended 30 June 2025
   (2024/25 – Profit after Tax of Rs. 464Mn). The Results include a trading gain on government securities
   amounting to Rs. 2.06Bn and net interest income of Rs. 503Mn (2024/25 – trading gain on government securities
   of Rs. 462Mn and net interest income of Rs. 387Mn were included).

   The Corporate Finance Advisory and Corporate Dealing Securities divisions reported a Profit after Tax of Rs.
   587Mn for the quarter ended 30 June 2025 (2024/25 – Profit after Tax of Rs. 73Mn).

   The Wealth Management division reported a Profit after Tax of Rs. 27Mn for the quarter ended 30 June 2025
   (2024/25 – Profit after Tax of Rs. 29Mn). The assets under management of the division stood at Rs. 108Bn as
   of 30 June 2025 (31 March 2025 – Rs. 112Bn).

   The Stock Brokering division recorded a Profit after Tax of Rs. 32Mn for the quarter ended 30 June 2025
   (2024/25 – Profit after tax of Rs. 15Mn).

   The Credit Rating of the Company was upgraded by Lanka Credit Rating Agency Limited (LRA) from “A” to
   “A+” changing the outlook from positive to stable in June 2025.

5   Financial instruments- Fair Value Measurement

      The Company measures fair values using the following fair value hierarchy that reflects the significance of the inputs used in
      making the measurements in accordance with SLFRS 13.

      Level - 1
      Financial instruments that are measured in whole or in part by reference to published quotes in an active market. A financial
      instrument is regarded as quoted in an active market if quoted prices are readily and regularly available from an exchange and those
      prices represent actual and regularly occurring market transactions on an arm's length basis.

      Level - 2
      Financial instruments that are measured at fair value on a recurring basis. As market quotes generally are not readily available or
      accessible for these securities, their fair value measures are determined using relevant information generated by market
      transactions involving comparable securities.

      Level - 3
      Valuation techniques using significant unobservable inputs. This category includes all instruments where the valuation technique
      includes inputs not based on observable data and the unobservable inputs have a significant effect on the instrument's valuation.
      This category includes instruments that are valued based on quoted prices for similar instruments where significant unobservable
      adjustments or assumptions are required to reflect differences between the instruments.

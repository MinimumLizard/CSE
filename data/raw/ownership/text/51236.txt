     Chief Executive Officer’s Review
     ___________________________________________________________________

     We are pleased to present the financial results for the three months and full year ended 31 March
     2026.

     The end of Q4 signals the successful conclusion of one of the most transformative years for the
     Company. Total revenue for the year grew 13% year-over-year to LKR 2,058 million. Gross profit
     increased 24% with gross margins expanding 417 basis points to 50%. Normalized EBITDA swung
     from a loss of LKR 120 million to a profit of LKR 200 million, with the margin improving 17 percentage
     points to 10%. These gains flowed through strongly to the bottom line, with Profit Before Tax improving
     LKR 313 million and Profit After Tax improving LKR 249 million year-over-year. Free Cash Flow turned
     positive at LKR 53 million, compared to a negative LKR 331 million in FY2025, representing a
     substantial turnaround of LKR 384 million.

     In Q4 FY2026, hSenid Business Solutions PLC delivered revenue of LKR 522.2 million, reflecting
     4.5% year-over-year growth in LKR terms (1% decline in USD constant currency terms). Subscription
     revenues grew strongly at 38% year-over-year in LKR terms (29% in USD constant currency), with
     recurring revenues rising further to 77% of total revenue. This continued shift toward higher-quality,
     predictable SaaS revenues helped offset a decline in PeoplesHR Tracking Solutions revenue. The
     decline was driven by a combination of softer demand and external supply chain disruptions during
     the quarter.

     While underlying operational profitability remained robust, the unwinding of deferred tax assets
     resulted in a net loss of LKR 8 million for the quarter and LKR 37 million for the full year. This was a
     non-cash item and does not reflect the strong cash generation and operating leverage delivered
     during the year.

     PeoplesHR Cloud remained the cornerstone of our performance, contributing LKR 380 million in Q4,
     up 20% year-over-year in LKR terms (12% in USD constant currency). Our exit Annualized Recurring
     Revenue (ARR) reached USD 5.5 million, providing strong forward visibility. New deal closures
     totalled USD 843,395 in the quarter, up 184% quarter-over-quarter and 3% year-over-year, with 56%
     attributable to PeoplesHR Cloud. This strong sequential recovery signals improving sales momentum
     as we enter FY2027. The Company also delivered positive Free Cash Flow in Q4, achieving an FCF
     margin of 2% for the quarter and 3% for the full year (compared to negative 18% in FY2025).

     During the year, we continued to advance our market development efforts across mid-market and
     enterprise segments in the region. We strengthened collaboration with local implementation partners
     to enhance market access and build customer trust through ecosystem-led engagement. This
     approach is contributing to improved funnel consistency and a strengthening pipeline heading into
     FY2027.

     On the innovation front, we launched Lexi Insights, an AI-powered analytics capability that leverages
     employee data within PeoplesHR to deliver actionable, predictive insights for C-suite decision-making.
     This marks an important step toward our vision of a comprehensive Human Capital Operating System.

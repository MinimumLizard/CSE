6) Restatement of Investment In Subsidiary

     In prior years, the Company held a significant equity interest in Amalgamated Theatres Limited, which provided the
     Company with control over the financial and operating policies of the investee. However, this investment was previously
     classified and presented as an Investment in Equity Securities at Fair Value through Other Comprehensive Income

     Following a detailed assessment of the rights held by the Company and the governance structure of Amalgamated Theatres
     Limited, management has determined that the Company exercises control over the investee, in line with the criteria set out
     in SLFRS 10 – Consolidated Financial Statements. Accordingly, the investment meets the definition of a subsidiary, and
     should have been accounted for using the consolidation method from the date control was obtained.

     This constitutes a misclassification and measurement error in prior periods.
     In accordance with LKAS 8 – Accounting Policies, Changes in Accounting Estimates and Errors, the Company has
     corrected this error retrospectively. As a result, the comparative information presented has been restated, and the opening
     balances of assets, liabilities, and equity for the earliest period presented have been adjusted to reflect the full
     consolidation of Amalgamated Theatres Limited.

     The restatement resulted in the following key adjustments:
       - Recognition of the assets, liabilities, income, and expenses of Amalgamated Theatres Limited in the consolidated
       - Derecognition of the previously presented investment under FVOCI;
       - Adjustment to retained earnings and other relevant equity components as at the beginning of the earliest comparative
       - Update of related disclosures including earnings per share, segment information, and non-controlling interests

    The company engaged in the business of Real Estate and Construction and incorporated as a Private Limited liability company on

02 Basis of Preparation
2.1 These condensed Interim Financial Statements of Myland Developments PLC are for the period ended 30th September 2026. They
    have been prepared in accordance with LKAS 34, Interim Financial Reporting, these Interim Financial Statements have been

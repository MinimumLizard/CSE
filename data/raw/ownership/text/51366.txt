Chairman & Chief Executive Officer’s Review

Dear Shareholders,

I am pleased to share Kapruka Holdings PLC's results for the quarter ended 31 March 2026, a quarter that marks an
inflection point in our journey from Sri Lanka's largest e-commerce platform to a fully integrated, AI-powered digital
commerce ecosystem.

A definitive return to profitability

The numbers tell a clear story. Group revenue grew 19% year-on-year to LKR 547.53 million, but the more
important signal is what happened beneath the top line. Gross profit expanded 39% to LKR 215.51 million, lifting
our gross margin meaningfully and reflecting a deliberate shift toward higher-quality revenue. Operating profit
swung to LKR 10.22 million from an operating loss of LKR 42.82 million in the prior year period, a turnaround
of more than LKR 53 million in a single quarter. Profit Before Tax reached LKR 22.25 million, compared to a loss of
LKR 37.33 million a year earlier.

For the full financial year, the Group delivered revenue of LKR 1.92 billion and Profit Before Tax of LKR 37.56 million.
At the Company level, Kapruka Holdings PLC posted revenue of LKR 210.61 million and Profit Before Tax of LKR 50.69
million.

This is the result of a deliberate two-year restructuring that has tightened cost discipline, reweighted the business
toward asset-light revenue streams, and laid the technology foundation for the next phase of growth.

Three engines compounding in parallel

Kapruka Partner Central, our marketplace, continues to expand both gross margin and capital efficiency by enabling
third-party sellers to plug into our customer base, logistics, and payments infrastructure. Each new partner adds
revenue without adding inventory risk, and the operating leverage compounds quarter on quarter.

Kapruka Services Platform extends Kapruka beyond physical goods into bookable digital services, opening a new
addressable market on top of the same customer network. This is high-margin, recurring-by-nature revenue that
benefits directly from the trust equity we have built over two decades.

Kapruka Cross Border continues to scale our USD revenue base by serving as the e-distributor for Sri Lankan brands
on Amazon US, Amazon UK, Amazon Canada, and other global marketplaces. In an economy where dollar revenue
carries strategic value well beyond its rupee-equivalent contribution, this segment is a structural hedge and a long-
duration growth lever.

AI as the operating system of the business

The most significant strategic shift this year is the embedding of AI across our operations. We have deployed AI-driven
automation in customer service, product discovery, content generation, supply chain workflows, and back-office
processing. We are on track to reach 40% back-office automation within the next several quarters, which we
expect to translate directly into lower unit costs, faster cycle times, and improved operating margins as volume grows.


In practical terms, AI is allowing us to scale revenue without proportionally scaling headcount or fixed costs. That is the
definition of operating leverage, and it is the lever that turns a profitable quarter into a structurally more profitable
business.

Insulating the cost base from fuel volatility

We have also commenced the conversion of our entire delivery fleet to electric vehicles, a multi-quarter program
designed to permanently de-risk one of the most volatile line items on our P&L. Fuel is a structural cost in any last-mile
business, and in the Sri Lankan context it has historically swung with global oil prices and exchange rate movements
outside our control. Electrifying the fleet replaces that exposure with predictable, lower per-kilometre energy costs,
materially reduces ongoing maintenance, and aligns the business with the direction both regulators and consumers are
heading. As the program rolls out, we expect a steady tailwind to logistics margins regardless of where fuel prices move
next.

Why the upside is asymmetric from here

Kapruka now sits at the intersection of three powerful trends: the digitisation of Sri Lankan commerce, the global
appetite for authentic Sri Lankan products, and the rapid drop in the cost of intelligence through AI. We have a stronger
balance sheet, a returning profitability profile, a marketplace model that compounds, USD revenue diversification, a
cost base increasingly insulated from fuel and FX shocks, and a technology stack that gets cheaper to operate every
quarter.

We have rebuilt the engine. The runway ahead is long, and the operating leverage embedded in our model is only
beginning to show up in the numbers.

On behalf of the Board and management team, thank you for your continued trust. We are building Kapruka for the
next decade, and the work we have done over the past two years positions us to create meaningful, durable value for
shareholders.

Sincerely,


Dulith Herath

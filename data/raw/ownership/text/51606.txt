REVIEW OF OPERATIONS



   First Capital Treasuries PLC (the Company) recorded a Profit after Tax of Rs. 1.61Bn for the year
   ended 31 March 2026 compared to Rs. 3.04Bn in the previous year (2024/25). The Company
   reported a Loss after Tax of Rs. 29Mn for 4th quarter of 2025/26 after accounting for a fair valuation
   loss of Rs. 416Mn on its government securities portfolios.

   The Company’s Net Trading Income before Operating Expenses for the year ended 31 March 2026
   was Rs. 3.11Bn compared to Rs. 5.35Bn in the previous year.

   Following a reduction in policy rates in May 2025, yields on government securities dropped sharply
   resulting in significant trading gains in 1st quarter of 2025/26. However, the yields rose moderately
   during 2nd and 3rd quarter of 2025/26 resulting in limited trading opportunities. The yields rose
   sharply in the latter part of 4th quarter as a result of the geopolitical tensions involving the middle
   east which impacted the results for the year 2025/26.

   The Company’s investment in government securities portfolio (total) as of 31 March 2026 stood at
   Rs. 53.60Bn compared to Rs. 81.41Bn at the start of the financial year.

   The Company reported total equity amounting to Rs. 7.65Bn as of 31 March 2026 after adjusting
   for the final dividend payout of Rs. 3.08Bn for 2024/25 (Equity as of 31 March 2025: Rs. 9.12Bn).

6     Financial instruments- Fair Value Measurement

      The Company measures fair values using the following fair value hierarchy that reflects the significance of the inputs used in making the
      measurements in accordance with SLFRS 13.

      Level - 1
      Financial instruments that are measured in whole or in part by reference to published quotes in an active market. A financial instrument is
      regarded as quoted in an active market if quoted prices are readily and regularly available from an exchange and those prices represent
      actual and regularly occurring market transactions on an arm's length basis.

      Level - 2
      Financial instruments that are measured at fair value on a recurring basis. As market quotes generally are not readily available or
      accessible for these securities, their fair value measures are determined using relevant information generated by market transactions
      involving comparable securities.

      Level - 3
      Valuation techniques using significant unobservable inputs. This category includes all instruments where the valuation technique includes
      inputs not based on observable data and the unobservable inputs have a significant effect on the instrument's valuation. This category
      includes instruments that are valued based on quoted prices for similar instruments where significant unobservable adjustments or
      assumptions are required to reflect differences between the instruments.

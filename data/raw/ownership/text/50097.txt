5.    CAPITAL EXPENDITURE
     Capital expenditure incurred during the period amount to Rs. 51.74 Mn and it is reflected in Property, Plant, and Equipement.

6.    CAPITAL COMMITMENTS
     The Board of Directors had approved to purchase capital assets totaling Rs. 77.40 million of which Rs. 59.44 million has been paid as an advance. The Company had no other capital
     commitments as at 31st December 2025.

7.    SEASONALITY OF OPERATIONS
     There is no seasonality / cyclical impact on the operations of the Company which need be disclosed.

8.   RECOGNITION OF A LOSS FROM THE IMPAIRMENT OF FINANCIAL ASSETS, PROPERTY, PLANT & EQUIPMENT AND INTANGIBLE ASSETS
     There were no material losses from the impairment of financial assets, property plant and equipment and Intangible assets.

9.   FINANCIAL ASSETS AT AMORTISED COST
     Financial Assets at amortized cost represents Treasury Bonds received during 2023/24 at an effective interest rate of 23.24% in lieu of outstanding receivable from the National Water
     Supply and Drainage Board of Sri Lanka for the value of Rs.189 Mn. The Financial Asset is measured at amortized Cost as at 31st December 2025 for the value of Rs. 205 Mn, which will
     mature on 15th September 2027

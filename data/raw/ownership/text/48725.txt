   INTERIM FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
FOR THE SIX MONTHS ENDED 30 JUNE 2025
QUARTERLY REVIEW for the second quarter ended 30 June 2025

The   Group's    revenue   for the second   quarter of 2025     was   Rs. 6,941.5Mn, compared        to

Rs. 5,487.8Mn during the same quarter of 2024, representing an increase of Rs. 1,453.7Mn or 26%.

The Group's revenue for the first half of 2025 was Rs. 14,658.9Mn, compared to Rs. 10,919.4Mn

during the first half of 2024, representing an increase of Rs. 3,739.5Mn or 34%.



The Group reported aprofit of Rs. 702.4Mn for the second quarter of 2025, compared to a profit of

Rs. 747.9Mn during the same quarter of 2024, representing a decrease of Rs. 45.5Mn or 6%. The

Group reported a profit of Rs. 1,662.7Mn for the first half of 2025, compared to a profit of
Rs. 1,601.2Mn during the first half of 2024, representing a slight increase of Rs. 61.5Mn or 4%.


The Group achieved revenue growth during the quarter under review, driven mainly by increased

sales in   both the feed and chicken segments, supported by demand for compounded feed.

Moreover, demand for chicken improved during the latter part of the quarter, due to higher

market prices and limited availability of alternative protein-rich substitutes.



The Group's     profitability during the quarter was marginally impacted compared to the same

quarter previous year, mainly due to a notable decrease in profits from breeder farms.


The Company and the Group have adhered to the Sri Lanka Financial Reporting Standards in the

preparation of the interim financial statements for the period ended 30 June 2025.



There have been no other events subsequent to the interim period which require disclosure in the

interim financial statements.

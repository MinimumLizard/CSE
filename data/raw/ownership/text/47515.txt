      NOTES TO THE INTERIM FINANCIAL STATEMENTS

 1    Corporate Information
      Bimputh Financ PLC, (the Company) was a quoted public limited liability company, incorporated and domiciled in Sri Lanka. The
      Company was incorporated in Sri Lanka on 27th July 2007 under the Companies Act No 07 of 2007.At the instructions of Central
      Bank of SriLanka to exclude using the word "Finance" Board decided to change the company name as "Bimputh Lanka Investment
      PLC" from 20th May 2024.The Registrar of Company has approved the change.Two New Directors Mr.D D K Gamage and Mr.P M
      D Weerasinghe were admited to borad on 18th March 2024 while Mr.C K Gamage and Mrs.P M K Gamage Resigned as Disrectors
      on the same day.




 2    Principal Activities
      The Company was mainly engaged in the business of micro finance ,SME,Personal loans,Leasing, Gold Loan advances along with
      term and savings mobilisation activities. The Company provided finance for cultivation of cash/ other crops, micro, MSME and
      SME , self employed individuals and groups.All the activities were ceased after the Central Bank of SriLanka freezing order on
      accepting deposits from 15th October 2021 and on Lending wef 26th May 2022



 3    Date of Authorization for Issue
      The financial statements of the Company for the year ended 31st December 2024 were authorized for issue by the directors on 15th
      of February 2025.



 4    Basis of Preparation
      The interim financial statements of the Company have been presented on the basis of the same accounting policies and methods
      applied for the year ended 31st March 2022 and are in compliance with Sri Lanka Financial Reporting Standards (SLFRS/LKAS) 34 -
      Interim Financial Reporting

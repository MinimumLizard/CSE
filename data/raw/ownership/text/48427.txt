1. These Interim Financial Statements have been prepared in accordance with the requirements of Sri Lanka Accounting Standard -LKAS 34 on
'Interim Financial Reporting'.

01 January 2025. Cinnamon rate was revised 5% to 2% and Pepper rate was revised 5% to 4%. This change has been applied prospectively from
the beginning of the financial year 2025. All other accounting policies, including depreciation rates for other asset categories, remain consistent
with those applied in the audited financial statements for the year ended 31 December 2024.

which has special rights and 20,250,660 number of Non voting shares.

4. The above figures are provisional and subject to audit.

6. The Company has been adopting Sri Lanka Accounting Standards (SLRFS/LKAS) with effect from 1st January 2012 .

7. Finance Cost incorporates a total of Rs. 67,190,286 attributable to FOREX loss, while Finance Income encompasses a total of Rs.
72,262,173 from FOREX gain for the six months ending in 2025.

8. In the first and second quarter of the previous year, interim Earnings Per Share (EPS) was presented on an annualised basis. From the first
quarter of the current year, EPS is reported on a non-annualised basis. Accordingly, comparative figures have been restated. Further, the
presentation and classification of the interim financial statements for the previous year have been amended, where relevant, to enhance
presentation and ensure comparability with the current period.

Interim Financial Statements for the Period Ended 31st December 2025

Analysis of Statement of Financial Position

Total Assets

Total Assets of the Group have increased by Rs. 572 Mn from Rs. 7,814 Mn as at 31st March 2025 to Rs.8,386
Mn as at 31st December 2025. Total Non-Current Assets were Rs. 4,856 Mn and Total Current Assets were
Rs.3,530 Mn as at 31st December 2025.

Equity Attributable to Owners of the Company

Equity attributable to Owners of the Company has increased by Rs. 944 Mn from Rs. 4,038 Mn as at 31st March
2025 to Rs. 4,982 Mn as at 31st December 2025.

Net Asset Per Share (NAPS)

The NAPS has increased to Rs. 6.24 as at 31st December 2025 compared with Rs. 5.06 per share recorded as at
31st March 2025.

Analysis on Statement of Profit or Loss and Other Comprehensive Income

Revenue & Expenses

Group revenue has increased by 75% to Rs. 3,089 Mn for the quarter ended 31st December 2025 compared with
Rs. 1,764 Mn in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

Total group admin and selling expenses have decreased by 28% to Rs. 115Mn for the quarter ended 31st
December 2025 compared with Rs. 161 Mn in the corresponding quarter in the previous year.

Profit / (Loss) After Tax

The Group reported a profit amounting to Rs. 481 Mn for the quarter ended 31st December 2025 compared to
the Loss of Rs. 187 Mn reported in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

Earnings / (Loss) Per Share

Earnings per Share was Rs. 0.61 for the quarter ended 31st December 2025, compared to Rs. 0.25 loss per
share in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

1. Basis of Preparation
The Interim Financial Statements of Renuka Agri Foods PLC have been prepared in compliance with Sri Lanka
Accounting Standard LKAS 34 -Interim Financial Reporting and are subject to audit. These Interim Financial
Statements are to be read in conjunction with the annual financial statements for the year ended 31st March 2025.

The presentation and classification of figures for the corresponding period of the previous year have been
rearranged where relevant, to be comparable with those for the current period.
For management purposes, the Group is organized into business units based on their products and services, and
performance reported under two segments (i.e. Manufacturing, Plantations & Distribution) during the period
under review.


3. Stated Capital
The stated capital of the company consists of 798,829,305 (31st March 2025 – 798,829,305) ordinary voting
shares as at 31st December 2025.



4. Earnings per share
Earnings per share have been calculated by dividing profit attributable to ordinary equity holders (Excluding
Non Controlling Interest) by the number of ordinary shares as given in the LKAS 33.

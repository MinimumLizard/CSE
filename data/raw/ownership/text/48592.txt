REVIEW OF OPERATIONS



   First Capital Treasuries PLC (the Company) recorded a Profit after Tax of Rs. 1.55Bn for the quarter
   ended 30 June 2025 compared to Rs. 464Mn in the corresponding period of the previous year.

   The Company’s Net Trading Income before Operating Expenses for the 1st quarter of 2025/26 was
   Rs. 2.54Bn compared to Rs. 828Mn in the corresponding quarter of the previous year. During the
   quarter, a moderate drop in yields on government securities following a reduction in the monetary
   policy rate resulted in creating a favorable market position for the Company.

   The Company’s investment in Government Securities portfolio (total) as of 30 June 2025 stood at
   Rs. 91.3Bn compared to Rs. 81.4Bn at the start of the financial year.

   The Company reported total net assets amounting to Rs. 10.7Bn as of 30 June 2025 (31 March 2025:
   Rs. 9.1Bn).

   In April 2025, the Company issued Listed, Rated, Subordinated, Unsecured, Redeemable debentures
   of 30,000,000 at a par value of Rs. 100/- each and raised Rs. 3Bn as Tier II capital funds.

6     Financial instruments- Fair Value Measurement
      The Company measures fair values using the following fair value hierarchy that reflects the significance of the inputs used in making the
      measurements in accordance with SLFRS 13.

      Level - 1
      Financial instruments that are measured in whole or in part by reference to published quotes in an active market. A financial instrument
      is regarded as quoted in an active market if quoted prices are readily and regularly available from an exchange and those prices represent
      actual and regularly occurring market transactions on an arm's length basis.

      Level - 2
      Financial instruments that are measured at fair value on a recurring basis. As market quotes generally are not readily available or
      accessible for these securities, their fair value measures are determined using relevant information generated by market transactions
      involving comparable securities.

      Level - 3
      Valuation techniques using significant unobservable inputs. This category includes all instruments where the valuation technique includes
      inputs not based on observable data and the unobservable inputs have a significant effect on the instrument's valuation. This category
      includes instruments that are valued based on quoted prices for similar instruments where significant unobservable adjustments or
      assumptions are required to reflect differences between the instruments.

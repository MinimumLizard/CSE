Restaurants
The Restaurants sector expanded its reach to 97 KFC outlets, adding 6 new restaurants during the quarter. Sales grew 19.0% YoY,
with same-store sales increasing by 17.4%. Selective price revisions were implemented across the menu to mitigate rising cost
pressures. However, these adjustments were not sufficient to fully offset the increase in operating costs, with Management taking
a measured approach to pricing in order to preserve consumer affordability and value. Transaction growth moderated following
the price revisions, although we expect sales volumes to strengthen in the months ahead, supported by the December festive
period. Operating profit declined by 4.0%, reflecting the impact of prevailing cost pressures. Importantly, all KFC Sri Lanka outlets
continue to generate positive EBITDA.
Despite increasing competition from both local and international quick-service restaurant brands, KFC Sri Lanka continued to
strengthen its market position, supported by strong brand equity, a menu tailored to local consumer preferences, ongoing product
innovation, and a continued focus on delivering value to customers. Restoring profit growth remains a key priority for the
Restaurant sector. Our focus will be on driving higher customer volumes while maintaining disciplined management of operating
expenses. At the same time, the sector will continue to pursue its expansion strategy through selective new outlet openings and
the refurbishment of existing restaurants.

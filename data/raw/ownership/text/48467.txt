         Management Review
         Dear Shareholder,


         Watawala Plantations PLC (CSE: WATA)
         Watawala Plantations PLC posted a revenue of LKR 2,492 million at group level for the
         quarter ended 30th June 2025 (1Q FY 25/26), up 20.6% YoY due to a 27.2% YoY increase in
         revenue in the oil palm business offset by a 14.6% YoY decrease in revenue in the dairy
         business.


         Profit after tax (PAT) at group level was LKR 826 million, up 41.9% YoY. The increase in
         net profit was mainly driven by revenue growth along with margin improvements in the
         oil palm business. The net assets per share at group level decreased to LKR 3.03 as of 30 th
         June 2025 from LKR 3.51 as of 31st March 2025.


         Oil Palm
         Revenue was LKR 2,213 million, up 27.2% YoY driven by an increase in both price and
         sales volume compared to last year. Sales volumes were above the corresponding period
         last year due to a higher volume of crop processed and better oil extraction rates. Cost of
         sales decreased 1.4% YoY primarily due to the lower fertilizer cost resulting from a lower vol-
         ume of fertilizer applied offset by a higher cost for purchase of oil palm crop.


         Lonach Dairy Limited (LDL)
         Revenue was LKR 278.5 million for 1Q FY25/26, down 14.6% YoY. Cost of sales reduced
         21.1% YoY mainly due to the reduction in cost of feed, thereby converting the gross loss of
         LKR 11.5 million in the corresponding quarter last year to a gross profit of LKR 12.0 million.
         The reduction in other operating income by LKR 67.0 million resulted in the Company
         recording a net loss of LKR 25.5 million.



         Outlook
         Global palm oil prices dipped in 1Q FY25/26 compared to the prices in 4Q FY24/25. The
         Company expect a marginal recovery in global price in 2Q FY25/26. Meanwhile, the domes-
         tic palm oil price which increased in 4Q FY24/25 is expected to continue into the next quar-
         ter. The Company expect 1H FY25/26 to be challenging for the dairy sector due to a drop in
         milk quantity, despite maintaining strong milk yield.

Dear Valued Shareholders,

It is with great pride and optimism that I present the consolidated financial results of Kapruka Holdings PLC
for the quarter ended 30th June 2025—a testament to our unwavering resilience, strategic foresight, and
relentless pursuit of excellence.

In a landscape marked by economic headwinds and fierce competition, Kapruka has not just endured but
thrived, posting an impressive revenue of LKR 448.52 million, representing a dynamic 14% year-on-year
surge. Our gross profit soared by an extraordinary 30% to LKR 171.19 million, propelled by razor-sharp
operational efficiencies, meticulous cost discipline, and an optimized sales portfolio that resonates deeply
with our customers. These triumphs have catalysed a remarkable 94% enhancement in operating profits,
shrinking our operating loss to a mere LKR 2.33 million from LKR 38.62 million the previous year. This
dramatic turnaround underscores the potency of our visionary strategies and the unyielding strength of our
business foundation.


Our bold rebranding initiative has ignited transformative momentum, yielding immediate dividends in
heightened customer loyalty, penetration into untapped demographics, and soaring repeat purchase rates.
This revitalized identity elevates Kapruka as the unrivaled beacon in digital commerce, championing
unparalleled choice, premium product quality, and seamless doorstep delivery that delights at every turn.
Bolstered by these brand-driven successes, our total assets have climbed to a robust LKR 1,494 million,
affirming our steadfast stability and competitive edge.


We are witnessing explosive growth in USD-denominated revenues, powered by the trust of our global
expatriate community who turn to Kapruka for reliable delivery of essentials to their loved ones in Sri Lanka.
Capitalizing on this surge, we've unveiled our groundbreaking "video delivery" service—a first-of-its-kind
innovation that offers real-time video glimpses of deliveries in action. This immersive feature redefines
gifting, fosters unbreakable trust, and forges profound emotional bonds with our international patrons.


Meanwhile, our Kapruka Partner Central program is accelerating at full throttle, forging powerhouse alliances
with Sri Lanka's premier brands. This pivotal initiative is reshaping our ecosystem, unlocking diversified
revenue streams, seamless supplier synergies, and solidifying our dominance as the vanguard of digital
commerce.


In parallel, we've rolled out the revolutionary Kapruka Petti-Petti cargo service, complemented by a suite of
lightning-fast quick commerce solutions, all designed to deliver unmatched convenience and value.
Harnessing cutting-edge technology and customer-centric ingenuity, these advancements set us leagues apart
from the competition, reimagining how Sri Lankans connect, shop, and celebrate.

As we forge ahead, our commitment to trailblazing innovation, exponential growth, and enduring shareholder
value remains unbreakable. Together, we are not just leading the market—we are redefining it, expanding our
horizons globally and unlocking boundless potential for the future.

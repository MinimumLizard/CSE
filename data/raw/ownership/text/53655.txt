                Financial review for the nine months ended 30 September 2026


Dialog Finance PLC reported a strong financial performance for the nine months ended 30 September 2026,
recording a Net Profit After Tax (NPAT) of Rs. 179.1 million, marking a significant turnaround from a profit of
Rs. 124.5 million in the previous year. Operating income surged by 48.9% year-on-year to Rs. 2,475.7 million,
reflecting the Company’s continued focus on scalable, technology-led financial solutions.

Profit before taxes rose sharply to Rs. 314.6 million, an increase of 44.8% compared to the previous year,
driven by strong core business momentum, disciplined cost management, and improved margins. The
growth in operating income was largely underpinned by strategically structured fee income from key growth
engines such as Device Financing and the payments business, supported by steady improvements in asset
quality and net interest margins.

Total assets expanded by 20.5% to Rs. 14.4 billion compared to December 2025, while the net loan book
grew by a robust 22.6% to Rs. 11.2 billion. Net Interest Income increased by 52.8% to Rs. 1,407 million,
supported by prudent pricing strategies and effective asset-liability management, resulting in a healthy Net
Interest Margin of 13.9%.

Operating expenses grew at a moderated pace of 47.8%, resulting in a slight improvement in the cost-to-
income ratio to 69.4% from 69.9% in the previous year. This reflects the company's continued focus on
maintaining operational efficiency while supporting business growth and expansion. Impairment charges
increased to Rs. 270.4 million, in line with the expansion of the loan portfolio. Despite this, asset quality
remained sound, with gross impairment ratio being at 6.7% and a strong stage 3 impairment coverage ratio
of 66.6%.

The Company’s capital position remained strong, with both Tier I and Total Capital Adequacy ratios reported
at 14.05%, comfortably above the minimum regulatory requirements, reinforcing balance sheet resilience.

Dialog Finance PLC, a subsidiary of Dialog Axiata PLC, is a registered finance company in Sri Lanka offering
innovative, technology-driven financial services, digital lending, and payment solutions. The Company is
rated ‘AA(lka)’ by Fitch Ratings with a stable outlook.

4       Fair value of financial instruments

        The Company uses valuation techniques that are appropriate in the circumstances and for which sufficient data
        are available to measure fair value, maximizing the use of relevant observable inputs and minimizing the use of
        unobservable inputs.
        All assets and liabilities for which fair value is measured or disclosed in the financial statements are categorized
        within the fair value hierarchy, described as follows, based on the lowest level input that is significant to the fair
        value measurement as a whole:

        Level 1 - Quoted (unadjusted) market prices in active markets for identical assets or liabilities

        Level 2 - Valuation techniques for which the lowest level input that is significant to the fair value measurement is
        directly or indirectly observable

        Level 3 - Valuation techniques for which the lowest level input that is significant to the fair value measurement is
        unobservable

        For assets and liabilities that are recognized in the financial statements on a recurring basis, the Company
        determines whether transfers have occurred between levels in the hierarchy by re-assessing categorization
        (based on the lowest level input that is significant to the fair value measurement as a whole) at the end of each
        reporting period.

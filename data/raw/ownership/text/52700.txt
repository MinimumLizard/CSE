Performance Review for the Quarter Ended 30 June 2026
Lanka Realty Investments PLC commenced FY2026/27 with a stronger underlying operating performance, supported
by the enlarged commercial property platform and improved contribution from the Group's income generating
assets. Group revenue increased to LKR 320.2 Mn, gross profit improved to LKR 179.4 Mn and operating profit
increased to LKR 79.7 Mn. The quarter also reflects a materially stronger liquidity position following the Group's
ongoing capital recycling and portfolio reorganisation initiatives.

Financial Performance and Profitability Review
Group revenue increased by 9.4% to LKR 320.2 Mn, while gross profit increased by 77.5% to LKR 179.4 Mn. Gross margin
improved to 56.0% from 34.5% in the corresponding period, reflecting a stronger revenue mix and improved contribution from
the Group's commercial property and other operating activities. Operating profit increased to LKR 79.7 Mn from LKR 10.9 Mn.

Finance income increased to LKR 17.0 Mn from LKR 7.0 Mn, which contributed to a 10.5% reduction in net finance expenses to
LKR 58.7 Mn. The Group recorded profit before tax of LKR 39.9 Mn compared with a loss before tax of LKR 54.7 Mn, while profit
after tax improved to LKR 48.4 Mn from a loss of LKR 73.9 Mn.

Profit attributable to equity holders of the parent amounted to LKR 6.6 Mn, resulting in Group earnings per share of Rs. 0.03
compared with a loss per share of Rs. 0.43 in the corresponding quarter. The quarter therefore reflects a meaningful
improvement in underlying operating profitability while preserving a strong asset-backed balance sheet.

First Quarter Performance Review
For the quarter ended 30 June 2026, the Group recorded improved operating results across the key profitability
indicators. The first full-quarter contribution from the enlarged commercial property platform strengthened recurring
income, while improved gross margins and higher other operating income supported the increase in operating profit.
The Group returned to positive profitability notwithstanding a more challenging interest rate and external operating
environment.

Balance Sheet Strength, Liquidity and Capital Allocation
The Group's total assets stood at LKR 30.33 Bn as at 30 June 2026 compared with LKR 29.63 Bn as at 31 March 2026.
Cash in hand and at bank increased significantly to LKR 3.40 Bn from LKR 165.0 Mn, while current assets increased to
LKR 6.18 Bn from LKR 2.81 Bn. The stronger liquidity position provides greater flexibility to manage working capital,
optimise the Group's funding structure and reduce exposure to higher-cost financing facilities where commercially
appropriate.

Interest-bearing borrowings were approximately LKR 3.77 Bn, while the Group maintained an LTV ratio of
approximately 12.4%. Equity attributable to owners of the parent remained broadly stable at LKR 10.92 Bn,
supporting a NAV per share of approximately Rs. 51.65. The combination of stronger cash resources and a
conservative LTV profile enhances the Group's capacity for disciplined capital allocation and selective strategic
investments.

Corporate Finance and Portfolio Reorganisation - LRD
During the quarter, Lanka Realty Investments PLC completed the divestment of its direct 51% shareholding in Lanka
Realty Developments (Private) Limited to Lee Hedges PLC as part of the Group's internal property reorganisation. At
Company level, LRI PLC recognised a gain on disposal of subsidiary of approximately LKR 709.98 Mn, contributing to
standalone profit after tax of LKR 750.90 Mn and standalone EPS of Rs. 3.55 for the quarter.

As the transaction was undertaken within the LRI Group, the corresponding disposal gain is eliminated on
consolidation. The transaction nevertheless represents an important capital recycling action at LRI PLC level,
strengthening holding company liquidity and supporting future debt optimisation and investment capacity while
retaining the underlying property exposure within the Group structure.

Event After the Reporting Period - Divestment of On'ally Holdings PLC Shares
Subsequent to the reporting date, on 22 July 2026, Lanka Realty Investments PLC completed the divestment of its
entire direct shareholding of 47,325,368 ordinary shares, representing 50.886% of the issued ordinary shares of
On'ally Holdings PLC, to Lee Hedges PLC at a consideration of Rs. 47.76 per share, amounting to total consideration of
approximately LKR 2.26 Bn.

The transaction represents an intra group restructuring of the Group's commercial property holding structure. LRI PLC
no longer holds a direct interest in On'ally Holdings PLC; however, the LRI Group continues to retain control of On'ally
Holdings PLC through LRI PLC's 51.00% controlling interest in Lee Hedges PLC. As the transaction was completed after
30 June 2026, it is a non-adjusting event after the reporting period in accordance with LKAS 10 and no adjustment
has been made to the interim financial statements for the quarter ended 30 June 2026.

  Property Market, Land Bank and Future Value Creation
  The domestic property market continues to show gradual recovery characteristics, although construction activity remains
  sensitive to financing costs, imported input prices and external volatility. CBSL's Construction PMI declined to 45.7 in April 2026
  due mainly to seasonal factors and higher input costs; however, new orders continued to expand and construction firms remained
  positive on the near-term project pipeline. This environment supports a selective approach centred on well-located,
  income-generating commercial properties and disciplined development activity.

  LRI PLC's portfolio combines recurring commercial-property income with strategic land-bank and development assets. The
  Group's land holdings provide multiple value-creation options, including phased development, strategic disposals, joint ventures
  and capital recycling. Together with the enlarged commercial property platform under Lee Hedges PLC, this provides a balanced
  base for recurring earnings, asset appreciation and future development-led growth.

  Tourism and Leisure Future Growth
  Sri Lanka recorded 405,939 tourist arrivals during April-June 2026 compared with 445,768 in the corresponding period of 2025,
  reflecting near term volatility arising from regional geopolitical conditions. May 2026 nevertheless recorded year-on-year growth
  of 9.6%, and total tourist arrivals reached approximately 1.15 Mn during the first half of 2026. A normalisation of regional travel
  conditions would provide renewed upside for the tourism sector and support demand for well positioned leisure assets.

  The Group's leisure exposure provides additional medium-term growth optionality alongside its core commercial property
  platform. Management's focus remains on disciplined capital deployment, improving operating cash generation and aligning
  future leisure development with sustainable demand and return thresholds.

  Overall Financial Positioning
  The quarter ended 30 June 2026 reflects improved operating profitability, a materially stronger cash position, stable parent NAV
  and a conservative LTV profile. The Group continues to execute an active corporate finance and portfolio reorganisation strategy,
  with capital recycling intended to strengthen liquidity, optimise financing costs and preserve capacity for selective value-accretive
  opportunities. The combination of recurring commercial property income, strategic land-bank optionality and leisure exposure
  provides multiple avenues for medium- to long-term value creation.

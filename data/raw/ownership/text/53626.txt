FINANCIAL REVIEW
Income and Profitability
The Company reported consistent year-on-year top-line growth of 29% during the third quarter of 2026, while maintaining a
stable financial position and profitability. This performance was supported by effective management strategies and prudent
underwriting practices.

Gross Written Premium (GWP) reported for the nine months ended 30 September 2026 was Rs. 8,085.16 Mn, compared to
Rs. 6,252.84 Mn reported in the corresponding period of 2025, reflecting a growth of 29% for the first nine months of the
year.

Profit before tax for the nine months ended 30 September 2026 stood at Rs. 766.53 Mn, compared to Rs. 472.64 Mn
recorded in the corresponding period of 2025. Profit after tax for the period under review was Rs. 539.70 Mn, compared to
Rs. 324.64 Mn reported in the same period of the previous year. Accordingly, the Company’s overall profitability recorded a
healthy growth of 66% compared to the previous year.

The Motor segment remained the largest contributor to the Company’s Gross Written Premium (GWP), accounting for 78%
of the total portfolio and recording a strong growth of 32% compared to the previous financial year. The Fire segment also
demonstrated notable growth, increasing by 26% and contributing 10% to the overall GWP during the first nine months of
the year.

The Company’s claims cost for the nine months ended 30 September of 2026 amounted to Rs. 3,206.42 Mn, compared to Rs.
2,195.25 Mn recorded in the corresponding period of 2025. Meanwhile, operating expenses increased by 14% to Rs.
1,717.98 Mn, up from Rs. 1,512.04 Mn in 2025, in line with the Company’s business expansion strategy.

The Company’s underwriting loss reduced significantly by 46% to Rs. 189.37 Mn in 2026, compared to Rs. 349.75 Mn in
2025, driven by improved underwriting performance.
Investment and other income which comprises interest and dividend income, net fair value gains/ losses and other income,
increased by 16% to Rs. 955.90 Mn in 2026 reflecting the impact of higher investment returns.

Financial Position
Total shareholders' equity increased to Rs. 6,511.20 Mn in 2026, reflecting a 9% growth compared to Rs. 5,970.13 Mn in
2025.

Solvency
The Company maintained its Capital Adequacy Ratio well above the regulatory minimum during the period, reflecting a
strong liquidity position and its continued ability to meet policyholder obligations with confidence.
The Company is committed to driving continued sustainable growth while meeting stakeholder expectations and aligning its
activities with strategic business objectives.

   The interim financial statements of the Company for the quarter ended 30th September 2026 have been prepared in

   should be read in conjunction with the annual financial statements for the year ended 31st December 2025. Further,
   provisions of the Companies Act No. 7 of 2007 have been considered in preparing the interim condensed financial
   statements of the Company. The same accounting policies have been followed in preparation of these interim condensed
   financial statements as stated in the audited financial statements.

   In accordance with LKAS 34.19, these interim financial statements do not fully comply with Sri Lanka Accounting
   Standards (SLFRSs), as the Company has elected to apply the Statement of Alternative Treatment (SoAT) issued by the
   Institute of Chartered Accountants of Sri Lanka (CA Sri Lanka) in relation to the initial adoption of SLFRS 17 – Insurance
   Contracts. The SoAT initially permitted insurance entities to continue applying SLFRS 4 – Insurance Contracts for the first
   two interim quarters of 2026. Subsequently, this transitional relief was extended to the third interim quarter of 2026, as set
   out in the letter issued by the Accounting Standards Committee of CA Sri Lanka bearing reference No.
   TEC/ACC/2026/10/01. The extension is subject to the disclosure of the expected financial impact arising from the adoption
   of SLFRS 17 – Insurance Contracts and SLFRS 9 – Financial Instruments.

   Accordingly, these interim financial statements are prepared on the alternative basis described below.
   Application of Statement of Alternative Treatment (SoAT) – Q3 Interim Financial Reporting
   SLFRS 17 Insurance Contracts is effective for annual reporting periods beginning on or after 01st January 2026. The
   company has adopted SLFRS 9 prior to the implementation of SLFRS 17 and is currently preparing its financial statements
   in accordance with SLFRS 9 requirements. In response to implementation challenges affecting the insurance industry, CA
   Sri Lanka has permitted insurers to continue applying SLFRS 4 Insurance Contracts for the first, second and third interim
   reporting quarters of the initial year of adoption, subject to enhanced disclosures on the expected impact of SLFRS 17.


   The Company has elected to apply this relief for the quarter ended 30th September 2026.
   Accordingly, for this interim period:
   • Insurance contracts continue to be recognised, measured, and presented in accordance with SLFRS 4 Insurance Contracts.

  Measurement of Insurance Contracts
  In preparation for the initial adoption of SLFRS 17 - Insurance Contracts, the Company has assessed its insurance contract
  portfolio and identified the Premium Allocation Approach (PAA) as the expected measurement approach for insurance contracts
  that meet the relevant eligibility criteria. This approach is considered appropriate given the nature and duration of the Company's
  insurance contracts and is expected to be applied upon the initial adoption of SLFRS 17.

  Insurance Revenue
  Insurance revenue is recognized in profit or loss over the coverage period in a manner that reflects the transfer of insurance services
  to policyholders. Under the Premium Allocation Approach, revenue is generally recognized on a time-apportioned basis, unless the
  expected pattern of risk release indicates a more appropriate systematic basis.

  Liability for Remaining Coverage (LRC)
  At initial recognition, the Liability for Remaining Coverage is measured as the premium received (or receivable), adjusted for
  insurance acquisition cash flows and any amount arising from onerous contracts. Subsequently, the LRC is reduced as insurance is
  recognized over the coverage period, with adjustments made for any change in estimates where applicable.


  Liability for Incurred Claims (LIC)
  The Liability for Incurred Claims represents the present value of fulfilment cash flows relating to claims incurred as at the reporting
  date. This includes reported claims and claims incurred but not reported (IBNR), together with an explicit risk adjustment for non-
  financial risk, determined in accordance with the principles of SLFRS 17.

  Insurance Acquisition Cash Flows
  Insurance acquisition cash flows that are directly attributable to portfolios of Insurance contracts are recognized as part of the
  Liability for Remaining Coverage. Such costs are systematically allocated to the profit or loss over the coverage period in line with
  the recognition of insurance revenue.

  Onerous Contracts / Loss Component
  Where facts and circumstances indicate that a group of insurance contracts is onerous at initial recognition or subsequently, a loss
  component is established within the Liability for Remaining Coverage. The loss component is recognized immediately in Income
  Statement and is subsequently adjusted for changes in fulfilment cash flows relating to future service.


  Reinsurance Contracts Held
  Reinsurance contracts held are measured separately under the Premium Allocation Approach, where applicable. The carrying
  amounts reflect premiums paid, recoveries on incurred claims and allowance for expected credit losses. Any net gain on
  reinsurance held that relates to onerous underlying contracts is recognized in Income Statement in accordance with SLFRS 17
  requirements.

Performance review for the Nine Months ended 31 December 2025
Lanka Realty Investments PLC (ASCO.N)
Business Overview and Revenue Profile

Lanka Realty Investments PLC operates as a diversified, asset backed real estate
investment platform with a core focus on generating stable recurring income from high
quality investment properties and leisure based assets.

The Group’s revenue profile is predominantly contracted, predictable, and cash
generative, providing strong earnings visibility and resilience across economic cycles.

The investment property portfolio comprises strategically located commercial and
hospitality assets positioned within prime and high demand locations. These assets
continue to demonstrate strong and resilient occupancy levels, underpinned by
disciplined asset management, a diversified tenant base, and sustained demand for well
located commercial and lifestyle oriented spaces.

The leisure and hospitality segment delivered strong results, benefiting directly from
the continued recovery of Sri Lanka’s tourism sector. Rising tourist arrivals, improving
sector wide occupancy ratios, and strengthening destination demand translated into
higher room nights, improved average daily rates, and stronger ancillary revenues
across the Group’s leisure portfolio.

Looking ahead, management maintains a positive forward outlook supported by:

   ●   stable and predictable rental cash flows from investment properties

   ●   sustained growth in inbound tourism volumes

   ●   improving destination demand across key leisure corridors

   ●   rising occupancy levels and pricing power within hospitality assets

The leisure segment is expected to remain a key earnings growth driver, with
performance tracking above national tourism recovery trends. As tourism activity
continues to normalize and expand, the Group’s leisure assets are well positioned to
deliver improved margins, stronger operating cash flows, and enhanced returns on
invested capital.

This combination of rental income and improving leisure sector performance positions
LRI PLC with a balanced and resilient earnings profile, offering downside protection
while providing meaningful upside exposure to Sri Lanka’s broader economic and
tourism recovery..



Land Bank and Property Market Outlook

In addition to its income generating investment properties and leisure assets, LRI PLC
maintains a strategic land bank comprising well located parcels in prime and emerging
urban areas. These land holdings provide the Group with embedded optionality for
future development, value enhancement, and capital appreciation.

Sri Lanka’s property market is currently positioned at an early stage of recovery,
supported by improving macroeconomic stability, easing interest rate conditions,
strengthening foreign inflows, and renewed investor confidence. Against this backdrop,
market valuations for prime land assets are expected to trend upward over the medium
to long term, particularly within high demand commercial and urban corridors.

The Group’s land bank positions it favorably to benefit from:

   ●   future land value appreciation

   ●   selective development opportunities aligned with market demand

   ●   potential asset monetization or joint venture structures

   ●   enhanced medium term capital returns

While these land assets are not dependent on near term development for value
realization, they represent a latent source of future profitability and balance sheet
upside, strengthening the Group’s long term earnings capacity and net asset value
profile. Accordingly, the land bank complements the Group’s recurring rental income
base, reinforcing LRI PLC’s positioning as a wellcapitalized, asset backed real estate
investment platform with long term growth potential.

Profit and Loss Analysis (FY 2026 vs FY 2025 - Nine Months)
Group revenue for FY 2026 amounted to LKR 825.5 Mn, compared to LKR 861.4 Mn in
FY 2025, reflecting a moderate year on year decline of 4%. The marginal contraction in
topline revenue was primarily attributable to portfolio rationalization and the
selective exit from lower margin activities, consistent with the Group’s strategy of
prioritizing earnings quality over volume led growth.

Direct operating expenses declined materially by 23%, resulting in gross profit
increasing by 19% to LKR 456.2 Mn (FY 2025: LKR 382.4 Mn). This translated into a
significant expansion in gross margins, driven by improved asset utilization, tighter cost
discipline, and enhanced operational efficiency.

Operating expenses were tightly controlled:

   ●   administrative expenses declined by 7%

   ●   selling and distribution expenses reduced by 70%

Other operating expenses reflected a one off increase of a largely non recurring nature.

As a result, operating profit increased by 22% to LKR 96.6 Mn (FY 2025: LKR 79.5 Mn),
demonstrating improved operating leverage despite softer revenue conditions.

Finance costs declined sharply by 59%, from LKR 460.7 Mn in FY 2025 to LKR 189.6
Mn in FY 2026. This structural improvement reflects active deleveraging initiatives,
debt restructuring, and reduced exposure to interest rate volatility.

The Group reported a loss before tax of LKR 77.4 Mn, compared to LKR 363.7 Mn in
the prior year, representing a 79% reduction in losses and signaling a decisive
operational turnaround.

Earnings per share improved from (LKR 2.07) in FY 2025 to (LKR 0.87) in FY 2026,
reflecting a 58% reduction in loss per share and a clear trajectory toward breakeven
and sustainable profitability.

Balance Sheet Analysis
As at 31 December 2025 vs 31 December 2024

Total assets remained broadly stable at approximately LKR 19.25 Bn, reflecting balance
sheet preservation alongside active deleveraging. Investment properties remained
robust at approximately LKR 15.0 Bn, reinforcing the strength and scale of the Group’s
core asset backed platform.

Current Assets and Inventory Movement

Current assets increased by 29%, primarily driven by a 44% increase in inventories,
higher related party receivables associated with operational repositioning, and an
overall strengthening of working capital. Inventory balances predominantly represent
non cash consideration receivable arising from strategic property development
arrangements. During the period, balance consideration amounting to LKR 600.0 Mn
was contractually agreed to be settled through the transfer residential apartment units
within a strategically located residential development. These apartment units, with a
combined total saleable area of approximately 19,950 square feet, are located on the
upper floors of the tower within the development to be constructed on the subject
property. The development is being undertaken by Prime Lands Residencies PLC, in
collaboration with Baseline Holdings (Private) Limited, under the Prime Baseline
project. The relevant Sale and Purchase Agreements have been duly executed between
the respective parties, securing the Group’s contractual entitlement to the residential
units.

Accordingly, the value attributable to these apartment units has been recognised as
inventory, reflecting the Group’s right to receive completed residential units which are
expected to be either monetised through sale or strategically retained, depending on
prevailing market conditions at completion. This asset backed settlement mechanism
preserves value, enhances balance sheet optionality, and mitigates near term cash
outflows while maintaining exposure to future property market upside.

Capital Structure and Solvency

Total equity increased by LKR 642.5 Mn (+6%) to LKR 10.56 Bn, supported by an
expansion in revaluation reserves. This increase was achieved despite lower retained
earnings resulting from restructuring initiatives, underscoring the Group’s improved
solvency and capital resilience. Furthermore, the strengthening of the equity base

reflects strong investor confidence in the Group’s future growth prospects, with
investors committing additional equity funding into the Group.

Total interest bearing borrowings declined by 19%, amounting to a reduction of
approximately LKR 884 Mn. Consequently, the Group’s Loan to Value (LTV) ratio
improved from approximately 23.7% in 2024 to 19.0% in 2025, representing a reduction
of approximately 470 basis points.

An LTV below 20% reflects a highly conservative and resilient capital structure,
reducing financial risk and enhancing refinancing flexibility.

Net Asset Value (NAV) per share increased from LKR 38.52 to LKR 41.06, representing
a 6.6% increase and demonstrating tangible value accretion to shareholders.

Events After the Reporting Period – Strategic Acquisition

On 08 January 2026, Lanka Realty Investments PLC acquired a 51.0% controlling
interest in Lee Hedges PLC through the purchase of 13,057,595 ordinary shares at Rs.
216.00 per share, for a total consideration of Rs. 2,820.44 Mn. In addition, Eighth
Wonder, the single largest shareholder of LRI PLC, acquired 5,148,684 shares (20.10%) at
the same price, resulting in an effective controlling interest of 71.10%.

Lee Hedges PLC owns two prime commercial properties located in high demand
Colombo commercial zones, generating stable and attractive rental yields supported by
established tenant profiles and long term income visibility.

As at 30 September 2025, Lee Hedges PLC reported cash and cash equivalents of
approximately Rs. 1.7 Bn, which is expected to materially strengthen Group level
liquidity. The acquisition price represents a discount to the reported NAV of Rs. 242.66
per share, indicating a value accretive entry multiple.

The transaction is expected to be positively accretive at Group level, strengthening
asset quality, expanding the recurring rental income base, enhancing balance sheet
liquidity, and supporting long term shareholder value creation. The financial impact
will be recognized in the subsequent reporting period.

LRI PLC (ASCO.N) has transitioned into a post restructuring consolidation and
growth phase, characterized by:

   ●   disciplined, value accretive strategic expansion

   ●   improved earnings quality and operating leverage

   ●   materially reduced leverage and finance costs

   ●   rising NAV per share

   ●   strong exposure to Sri Lanka’s tourism recovery

   ●   embedded upside from a strategic land bank

The Group enters 2026 with a cleaner balance sheet, resilient recurring income base,

sustainable long term shareholder value creation.

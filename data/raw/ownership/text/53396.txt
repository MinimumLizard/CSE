NOTES TO THE FINANCIAL STATEMENTS

(1) Corporate Information

     On’ally Holdings PLC is a public limited liability Company which was listed on the Colombo Stock Exchange on 25th April 1991 and re-registered as
     per the Companies Act, No 07 of 2007 on 13th December 2007 under the new Number PQ 107. The Registered office of the company is situated at 4th
     Floor, Unity Plaza Building, Colombo 04.
(2) Basis of Preperation and Accounting Policies

     The interim financial statements of the Company have been prepared in accordance with Sri Lanka Accounting Standard - LKAS 34 – Interim
     Financial Reporting. This interim financial statement should be read in conjunction with the Annual Financial Statements for the year ended 31 March
     2026 including the changes to accounting policies as a result of adapting Sri Lanka Financial Reporting Standards, (SLFRS/LKAS).

     The Company has followed the accounting policies and methods of computation as disclosed in the Annual Financial Statements for the year ended
     31 March 2026 in the preparation of the interim accounts for the quarter.

(3) Disclosures

(3.1) The figures are provisional and subject to audit.
(3.2) There have been no material events since the Balance Sheet date which require separate disclosure in the provisional financial statements.

(3.3) Provisions have been made for all possible expenses as at 30 September 2026.
(3.4) Renting of investment property is considered as one operating segment and accordingly this interim financial report do not provide information by
      segment.
(3.5) There are no significant changes in the nature of the contingent liabilities disclosed in the Audited Financial Statements for the year ended 31st March
      2026.
(3.6) The Directors approved the final dividend of Rs. 1.40 per share for the financial year ended 31st March 2026, which was duly paid on 11th September
      2026.

Chairman & Chief Executive Officer’s Review

Dear Shareholders,

I am pleased to share Kapruka Holdings PLC's results for the quarter ended 30 June 2026. This is our second
consecutive profitable quarter. Last quarter marked our return to profitability, and this quarter shows that the
improvement is holding. We are building a more profitable, capital-efficient and scalable business designed to create
sustainable long-term value for our shareholders

A Stronger, More Profitable Kapruka
Group revenue grew 20% year-on-year to LKR 537.90 million, and gross profit increased 41% to LKR 220.65
million. Gross margin improved to around 41% of revenue, up from roughly 35% a year earlier. Gross profit
growing twice as fast as revenue reflects the improving quality of our sales mix and the shift toward asset-light
revenue.

Operating profit was LKR 5.07 million, compared with an operating loss of LKR 2.3 million in the same quarter last
year. Profit Before Tax rose to LKR 24.90 million, against a loss of LKR 1.61 million in the previous year. Profit
Before Tax also includes other income of approximately LKR 19.8 million, primarily exchange gains and interest
income arising from our growing USD revenue base and stronger cash position.

At the Company level, Kapruka Holdings PLC recorded revenue of LKR 52.53 million and Profit Before Tax of
LKR 2.94 million.

These results follow two years of deliberate restructuring: tighter cost discipline, better margins, a larger share of
technology-enabled revenue, and investment in platforms that can grow without a matching increase in capital or
operating costs. Our objective is simple. We want growth that produces greater returns from the resources we have
already invested, rather than growth that constantly demands more resources.

Three Growth Engines
Kapruka Partner Central, our marketplace, added more active partners during the quarter each new seller uses our
customer base, logistics and payments infrastructure, so revenue grows without adding inventory risk. Partner Central
was a meaningful contributor to this quarter's margin improvement.

Kapruka Services Platform continues to take Kapruka beyond physical goods into bookable services. This opens a
new market on top of the same customer network, with high-margin revenue built on the trust we have earned over
two decades.

Kapruka Cross Border recorded a strong increase in international order volumes this quarter. We act as the e-
distributor for Sri Lankan brands on Amazon US, Amazon UK, Amazon Canada and other global marketplaces. Dollar
revenue carries strategic value well beyond its rupee contribution, and this segment remains both a hedge and a long-
term growth driver.

AI Across the Business

AI is now in daily use across Kapruka. This quarter, our AI customer-care agents on WhatsApp began converting
enquiries into orders at rates well above our human-assisted baseline, and they work around the clock at very
little additional cost. AI automation now supports customer service, product discovery, content generation, supply
chain workflows and back-office processing. We remain on track to reach 40% back-office automation in the coming
quarters.

In practical terms, AI lets us grow revenue without growing headcount and fixed costs at the same pace. That is what
turns a profitable quarter into a structurally more profitable business.

Electric Fleet Progress
Our fleet conversion has moved from plan to execution. Electric vans now make up 30% of the Kapruka delivery fleet,
and they are already producing a clear downward trend in per-delivery energy and maintenance costs compared with
our petrol vans.This initiative supports our ESG objectives, particularly by reducing our environmental footprint and
improving the sustainability of our last-mile delivery operations. Fuel has always been one of the most volatile costs in
last-mile delivery, moving with global oil prices and exchange rates outside our control. Every portion of the fleet we
electrify replaces that exposure with predictable, lower per-kilometre costs. This combination of environmental
benefits, cost efficiency and greater resilience will continue to strengthen our logistics margins while supporting
Kapruka’s broader ESG agenda.


Looking Ahead
Kapruka benefits from three trends at once: the digitisation of Sri Lankan commerce, global demand for authentic Sri
Lankan products, and the falling cost of AI. We now have two consecutive profitable quarters, expanding gross margins,
a marketplace model that scales, growing USD revenue, a cost base less exposed to fuel and currency shocks, and a
technology platform that gets cheaper to run every quarter.

The foundation is rebuilt, and the benefits of this model are only beginning to show in the numbers. On behalf of the
Board and the management team, thank you for your continued trust. We are building Kapruka for the next decade, and
the work of the past two years positions us to create meaningful, durable value for shareholders.

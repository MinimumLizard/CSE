6.   Capital Work-in-Progress of Rs. 857,095,108/- represents expenditure incurred on the construction of
     two 5MW Ground-Mounted Solar PV Projects, Dolahena I & II, located in Matara. The balance under
     Capital Work-in-Progress will be transferred to the appropriate asset categories once the projects are
     ready for electricity generation and supply to the National Grid.

     Subsequent to the reporting date, an additional amount of Rs. 263,960,756/- has been capitalized under
     Capital Work-in-Progress. Furthermore, the Company has an estimated remaining capital expenditure
     commitment of approximately Rs. 615.3 million for the completion of the ongoing construction work
     related to these projects.


7.   Term Loan Facility – Dolahena I & II Ground-Mounted Solar PV Projects

     The Company entered into a term loan facility agreement with Commercial Bank of Ceylon PLC on 5th
     February 2025 for a total amount of Rs. 1,372,000,000/-, with a repayment period of 84 months, after a
     one-year grace period.


     The interest rate applicable is as follows:

        11% per annum (fixed) for the first five years
        AWPLR + 0.5% per annum for the remaining three years

     During the reporting period, a sum of Rs. 418,121,242/- was disbursed under this facility to finance the
     construction of the Dolahena I & II Ground-Mounted Solar PV Projects. The loan is secured by:


        A floating primary mortgage of Rs. 472,000,000/- over the project land (immovable assets)
        A floating primary mortgage of Rs. 900,000,000/- over the project’s movable assets

     Subsequent to the reporting date, an additional Rs. 275,310,930/- was disbursed under the same facility.

     The first capital repayment, amounting to Rs. 16,334,000/-, is scheduled to commence on 10th July
     2026.

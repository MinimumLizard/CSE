Performance Review of Kotagala Plantations PLC (KPPLC)- Financial Year
2026/2027

KPPLC has recorded a loss of Rs.91.5 Mn before income tax for the second quarter in
financial year of 2026/27 which is a decrease of Rs.265 Mn when compared with the
corresponding period of the last year. This decline is due to the reduction in gross profit
and sundry income, however the total reduction of Rs.315 Mn in gross profit and sundry
income offset by the reduction in administration cost and finance cost.

The reduction in gross profit is due to the decrease in quantity sold as a result of the
geopolitical impact. Tea and Rubber prices increased, together with the increase in
wages as a result of the government wage increase of 15%.

However, the overall gross profit reported a decrease mainly due to the decrease in
quantity sold. Rubber performance compared to last year reported an increase in loss.
Oil Palm too reflected a decrease in profitability mainly due to the reduction in quantity
sold and wage increase.

The Group recorded a loss of Rs.93.2 Mn before income tax, which includes results of
Rubber & Allied Products (Colombo) Limited a wholly owned subsidiary.

The tea sector contributed a gross profit of Rs.61 Mn, compared to Rs.241 Mn. recorded
in the previous year. The gross loss in the rubber sector has increased to Rs.97.8 Mn from
Rs.46.3 Mn in last year. The gross profit of the oil palm segment too decreased to Rs.193.8
Mn, from Rs.210.8 Mn last year. The overall gross profit stood at Rs.157.1 Mn, reflecting a
decrease of 61% compared to last year.

The net assets per share has decreased to Rs.7.86 from Rs.8.23 recorded as of
31st March 2026.

Future Outlook

The company is focused in maintaining a positive bottom-line performance despite the
challenges faced by the industry.

To mitigate the recent increase in wages and the dearth of workers, the company has already
commenced mechanization including harvesting and field operations along with automation
in the processing Centers.

KPPLC is also committed to the development of Human Capital in terms of training &
development, providing opportunities to enhance worker earnings including compliance
with Social Capital together with Regulatory compliances & Corporate Governance.

Interim Financial Statements for the Period Ended 30th June 2025

Analysis of Statement of Financial Position

Total Assets

Total Assets of the Group have increased by Rs. 675 Mn from Rs. 7,566 Mn as at 31st March 2025 to Rs.8,240
Mn as at 30th June 2025. Total Non-Current Assets were Rs. 4,432 Mn and Total Current Assets were Rs.3,809
Mn as at 30th June 2025.

Equity Attributable to Owners of the Company

Equity attributable to Owners of the Company has decreased by Rs. 9 Mn from Rs. 3,752 Mn as at 31st March
2025 to Rs. 3,743 Mn as at 30th June 2025.

Net Asset Per Share (NAPS)

The NAPS has decreased to Rs. 4.69 as at 30th June 2025 compared with Rs. 4.70 per share recorded as at 31st
March 2025.

Analysis on Statement of Profit or Loss and Other Comprehensive Income

Revenue & Expenses

Group revenue has increased by 41% to Rs. 2,024 Mn for the quarter ended 30th June 2025 compared with Rs.
1,440 Mn in the corresponding quarter of the previous year. The composition of the Group Revenue for the
quarter was 88% from exports and 12% from local sales.

Total group admin and selling expenses have decreased by 24% to Rs. 99 Mn for the quarter ended 30th June
2025 compared with Rs. 130 Mn in the corresponding quarter in the previous year.

Profit / (Loss) After Tax

The Group reported a loss amounting to Rs. 13 Mn for the quarter ended 30th June 2025 compared to the Loss
of Rs. 32 Mn reported in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

Earnings / (Loss) Per Share

Loss per Share was Rs. 0.02 for the quarter ended 30th June 2025, compared to Rs. 0.04 in the corresponding
quarter of the previous year.

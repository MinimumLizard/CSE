Performance Review of Kotagala Plantations PLC (KPPLC)- Financial Year
2026/2027

KPPLC has recorded a loss of Rs.47.9 Mn. for the first quarter in financial year of 2026/27
which is a decrease of Rs.100 Mn when compared with the corresponding period of the
last year. This decline is partly due to the reduction in gross profit and sundry income
which was partially offset by the reduction finance cost. The reduction in gross profit is
due to the decrease in quantity sold with a corresponding increase in stock holding as a
result of the geopolitical impact. Tea prices increased, together with the increase in
wages as a result of the government wage increase of 15%, resulting in net increase in
profitability per Kg. However the overall gross profit reported a decrease mainly due to
the decrease in quantity sold. Rubber performance compared to last year reported a
marginal decrease in loss. Oil Palm too reflected a significant decrease in profitability
mainly due to the reduction in quantity sold and wage increase.

The Group recorded a loss of Rs.49.3 Mn, which includes results of Rubber & Allied
Products (Colombo) Limited a wholly owned subsidiary.

The tea sector contributed a gross profit of Rs.105 Mn, compared to Rs.127 Mn. recorded
in the previous year. The gross loss in the rubber sector has slightly decreased to Rs.76.6
Mn from Rs.77.6 Mn in last year. However, the gross profit of the oil palm segment has
decreased to Rs.58 Mn, from Rs.85 Mn last year, due to reduction in crop by 12%. The
overall gross profit stood at Rs.86.6 Mn, reflecting a decrease of 35% compared to last
year.

The net assets per share has decreased to Rs.8.12 from Rs.8.23 recorded as of 31st March
2026.

Future Outlook

The company is focused in maintaining a positive bottom-line performance despite the
challenges faced by the industry.

To mitigate the recent increase in wages and the dearth of workers, the company has already
commenced mechanization including harvesting and field operations along with automation
in the processing Centers.

KPPLC is also committed to the development of Human Capital in terms of training &
development, providing opportunities to enhance worker earnings including compliance
with Social Capital together with Regulatory compliances & Corporate Governance.

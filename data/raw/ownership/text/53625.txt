Performance overview
Senkadagala Finance PLC recorded continued growth in its core lending operations during the six months ended 30 September 2026, supported by
expansion in its leasing, hire purchase, margin trading and gold-backed lending portfolios. Interest income increased by 17% to Rs. 5,275 Mn, compared
to Rs. 4,507 Mn in the corresponding period of the previous year. However, interest expenses rose by 48% to Rs. 2,096 Mn, reflecting higher funding
costs and increased funding requirements associated with portfolio growth. Consequently, net interest income increased by a modest 3% to Rs. 3,179
Mn. The Company maintained a healthy Net Interest Margin (NIM) of 12.09%, well above the reported industry average of 9.72%, although lower than
the 15.85% recorded in the corresponding period of the previous year.
The Company recorded a net profit of Rs. 810 Mn, compared to Rs. 981 Mn in the corresponding period of the previous year, representing a decline of
17.4%. Profitability was primarily impacted by a reduction of approximately Rs. 380 Mn in fair value gains on financial assets measured at Fair Value
Through Profit or Loss (FVTPL), reflecting less favourable market conditions, together with increased operating expenses arising from business
expansion. Trading gains also declined by 19%, while other operating income increased by 139% to Rs. 303 Mn, mainly due to higher gains on the
disposal of property, plant and equipment.
Operating expenses increased by Rs. 678 Mn (48.2%) compared to the corresponding period of the previous year, reflecting the expansion of the
Company's operations, including growth in staff numbers and the branch network from 125 to 150 branches. Personnel expenses increased by 66%, while
depreciation and amortisation rose by 38% and other operating expenses by 25%. Together with the reduction in investment-related income, these
increases contributed to a rise in the cost-to-income ratio from 37.69% to 57.38%. While the expanded branch network has increased the Company's
operating cost base in the near term, it is expected to strengthen market reach and support future business growth as the new branches progressively
mature.
Meanwhile, asset quality continued to improve in terms of non-performing loan ratios, with the gross Stage 3 loan ratio declining to 5.14% from 6.58% a
year earlier, remaining below the reported industry average of 6.29%. The improvement in credit risk indicators, including lower Probability of Default
(PD) estimates following quarterly recalibration, contributed to a net impairment reversal of Rs. 191 Mn, compared to an impairment charge of Rs. 339
Mn in the corresponding period of the previous year. However, both Stage 3 and total impairment coverage ratios declined during the period.
The Company's balance sheet continued to expand, with total assets increasing to Rs. 62.77 Bn as at 30 September 2026, from Rs. 57.07 Bn as at 31
March 2026. Finance leases and hire purchases increased to Rs. 39.64 Bn from Rs. 34.63 Bn over the same period, reflecting sustained demand for the
Company's principal lending products. The Company maintained its capital adequacy and liquidity ratios well above the applicable regulatory minimum
requirements.
Overall, the Company's performance during the first half of the financial year reflected continued growth in lending and an improvement in Stage 3 loan
ratios, against a backdrop of higher funding and operating costs and lower investment-related income. While these factors weighed on profitability, the
Company's expanded distribution network, capital position and liquidity provide a foundation for pursuing further growth, with continued emphasis on
funding cost management, operating efficiency and prudent credit risk management.

   6     Notes to the interim financial statements
  6.1    Condensed Interim financial statements
         The Statement of Financial Position as at 30th September 2025, Statement of Income, Statement of Comprehensive Income, Statement
         of Cash Flows and Statement of Changes in Equity of the Company for the period ended there of are drawn up from Un-audited financial
         statements of the Company and provide information as required by the Colombo Stock Exchange and LKAS 34. Further, provisions of the
         Companies Act No.7 of 2007 has been considered in preparing the said financial statements of the Company.


         The presentation and classification of the financial statements of the previous period have been amended, where relevant, for better
         presentation and to be comparable with those of the current period.
         The interim condensed financial statements are presented in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR) and all values are rounded to the nearest
         thousand except when otherwise indicated.

  6.2    Basis of preparation
         The same Accounting Policies and methods of computation as mentioned in the audited financial statements for the period ended 31
         December 2024 have been adopted in the preparation of these condensed consolidated financial statements.

         The Company has adopted Amendments to SLFRS 4 in the preparation of financial statements. Application of SLFRS 9 is not considered,
         considering the temporary exemption permitted under Para 20B of SLFRS 4 as the Company's activities are predominantly connected
         with insurance.


  6.3    Other income / (expenses) includes foreign exchange gains / (losses) and rent income.

  6.4    All expenses related to management expenditure is fully provided for in the financial statements.

  6.5    Contingencies, capital and other commitments
         The Company has obtained letters of guarantees to the value of Rs. 28,332,348.49 from Nations Trust Bank PLC as at 30 September
         2025 for the purpose of acquiring insurance businesses. There are no significant changes in the nature of the contingent liabilities that
         were disclosed in the Financial Statements for the year ended 31 December 2024.

  6.6    Event after the reporting period
         No circumstances have arisen since the reporting date, which would require adjustments to or disclosure in the financial statements.

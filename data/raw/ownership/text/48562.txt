Explanatory Notes

1.   Basis of Preparation
     The result for the period ended 30th June 2025 and the Statement of Financial Position as at 30th June 2025 are provisional and subject to audit.
     The Condensed Interim Financial Statements of the Group and the Company have been prepared in accordance with Sri Lanka Accounting Standard LKAS 34 - Interim Financial Reporting.
     These Condensed Interim Financial Statements should be read in conjunction with the Annual Financial Statements for the year ended 31st March 2025.


2.   Fair value measurement and related fair value disclosures
     The fair values of all the financial assets and financial liabilities recognised during the period were not materially different from the transaction prices at the date of initial recognition. There
     were no transfers between Level 1 and Level 2 and no transfers into or out of Level 3 categories as per the fair value hierarchy, during the period. The fair value changes on financial
     instruments in Level 3 category was properly recorded in the statement of other comprehensive income.

     Fair valuation was done as of 31st March 2025 for all unquoted equity shares classified as Level 3 within the fair value hierarchy according to fair valuation methodology, fair value would not
     significantly vary if one or more than inputs were changed.
3.   VAT exemption claimed by the Companies in the Group on the localy produced dairy products previously is now liable for VAT at the rate of 18% with effect from 01st January 2024, due to the
     amendment made to the Value Added Tax Act, No. 14 of 2002 by the Value Added Tax (Amendment) Act, No. 32 of 2023. Hence, the net turnover of the group is stated after deducting Rs. 456
     Mn as VAT (18%) for the quarter ended 30th June 2025.
     However, Value Added Tax (Amendment ) Act No 4 of 2025, has been certified on 11th April 2025 and changes from the amendment Act to the Value Added Tax Act, No 14 of 2002, the locally




                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     Condensed Interim Financial Statements 30th June 2025
     produced liquid Milk and Yoghurt are exempt from Value Added Tax (VAT) effective from 11th April 2025.

4.   The Company continues to evaluate options to complete the actions taken to date as per the disclosed intention to de-list and merge the operations of the Company with Cargills Dairies (Pvt)
     Ltd, a wholly owned subsidiary in the Cargills Group of Companies to create a unified entity focusing on carrying out the dairy operations of the Group. However, consequent to the Company
     being non-compliant with Rule 7.13.1 (a) as of 01st July 2018, the Securities of the Company has been transferred to Watch List on 01st July 2018, Subsequently, with the introduction of the
     Second Board under Section 7.13.2(I)(c) , the Company's securities were transferred to the Second Board.

5.   Contingent liabilities




                                                                                                                                                                                                              Kotmale Holdings PLC
     The Company does not have significant contingencies as at the reporting date.
6.   Events after the reporting period
     No events have occurred since the reporting date which would require any adjustment to, or disclosure in, the financial statements.
7.   Comparative figures
     Where appropriate, the previous year's figures have been reclassified to confirm to the current classification. All per share details have been calculated, for all periods, based on the number of
     shares in issue as at 31st March 2025.

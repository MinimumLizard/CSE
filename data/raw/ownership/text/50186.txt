      Related Party Transactions


      The change in the principal activity of the company from printing to commercial real estate was approved at the Extraordinary General Meeting (EGM) on 4th
      August 2025. Accordingly, the company commenced commercial operations on the 1st of October 2025 generating revenue. The company entered into two
 5    separate property management agreements with its related entities VKM Services (Pvt) Ltd and Kerner Haus 1705 (Pvt) Ltd to manage the properties situated
      in Nawam Mawatha and Mount Lavinia respectively for the quarter ended 31st December 2025. The curent period revenue is property management fee
      where-as the comparative revenue is from printing operations.

      At the EGM held on 4th August 2025 it was approved to borrows a sum of Rupees Ten Million (Rs.10,000,000/-) at an annual interest rate of 11% with an
      initial disbursement of Rs.5,000,000 and the balance on requirement from the parent, Ekta Global Pte. Ltd in order to support the Company’s ongoing
 6
      operational requirements and towards the settlement of trade payables and accrued expenses. Accordingly the initial tranche of Rs. 5,000,000 loan was
      received on the 22nd of October 2025.

      Kerner 1 (Pvt) Ltd was incorporated with Kerner Haus Global Solutions PLC and a related entity holding 49% and 51% of the shares respectively. By a
 7
      management agreement Kerner Haus Global Solutions PLC will be managing the company. Operations are not commenced yet.

 8    Events after the reporting period

      The Board ofDirectors has approved the execution of the third and fourth Property Management Agreements with its related entities Kerner Haus 2203 (Pvt)
      Ltd and RKS 17 Holdings (Pvt) Ltd to manage the properties situated in Kew Road and Katukale respectively.

      There are no other material events, which have occurred subsequent to the period where adjustments are necessary.

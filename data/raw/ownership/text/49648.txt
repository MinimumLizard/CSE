  6.8   Reclassification of the investment in Hatton National Bank PLC (HNB)
        For the year ended 31st March 2025, the Group’s investment in HNB was classified as a non-current asset held for sale in accordance with SLFRS 5. During the period, the Group disposed
        9.99% of HNB’s voting shares and 44% of its non-voting shares. As a result of this disposal, the Group ceased to have significant influence over HNB. Accordingly, the remaining investment,
        consisting solely of non-voting shares, has been reclassified as a financial asset measured at fair value through profit or loss (FVTPL) in accordance with SLFRS 9.


  6.9   Sale and Purchase agreement for Browns Ari Resorts (Pvt) Ltd
        Browns Ari Resorts (Pvt) Ltd, a subsidiary of the Group, has entered into a Sale and Purchase Agreement with ASB Hotel Properties Maldives (Private) Limited for the sale of the assets and
        business of Barceló Whale Lagoon Maldives Resort for a total consideration of USD 57,500,000.
        Completion of the transaction is subject to the satisfaction (or waiver, where applicable) of certain conditions precedent set out in the Sale and Purchase Agreement, including the receipt of
        required regulatory and third-party approvals in the Maldives.

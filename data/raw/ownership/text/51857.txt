QUARTER REVIEW

Blue Diamonds Jewellery Worldwide PLC (BDJW) has released its unaudited financial results for the quarter
ended 31 March 2026.

The quarter ended 31 March 2026 demonstrated gradual improvement in business activity despite continued
market challenges. Revenue increased compared to the corresponding period of the previous year, supported by
improved customer engagement and operational initiatives.

Financial Performance
Revenue for the quarter increased to Rs. 5.15 million from Rs. 4.85 million recorded in the corresponding quarter
of the previous year. Gross profit improved significantly, reflecting enhanced cost control measures and better
inventory management.

Although operating losses remained at a significant level, management continued to focus on reducing overhead
costs and improving operational efficiency. The loss after tax for the quarter was Rs. 30.4 million.

Operational Review
During the quarter, the Company continued to strengthen internal processes, optimize resource utilization, and
review business strategies aimed at improving profitability. Special attention was given to cost management,
working capital optimization, and customer retention initiatives.

Outlook

Management remains cautiously optimistic about future performance. The Company will continue to explore new
business opportunities, strengthen revenue streams, and implement measures to improve financial stability.
Ongoing efforts are expected to support sustainable growth and enhance shareholder value over the long term.

Key Focus Areas for the Next Quarter
• Revenue growth through expanded business opportunities
• Further reduction of operating costs
• Improved cash flow management
• Strengthening customer relationships and market presence
• Enhancing overall operational efficiency

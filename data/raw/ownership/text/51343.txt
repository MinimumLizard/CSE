NOTES TO THE INTERIM FINANCIAL STATEMENTS FOR THE TWELVE MONTHS ENDED 31ST MARCH 2026


1. Principal Activities and Nature of Operations
      Jetwing Symphony PLC is the holding company that owns, directly and indirectly, investments in a number of companies
      that are involved in the business of hoteliering.


2.    Approval of Financial Statements

      The interim financial statements of the Group and the Company for the twelve months ended 31st March 2026, were authorized
      for issue by the Board of Directors on 26th May 2026.

3. Basis of Preparation

      The interim financial statements have been prepared in accordance with the Sri Lanka Accounting Standard (LKAS) 34- Interim
      financial reporting.

4. Changes in Accounting Policies

      These interim financial statements have been prepared in accordance with the accounting policies set out in the Annual
      Report for the year ended 31st March 2025.

5.    Fair value measurement and related fair value disclosures

      The fair values of all the financial assets and financial liabilities recognized during the quarter were not materially different
      from the transaction prices at the date of initial recognition. There were no transfers between Level 1 and Level 2 and no
      transfers into or out of Level 3 categories as per the fair value hierarchy, during the period. The fair value changes on financial
      instruments in Level 3 category was properly recorded in the statement of other comprehensive income and there were no
      purchases and/or disposals during the period.

6. Contingent Liabilities

      There are no significant contingent liabilities as at 31st March 2026, other than the following:

      Local authorities have claimed a levy amounting to 1% of the turnover for the issuance of trade license. The Group does not
      agree with such a claim. Therefore, 50% of the 1% of turnover have been provided in the financial statements and balance
      50% is disclosed as a contingent liability. The estimated contingent liability as of 31st March 2026 is Rs. 81 Mn. At the moment,
      four cases at Tissamaharama, four cases are pending at Magistrate’s courts in Kandy, two cases at Colombo & one case at
      Dambulla. Other than that, two cases are pending at Provincial High Courts in Kandy.


7. Information for the twelve months ended 31st March 2025 has been reclassified to conform with the current presentation where
   applicable.

8. There are no distinguishable components to be identified as segments for the Company.

9. Events after the reporting period

     No circumstances have arisen since the reporting date, which would require adjustments to or disclosure in the Financial
     Statements other the following –
     - The Board of Directors has recommended a final dividend of Rs. 0.50 per share (2025 - Nil) amounting to Rs. 301 Mn
          (2025 - Nil) which is payable on 20th July 2026 if approved by the Shareholders at the Annual General Meeting.

10. Five months of the period under review relates to the off-season of the tourism industry in Sri Lanka. During the period
   under review 2,380,879 tourists visited the country.

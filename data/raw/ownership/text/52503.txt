Performance overview
The Company recorded a net profit of Rs. 472.6 Mn for the three months ended 30 June 2026, representing a
marginal increase over the corresponding period of the previous year. Interest income increased by 16% year-on-year
to Rs. 2,545.9 Mn, driven by growth in the leasing, hire purchase, margin trading and gold loan portfolios. Higher
funding costs resulted in interest expense increasing by 45% to Rs. 989.3 Mn, limiting the growth in net interest
income to 3%, which amounted to Rs. 1,556.6 Mn. Other operating income rose by 26% to Rs. 423.6 Mn, primarily
due to higher gains on the disposal of property, plant and equipment.
While asset quality continued to improve during the period, impairment charges and other credit losses increased by
51% compared to the corresponding period of the previous year. This increase was mainly attributable to the
expansion of the Company's loan portfolio from Rs. 31.5 Bn to Rs. 44.2 Bn year-on-year, together with the
maintenance of prudent provision coverage levels under the expected credit loss framework.
In line with the growth in the loan portfolio, external borrowings, excluding deposits, also increased to support the
continued expansion of the Company's asset base. The expansion of business operations, including the growth of the
branch network, resulted in higher personnel expenses, depreciation, amortisation and other operating expenses
during the period.
Credit quality indicators strengthened further during the period, with the gross Stage 3 loan ratio declining to 5.25%
from 7.61% a year earlier. The Company also maintained liquidity and capital adequacy ratios above the regulatory
minimum requirements throughout the period. These results reflect the continued expansion of the Company's core
lending operations while maintaining prudent risk management and regulatory capital and liquidity buffers.

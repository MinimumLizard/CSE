Explanatory Notes

  1    Financial Statements of the Company have been prepared in accordance with the Sri Lanka Accounting Standards (SLFRSs/LKASs) issued by The Institute of
       Chartered Accountants of Sri Lanka. There were no changes to the Accounting Policies and methods of computation since the publication of the Annual Report for the
       year ended December 31, 2025.

  2    These Interim Financial Statements have been prepared in compliance with the requirements of the Sri Lanka Accounting Standard - LKAS 34 on ''Interim Financial
       Reporting'' and provide the information as required in terms of Rule 7.4 of the Colombo Stock Exchange.

  3    During the 3rd quarter, the Company purchased 19 motor vehicles, including 14 electric vehicles, as replacement and modernization of its vehicle fleet. Further,
       during the period under review, the company disposal of seven vehicles under the vehicle fleet replacement programme and it resulted in a gain of Rs. 43.7 Mn.,
       which significantly contributed to the increase in other income for the period.

  4     The increase in the cost of sales for the period ended September 30, 2026, was primarily driven by maintenance project expenditures. These included progress
       payments of Rs. 35.1 million o/a the Indoor Air Quality Improvement Project and Rs. 10.5 million for replacing and modifying the sanitary waste and vent riser system
       within the Commercial House Building’s service shaft. Costs were further impacted by the full-period operating expenses of CCTV operations commenced in August
       2025 (Rs. 28.2 million), higher depreciation resulting from the expansion of the vehicle hiring fleet (Rs. 27.8 million), and an increase in outsourced staff costs
       following salary revisions (Rs. 18.6 million). Additionally, a Rs. 14.7 million provision for repair and waterproofing works on the Commercial House Building, alongside
       increased municipal rates and broader inflationary pressures, contributed to the YOY increase.

  5    Related Party Transactions
       ( a ) Mr. M.D.C.P. Dharmasena, a Non-Executive Director of the Company, functions as the Assistant General Manager - Services of Commercial Bank of Ceylon
             PLC (CBC), the sole tenant and parent company of the Company. During the quarter ended September 30, 2026, the Company received Rs. 681.2 Mn. as rental
             income, outsourcing service charges, vehicle hiring charges and fees for other utility services from CBC (Rs. 601.4 Mn. during the quarter ended September 30,
             2025).

       ( b ) As at the reporting date, the Company has invested Rs. 627.5 Mn. in Fixed Deposits (inclusive of interest) at market interest rates at CBC Finance Limited, which
             is a fully owned subsidiary of the CBC (Rs. 1,094.0 Mn. as at September 30, 2025). The interest income recognised on the fixed deposits for the quarter ended
             September 30, 2026, amounted to Rs. 16.8 Mn (Rs.23.8 Mn. for the quarter ended September 30, 2025).

            Further, the Company has also invested Rs. 255.1 Mn (inclusive of interest) in listed debentures issued by CBC Finance Limited at the rate of 11.5% per annum
            as at September 30, 2026. The interest income recognised on these debentures during the quarter amounted Rs.6.8 Mn.
       ( c ) The Company paid Rs. 0.4 Mn. to Orysis Limited, a wholly-owned subsidiary of the CBC, for the purchase of desktop computers during quarter ended september
             30, 2026. (No payments were made during the quarter ended September 30, 2025).

  6    All known expenses have been provided for in these Interim Financial Statements.
  7    There were no material events that took place since September 30, 2026, that require disclosure in these Interim Financial Statements other than those disclosed
       above.
  8    There were no contingent assets, contingent liabilities or capital commitments outstanding as at the Reporting date.

      CEO Review
      Dear Shareholders,

      The Group delivered revenue growth in 1QFY27, supported by resilient oil palm performance, while
      profitability was affected by dairy losses and higher operating cost pressures. Management remains
      focused on protecting margins, strengthening operational discipline, and advancing sustainability-led
      improvements across both business segments.

      Group Performance

      Watawala Plantations PLC recorded Group revenue of LKR 2.8 bn for the quarter ended 30 June 2026
      (1QFY27), a 15.1% YoY increase. This growth was driven primarily by the oil palm business, where revenue
      rose 17.9% YoY, partly offset by a 7.1% YoY contraction in dairy revenue.

      Group profit after tax (PAT) was LKR 884.7 Mn, up 7.1% YoY. The growth was primarily driven by the
      performance in oil palm segment. Net assets per share decreased marginally to LKR 2.94 as of 1QFY27,
      compared with LKR 3.03 as of 1QFY26.

      Oil Palm

      Oil Palm recorded revenue of LKR 2.5 bn, up 17.9% YoY, supported by higher Crude Palm Oil selling
      prices and increased volumes compared with the same period last year, underpinned by improved
      crop availability. Productivity improvements across the estates led to higher fresh fruit bunch output
      per labourer per hectare.

      Company profit after tax (PAT) rose to LKR 949.2 million, up 11.5% YoY from LKR 851.5 million, with the
      PAT margin narrowing slightly to 37.2% from 39.4% in the corresponding quarter last year.

      Dairy

      Dairy revenue stood at LKR 258.9 Mn for 1QFY27, down 7.1% YoY due to lower milk sales volumes during
      the period. The dairy business recorded a net loss of LKR 64.5 Mn, widening from a net loss of LKR
      25.5 Mn in the corresponding quarter of the previous year. The larger loss was primarily attributable
      to ongoing operational challenges, including higher effluent management costs incurred during the
      period.

      Outlook

      Palm oil prices continued to strengthen during 1QFY27, supported by favourable demand conditions.
      The company expects demand conditions to remain favourable in the near term, supported by stable
      domestic consumption and global market dynamics.

      In the dairy business, management remains focused on improving herd productivity and operational
      efficiency. Across both business segments, the company will continue to focus on operational efficien-
      cies, disciplined cost management, and sustainable agricultural practices.

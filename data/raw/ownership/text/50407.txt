01. Corporate Information
Raigam Wayamba Salterns PLC is a Public Listed Company incorporated and domiciled in Sri Lanka and listed on the
Colombo Stock Exchange.
The Company was incorporated on 15.06.2005 as a Private Limited Company (N(PVS) 42056) and re-registered on
15.01.2009 (PV 10922) and then, converted to a Public Limited Company on 21.12.2009 (PV 10922 PB) and upon listing on
the Colombo Stock Exchange, converted to a Public Listed Company on 13.08.2010 (PV 10922 PB / PQ).

The interim condensed financial statements of the Group and the Company for the Nine months ended 31 December 2025
were authorized for issue by the Board of Directors on 12.02.2026.

04. Significant Events and Transactions
During the reporting period, industrial raw salt inventory amounting to Rs. 243 million, stored in open areas within the Puttalam factory premises, was
destroyed due to flooding caused by Cyclone Ditwah, which impacted Sri Lanka in late November 2025.

In accordance with the Group’s insurance arrangements and prevailing insurance industry practices, inventories stored in open areas are not covered for
losses arising from natural perils. Accordingly, the carrying amount of the affected inventory has been written off and recognized as an expense in profit
or loss for the period, in accordance with LKAS 2 – Inventories.

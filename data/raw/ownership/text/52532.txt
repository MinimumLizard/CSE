REVIEW OF OPERATIONS


During the quarter under review, the Group’s financial performance was impacted as a result of heightened
global economic uncertainties driven through geopolitical tensions via the Middle East conflict. In May 2026,
the Central Bank of Sri Lanka increased its policy rate by 100 basis points in response to the resulting
macroeconomic pressures. Accordingly, trading opportunities in fixed income securities as well as equity
securities were substantially restrained and adverse mark-to-market movements in trading portfolios were
experienced.

First Capital Holdings PLC (the Group) recorded a Loss after Tax of Rs. 726Mn for the quarter ended 30 June
2026 compared to Profit after Tax Rs. 2.15Bn in the corresponding period of the previous year.

The Primary Dealer division reported a Loss after Tax of Rs. 741Mn for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 (1st
quarter of 2025/26 – Profit after Tax of Rs. 1.55Bn). The Results include a net interest income of Rs. 351Mn
and trading loss on government securities amounting to Rs. 1.80Bn (1st quarter of 2025/26 – net interest income
of Rs. 503Mn and trading gain on government securities of Rs. 2.06Bn and were included).

The Corporate Finance Advisory and Corporate Dealing Securities division reported a Profit after Tax of Rs.
22Mn for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 (1st quarter of 2025/26 – Profit after Tax of Rs. 587Mn).

The Wealth Management division reported a Profit after Tax of Rs. 6Mn for the quarter ended 30 June 2026
(1st quarter of 2025/26 – Profit after Tax of Rs. 27Mn). The assets under management of the division stood at
Rs. 93Bn as of 30 June 2026 (31 March 2026 – Rs. 96Bn).

The Stock Brokering division recorded a Profit after Tax of Rs. 24Mn for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 (1st
quarter of 2025/26 – Profit after tax of Rs. 32Mn).

First Capital Holdings PLC and First Capital Treasuries PLC successfully achieved independent GHG
emissions verification for year 2025/26 via the Sri Lanka Climate Fund as per ISO 14064.

8     Financial instruments- fair value measurement
      The Company measures fair values using the following fair value hierarchy that reflects the significance of the inputs used in making the
      measurements in accordance with SLFRS 13.

      Level - 1
      Financial instruments that are measured in whole or in part by reference to published quotes in an active market. A financial instrument is
      regarded as quoted in an active market if quoted prices are readily and regularly available from an exchange and those prices represent
      actual and regularly occurring market transactions on an arm's length basis.

      Level - 2
      Financial instruments that are measured at fair value on a recurring basis. As market quotes generally are not readily available or
      accessible for these securities, their fair value measures are determined using relevant information generated by market transactions
      involving comparable securities.

      Level - 3
      Valuation techniques using significant unobservable inputs. This category includes all instruments where the valuation technique includes
      inputs not based on observable data and the unobservable inputs have a significant effect on the instrument's valuation. This category
      includes instruments that are valued based on quoted prices for similar instruments where significant unobservable adjustments or
      assumptions are required to reflect differences between the instruments.

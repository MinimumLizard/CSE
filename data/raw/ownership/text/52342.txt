 8 Right Issue

     The Board of Directors resolved on 1 June 2026 to increase the Stated Capital of the Company by way of a Rights Issue of up to 10,502,975 Ordinary Voting Shares,
     in the proportion of One (01) New Ordinary Voting Share for every Four (04) existing Ordinary Voting Shares held as at the Date of Entitlement, at a consideration
     of Rs. 40/- per share, amounting to Rs. 420,119,000/- if fully subscribed. This was announced to the Colombo Stock Exchange on 2 June 2026.

     The Rights Issue remains subject to the approval in principle of the Colombo Stock Exchange for the issue and listing of the shares, and to shareholder approval at
     a General Meeting. Neither approval had been obtained as at 30 June 2026 or as at the date of these Interim Financial Statements.
     Accordingly, no effect has been given to the proposed Rights Issue in these Interim Financial Statements. The Stated Capital of the Company as at 30 June 2026
     remains Rs. 30,358,500/-, represented by 42,011,900 Ordinary Voting Shares, and there is no impact on earnings per share or net asset Value per share for the
     period.

        Management Review
        Dear Shareholder,

        Watawala Plantations PLC (CSE: WATA)
        Watawala Plantations PLC posted a revenue of LKR 5,189 million at group level for the six
        months ending 30th September 2025 (1HFY26), up 21.9% YoY due to a 26.7% YoY increase in
        revenue in the oil palm business, offset by a 6.4% YoY decrease in revenue in the dairy business.
        Profit after tax (PAT) at group level was LKR 1,725 million, up 75.2% YoY. The increase in net
        profit was mainly driven by revenue growth along with margin improvements in the oil palm
        business. The net assets per share at group level decreased to LKR 3.92 as of 30th September
        2025 from LKR 3.51 as of 31st March 2025.

        Oil Palm
        Revenue was LKR 4,611 million in 1HFY26, up 26.7% YoY driven by an increase in both price and
        sales volume compared to last year. Sales volumes were above the corresponding period last
        year due to a higher volume of crop processed and better oil extraction rates. Cost of sales
        increased 6.2% YoY primarily due to the higher cost of crop purchased for milling.

        Dairy
        Revenue was LKR 578 million in 1HFY26, down 6.4% YoY due to a drop in price offset by an
        increase in sales volume. Cost of sales reduced 13.2% YoY mainly due to the reduction in cost of
        feed, thereby converting the gross loss of LKR 12.1 million in the corresponding period last year
        to a gross profit of LKR 31.9 million. The increase in net other operating expenditure to LKR 69.5
        million due to reduced compost sales and higher cost of effluent management resulted in the
        Company recording a net loss of LKR 93.5 million.

        Outlook
        Global palm oil prices marginally increased in 2QFY26 following the dip in 1QFY26.
        The momentum is expected to continue into 2HFY26 with a marginal price increase compared
        to 2QFY26. Meanwhile, the domestic palm oil price is expected to remain at similar levels during
        the next quarter.

        Whilst we expect the positive gross profit margins to continue into 2HFY26 in the dairy sector,
        the increased net other operating expenditure will continue due to the higher cost incurred
        for effluent management.

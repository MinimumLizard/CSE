Interim Financial Statements for the Period Ended 30 th June 2026

Analysis of Statement of Financial Position

Total Assets

Total Assets of the Group has increased by Rs. 436Mn from Rs. 14,762Mn as at 31st March 2026 to Rs.
15,198Mn as at 30th June 2026. Total Non-Current Assets were Rs 7,080Mn and Total Current Assets were Rs.
8,117Mn as at 30th June 2026.

Equity Attributable to Parent Company’s Shareholders
Equity attributable to Parent Company’s Shareholders has increased by Rs. 106Mn from Rs. 6,594Mn as at
31st March 2026 to Rs. 6,700Mn as at 30th June 2026.

Net Assets Per Share (NAPS)

The NAPS has increased to Rs. 17.88 as at 30th June 2026 compared with Rs. 17.60 per share recorded as at
31st March 2026.

Analysis on Statement of Profit or Loss and Other Comprehensive Income

Revenue & Expenses
Group Revenue has decreased by 1 % to Rs. 3,769Mn for the quarter ended 30th June 2026 when compared
with Rs. 3,798 Mn in the corresponding quarter of the previous year. The composition of the Group Revenue
for the quarter is 43 % from exports sales and 57% from local sales.

Total group admin & selling expenses has increased by 11% to Rs. 526 Mn for the quarter ended 30 th June
2026 compared with Rs. 474 Mn in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

Profit / (Loss) After Tax

The Group reported a profit after tax amounting to Rs. 217Mn for the quarter ended 30th June 2026 compared
to a profit of Rs 80 Mn reported in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

Earnings / (Loss) Per Share
Earnings per Share was Rs 0.28 for the quarter ended 30th June 2026, compared to a earnings per share of Rs
0.43 in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

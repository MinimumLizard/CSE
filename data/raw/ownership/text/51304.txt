NOTES TO THE INTERIM FINANCIAL STATEMENTS FOR THE TWELVE MONTHS ENDED 31 MARCH 2026


1. Principal Activities and Nature of Operations

   The Company owns and operates Jetwing Lighthouse and Jetwing Kurulubedda which are targeted at
   the up-market leisure travelers.

   The Fully owned subsidiary of the Company, Unawatuna Properties (Pvt) Ltd (Incorporated in Sri
   Lanka) which operates Hotel J Unawatuna commenced commercial operation during the month of
   December 2017. On 8th April 2025, the Company acquired 100% of the voting shares of Dream Beach
   Resort (Pvt) Ltd an unlisted company (Incorporated in Sri Lanka) which owns Mask & Tide
   Ambalangoda.

2. Basis of Preparation

   The interim financial statements have been prepared in accordance with the Sri Lanka Accounting
   Standard (LKAS) 34 - Interim financial reporting.

3. Approval of Financial Statements
   The interim financial statements of the Group and the Company for the twelve months ended 31st
   March 2026, were authorized for issue by the Board of Directors on 25th May 2026.

4. Changes in Accounting Policies
   These interim financial statements have been prepared in accordance with the accounting policies set
   out in the Annual Report for the year ended 31.03.2025.

5. Fair value measurement and related fair value disclosures

   The fair values of all the financial assets and financial liabilities recognized during the quarter were
   not materially different from the transaction prices at the date of initial recognition. There were no
   transfers between Level 1 and Level 2 and no transfers into or out of Level 3 categories as per the fair
   value hierarchy, during the period. The fair value changes on financial instruments in Level 3 category
   was properly recorded in the statement of other comprehensive income and there were no purchases
   and/or disposals during the period. Fair valuation was done as of 31st March 2026 for unquoted equity
   shares classified as Level 3 within the fair value hierarchy according to fair valuation methodology.

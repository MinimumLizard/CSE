 10         Proposed Rights Issue
            On 16 July 2026, the Board of Directors of the Company resolved to issue up to 100 million new ordinary voting shares by way of a rights issue to
            existing shareholders, at an issue price of Rs. 32.00 per share, in the proportion of one (1) new share for every 10.22352863 existing shares held,
            with the objective of raising up to Rs. 3.2 billion.


            The proceeds of the proposed rights issue are intended to strengthen the Company's capital structure by reducing its gearing ratio and to facilitate
            new equity investments in high-growth sectors, including technology, real estate and fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG).

            In addition, the Company has resolved to issue up to 25 million ordinary voting shares by way of a private placement, subject to the full subscription
            of the rights issue and the relevant subscribers applying for such shares.

            The proposed rights issue is subject to obtaining the approval in principle of the Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE) for the issue and listing of the
            shares and shareholder approval by way of an ordinary resolution at a General Meeting.

Interim Financial Statements for the Period Ended 31st March 2026

Analysis of Statement of Financial Position

Total Assets

Total Assets of the Group have decreased by Rs. 122 Mn from Rs. 7,814 Mn as at 31st March 2025 to Rs.7,691
Mn as at 31st March 2026. Total Non-Current Assets were Rs. 4,409 Mn and Total Current Assets were Rs.3,282
Mn as at 31st March 2026.

Equity Attributable to Owners of the Company

Equity attributable to Owners of the Company has increased by Rs.773 Mn from Rs. 4,038 Mn as at 31st March
2025 to Rs. 4,810 Mn as at 31st March 2026.

Net Asset Per Share (NAPS)

The NAPS for the group has increased to Rs. 6.02 as at 31st March 2026 compared with Rs. 5.06 per share
recorded as at 31st March 2025.

Analysis on Statement of Profit or Loss and Other Comprehensive Income

Revenue & Expenses

Group revenue has decreased by 25% to Rs. 1,650 Mn for the quarter ended 31st March 2026 compared with
Rs. 2,207 Mn in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

Total group admin and selling expenses have increased by 19% to Rs. 194Mn for the quarter ended 31st March
2026 compared with Rs. 163Mn in the corresponding quarter in the previous year.

Profit / (Loss) After Tax

The Group reported a loss amounting to Rs.255 Mn for the quarter ended 31st March 2026, of which a loss of
Rs. 220.4 Mn was from the disposal of shares, compared with a loss of Rs. 61 Mn reported in the corresponding
quarter of the previous year.

Earnings / (Loss) Per Share

Earnings loss per Share was Rs. 0.32 for the quarter ended 31st March 2026, compared with a loss of Rs. 0.11
per share in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

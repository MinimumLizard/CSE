1     REPORTING ENTITY
Bukit Darah PLC is a limited liability company incorporated and domiciled in Sri Lanka. The ordinary shares of the Company have
a primary listing on the Colombo Stock Exchange. The interim consolidated financial statements as at and for the six months
ended 30th September 2026 comprise the Company and its subsidiaries (together referred to as the “Group” and individually as

oil refining in Malaysia, beverages, investment holdings, portfolio & assets management, real estate and leisure in Sri Lanka, and
has offices in Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia, Mauritius and Sri Lanka.

There were no significant changes in the nature of the principal activities of the Company and the Group during the financial
period under review.

9.3   Material litigation
An order has been made for the enforcement of an ex-parte judgment (in default of appearance) issued against Carson
Cumberbatch PLC (CCPLC) by an overseas Court for a sum of Sterling Pounds 304,671 which includes the claim plus costs, in an
action filed by a former consultant to the CCPLC (Claimant). CCPLC’s appeal against the said enforcement order was dismissed
in the Supreme Court and accordingly the aforesaid amount together with accumulated interest has become payable to the
Claimant.

CCPLC has made a provision for Rs 374 mn for the estimated potential liability, including interest and applicable costs.

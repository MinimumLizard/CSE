Summary of Performance for the 3 months ended 30th June 2025

On the back of strong brand equity and trade initiatives, CTC delivered volume growth in the past three months vs
same period last year, despite the excise driven price increase in January 2025.

The cost base of the Company is impacted by the increases driven by inflation and other trade costs, fully offset by
the savings initiatives run across the business and timing variance. The Company’s profit before income tax for the
three months ended 30th June 2025 stood at Rs. 13.9bn, while the Company’s profit after tax for the same period
was Rs. 7.4bn.

Dividends
The Directors recommend a second interim dividend of Rs 41 per share to be paid by the 27th August 2025.

Notes to the interim financial statements

1   General information

    Ceylon Tobacco Company PLC is a public limited liability Company domiciled in Sri Lanka. The principal
    operations of the Company are manufacturing, marketing and selling cigarettes. The registered office of
    business is 178, Srimath Ramanathan Mawatha, Colombo 15. The ultimate holding company of Ceylon
    Tobacco Company PLC is British American Tobacco PLC through British American Tobacco International
    Holdings BV.


2   Basis of preparation

    The condensed interim financial statements of Ceylon Tobacco Company PLC are for the six month ended
    30th June 2025. They have been prepared in accordance with LKAS 34 - Interim Financial Reporting.
    These interim financial statements have been prepared in accordance with the SLFRS standards and IFRIC
    interpretations issued and effective as at the time of preparing these statements. These interim condensed
    financial statements should be read in conjunction with the last audited annual financial statements for the
    year ended 31st December 2024, as the same accounting policies have been applied consistently.


3   Comparatives
    Comparatives are provided for the same period last year and for the statement of financial position at the
    date 31 December 2024. Where necessary, comparative figures have been reclassified to conform with the
    current year's presentation.

4   Contingent liabilities
    There have been no material changes to the contingent assets or contingent liabilities since the last annual
    reporting period.

5   Events occurring after the reporting period

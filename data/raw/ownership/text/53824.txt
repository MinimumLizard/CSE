 7. Contingent Liabilities


      There has been no significant change in the contingent liabilities disclosed in the Annual Report for the year ended 31 March
      2026, except as set out below.
      Income tax assessment for the year of assessment 2016/17
      As disclosed in Note 29 to the financial statements for the year ended 31 March 2026, the Commissioner General of Inland
      Revenue issued an income tax assessment dated 9 August 2022 to the Company for the year of assessment 2016/17, in
      respect of the restructuring transactions of the Melstacorp Group. The assessment imposes additional income tax of Rs. 4.1
      billion and a penalty of Rs. 2.0 billion, a total disputed amount of Rs. 6.1 billion. The Company challenged the assessment by
      way of a writ application to the Court of Appeal.
      On 9 October 2026, after the reporting date, the Court of Appeal dismissed the application and awarded costs of Rs. 500,000
      against the Company. The Company is taking steps to appeal against the judgment to the Supreme Court.
      Having considered the procedural and substantive grounds on which the assessment is being contested, and based on legal
      and tax advice obtained, the Directors are of the view that the assessment is not likely to result in any tax liability to the
      Company. Accordingly, no provision for the assessed amount has been made in these interim financial statements.

 9. Events occurring after the reporting period
    Subsequent to the reporting period, no circumstances have arisen which would require adjustment to or disclosure in the
    financial statements except for those disclosed below
      Distilleries Company of Sri Lanka PLC (DCSL PLC) wholly owned subsidiary, DCSL Breweries Lanka Ltd, has transferred its
      brewing and packaging activities to DCSL PLC with effect from 28th October 2026.
      The Company has obtained the approval of the Department of Excise for the transfer of the relevant licenses. Accordingly,
      DCSL PLC will undertake the brewing, packaging and distribution of beer in addition to its existing activities of distilling,
      bottling and distributing hard liquor. While this change would be convenient for the Company and the retail shops, it would
      not significantly impact the Group's profits.
      After the transfer, DCSL PLC will produce and distribute licensed beer brands, including Heineken, Tiger, and Anchor, as
      well as the Group's own brands, including Aliya, Bison, and DCSL Beer, in the Sri Lankan market.

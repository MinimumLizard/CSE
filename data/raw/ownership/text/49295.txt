Interim Financial Statements for the Period Ended 30th September 2025

Analysis of Statement of Financial Position

Total Assets

Total Assets of the Group has increased by Rs. 1,088Mn from Rs. 12,476 Mn as at 31st March 2025 to Rs.
13,565 Mn as at 30th September 2025. Total Non-Current Assets were Rs 6,797Mn and Total Current Assets
were Rs. 6,768Mn as at 30th September 2025.

Equity Attributable to Parent Company’s Shareholders
Equity attributable to Parent Company’s Shareholders has increased by Rs. 814Mn from Rs. 5,497 Mn as at
31st March 2025 to Rs. 6,311 Mn as at 30th September 2025.

Net Assets Per Share (NAPS)

The NAPS has increased to Rs. 33.69 as at 30th September 2025 compared with Rs. 29.35 per share recorded
as at 31st March 2025.

Analysis on Statement of Profit or Loss and Other Comprehensive Income

Revenue & Expenses
Group Revenue has increased by 77 % to Rs. 4,974 Mn for the quarter ended 30th September 2025 when
compared with Rs. 2,812 Mn in the corresponding quarter of the previous year. The composition of the Group
Revenue for the quarter is 57 % from exports sales and 43% from local sales.

Total group admin & selling expenses has increased by 16% to Rs. 502 Mn for the quarter ended 30th
September 2025 compared with Rs. 433 Mn in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

Profit / (Loss) After Tax

The Group reported a profit amounting to Rs. 625Mn for the quarter ended 30th September 2025 compared to
loss of Rs 81 Mn reported in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

Earnings / (Loss) Per Share
Earnings per Share was Rs 3.3 for the quarter ended 30th September 2025, compared to Loss per Share of Rs.
0.43 in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

CEO’S MESSAGE

     Dear Stakeholder,

     On behalf of the Board of Directors, I am pleased to present the unaudited Interim Financial Statements of Sarvodaya
     Development Finance PLC (SDF) for the nine months ending 31 December 2025. The third quarter reflected continued
     execution of the Company’s strategic priorities, with strong earnings growth, balance sheet expansion, and key capital
     market developments, while maintaining disciplined risk management in a volatile operating environment mainly driven by
     Ditwah Cyclone at the middle of the quarter.

     For the nine months ending 31 December 2025, the Company recorded a profit attributable to shareholders of LKR 553.3
     million, surpassing the half-billion-rupee milestone by the end of the third quarter. This performance represents a substantial
     improvement of 63.0% over the corresponding period of the previous year and was driven to 46.5% growth in gross income
     across lending, leasing, and fee-based income streams. Net interest income growth was supported by portfolio expansion,
     improved asset mix, and disciplined pricing strategies. Impairment charges were maintained in line with portfolio growth
     and credit risk assessments, reflecting the Company’s prudent approach to credit and risk management.

     SDF’s total asset base surpassed the LKR 30 billion mark, reaching LKR 33.8 billion with strong 50.1% increase from the
     previous financial year end, reflecting robust expansion of the loan and lease portfolio by 41.6%. This growth underscores
     the Company’s ability to scale responsibly while maintaining liquidity and capital adequacy well above regulatory
     thresholds.

     During the quarter, SDF successfully completed Sri Lanka’s first listed, rated, high-yield Sustainable Bond issuance, raising
     LKR 2 billion through a five-year Tier 2, unsecured, subordinated structure, which was later approved by CBSL into the Tier 2
     capital bringing the Company’s Total Capital Ratio into 21.6%, significantly exceeding the minimum regulatory requirement
     of 12.5%. The bonds, rated ‘BB+’, were oversubscribed shortly after listing, strengthening the Company’s capital base and
     supporting medium-term growth objectives. Subsequent to the local listing, the Company successfully completed the dual
     list its High-Yield Sustainable Bond on the Luxembourg Stock Exchange, with visibility on the Luxembourg Green Exchange
     (LGX) platform.

     In addition, the SDF secured USD 5 million in foreign funding from Triple Jump and Incofin during the period under review,
     further diversifying and strengthening its funding base with strong confidence with foreign lenders. The CBSL has granted
     the approval for the issuance of a five-year listed, rated, unsecured senior ‘Social-Orange Bond’ of up to LKR 2 billion,
     supporting the Company’s social financing strategy towards the women empowerment.

     The Company’s financing activities remain aligned with the principles of the Sarvodaya Movement, with funds directed
     towards women-led enterprises, MSMEs, affordable housing, sustainable agriculture, and community-based economic
     activities, consistent with relevant UN Sustainable Development Goals. During the period under review, SDF also extended
     support to staff members and communities affected by Ditwah Cyclone through the Sarvodaya network, reaffirming its
     commitment to inclusive development.

     The third quarter reflects continued progress in strengthening earnings, expanding the balance sheet, and advancing the
     Company’s sustainable finance agenda. With a reinforced capital position and growing international engagement, SDF
     remains well positioned to navigate evolving market conditions while preserving the visionary philosophy of the late Dr. A.T.
     Ariyaratne.



     Sincerely




     Nilantha Jayanetti
     Chief Executive Officer

3. FINANCIAL REPORTING BY SEGMENT									
For management purposes, the Company is organised into business units based on its products and services and has three reportable
segments, as follows:													
						
Lease									
This segment includes Leasing products offered to the customers.									
														
Loan									
This segment includes SME, Micro, Personal and Gold loans offered to the customers.							
												
Other Business									
This segment includes all other business activities that the Company engaged other than above segments.					
				
The Management Committee monitors the operating results of its business units separately for the purpose of making decisions about
resource allocation and performance assessment. Segment performance is evaluated based on profit or loss and is measured consistently
with profit or loss in the financial statements. Also, the Company’s financing (including finance costs and finance income) and income taxes
are managed on a Company basis and are not allocated to operating segments.							
								
Transfer prices between operating segments are on an arm’s length basis in a manner similar to transactions with third parties.

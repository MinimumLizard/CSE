NOTES TO THE FINANCIAL STATEMENTS


10 Disclaimer of Opinion and Transfer of Company’s Securities to the Watch List –

   The Securities of the Company had been transferred to the Watch List of the CSE with effect from
   20th October 2026 due to the Disclaimer of Opinion contained in the Independent Auditor’s Report in
   the Audited Financial Statements for the year ended 31st March 2026.

   The main two (2) bases for the Disclaimer of Opinion are the fair valuation of the investment property
   and the advance given to the Key Management Personnel (KMP) during the financial year 2025/26.

   The External Auditors, B.R De Silva & Co. were in disagreement with the investment property
   valuation report for 31st March 2026 which was valued by KPMG Real Estate & Valuation Services
   (Pvt) Ltd, despite detailed explanation and further written clarification provided by the KPMG Real
   Estate team.
   								
   KPMG Real Estate & Valuation Services (Pvt) Ltd has provided written clarification that they have
   clearly provided the methodology, market analysis and principal assumptions applied in arriving at
   the fair value. The Board of Directors are not in agreement to the disclaimer of opinion and the rea-
   sons for disagreement was not mentioned by the auditors, B R De Silva & Co.			
   					
   Further, the Board Audit Committee obtained an independent legal opinion from an expert legal firm
   confirming the validity of the advance payment made to Key Management Personnel during the FY
   2025/26, which was subsequently settled in full. The auditors, B.R. De Silva & Co., have rejected the
   expert legal opinion given by the legal firm which caused a basis for Disclaimer of Opinion stating
   that their internal expert has rejected it. The Board obtained a second legal opinion which was
   consistent with the legal opinion given by expert legal firm. 					
   			
   Remedial action proposed to be taken by the Company to resolve the matters giving rise to the
   Disclaimer of Opinion –
   								
   The Board of Directors will continue to seek clarification from the auditors, B R De Silva & Co., with
   the reasons for disputing the KPMG Valuation and the reason for not agreeing to the expert legal
   opinion together with the profiles of their internal experts who disputed these reports. 			
   					
   The Board of Directors would take necessary steps to ensure an independent audit opinion is
   obtained from an audit firm to present the true and fair view of the financial statements based on the
   CSE Listing Rules to transfer the company out of the watch list and the AGM date will be notified in
   due course to take this process forward.

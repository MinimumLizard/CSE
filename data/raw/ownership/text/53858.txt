Interim Financial Statements for the Period Ended 30th September 2026

Analysis of Statement of Financial Position

Total Assets

Total Assets of the Group have decreased by Rs. 59 Mn from Rs. 8,353 Mn as at 31st March 2026 to Rs. 8,294
Mn as at 30th September 2026. Total Non-Current Assets were Rs. 4,582 Mn and Total Current Assets were Rs.
3,712 Mn as at 30th September 2026.

Equity Attributable to Owners of the Company

Equity attributable to Owners of the Company has increased by Rs. 425 Mn from Rs. 4,582 Mn as at 31st March
2026 to Rs. 5,007 Mn as at 30th September 2026.

Net Assets Per Share (NAPS)

The NAPS for the Group has increased to Rs. 6.27 as at 30th September 2026 compared with Rs. 5.74 per share
recorded as at 31st March 2026.

Analysis of Statement of Profit or Loss and Other Comprehensive Income

Revenue & Expenses

Group revenue has decreased by 39% to Rs. 1,879 Mn for the quarter ended 30th September 2026 compared
with Rs. 3,071 Mn in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

Total group admin and selling expenses have increased by 7% to Rs. 151 Mn for the quarter ended 30th
September 2026 compared with Rs. 141 Mn in the corresponding quarter in the previous year.

Profit / (Loss) After Tax

The Group reported a profit of Rs. 198 Mn for the quarter ended 30th September 2026, compared with a profit
of Rs. 532 Mn reported in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

Earnings / (Loss) Per Share

Earnings per share amounted to Rs. 0.25 for the quarter ended 30th September 2026, compared with Rs. 0.66
earnings per share in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

1. Basis of Preparation
The Interim Financial Statements of Renuka Agri Foods PLC have been prepared in compliance with Sri Lanka
Accounting Standard LKAS 34 - Interim Financial Reporting and are subject to audit. These Interim Financial
Statements are to be read in conjunction with the annual financial statements for the year ended 31st March 2026.

The presentation and classification of figures for the corresponding period of the previous year have been
rearranged where relevant, to be comparable with those for the current period.
The Group is organized into business units based on their products and services, and performance is reported
under two segments, namely Manufacturing and Plantations during the period under review.

The stated capital of the company consists of 798,829,305 (31st March 2026 – 798,829,305) ordinary voting
shares as at 30th September 2026. A scrip dividend was declared by the Company, in respect of which 6,117,161
ordinary voting shares were allocated on 28th September 2026.



4. Earnings Per Share
Earnings per share have been calculated by dividing profit attributable to ordinary equity holders (excluding
Non-Controlling Interest) by the weighted average number of ordinary shares outstanding during the period
in accordance with LKAS 33, Earnings per Share.

  BAIRAHA FARMS PLC.




FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
     30TH SEPTEMBER 2025
Bairaha Farms Plc




For the quarter ended 30 September 2025, Bairaha Farms PLC recorded a net profit of Rs. 425 Mn,
compared to a loss of Rs. 109 Mn reported for the corresponding quarter in the last year. The cumulative
profit for the six-month period stood at Rs. 403 million, marking a significant turnaround from the profit
of Rs. 38 Mn recorded in the same period of the previous year. This improvement reflects a notable
recovery in market conditions, with prices of live birds and day-old chicks gradually increasing from June
and peaking in September. During this period, the industry also witnessed a gradual reduction in day-old
chick and live bird output due to several factors, including lower average weights in broiler birds arising
from feed quality issues in using poor quality of locally sourced maize, as well as a reduction in parent
stock placements. These developments contributed to a tightening of supply, supporting the upward
movement in prices.

Despite the improved market environment, the industry continued to face challenges in feed quality.
Although imports of maize were permitted during the quarter, the locally purchased maize of inferior
quality and procured at higher prices had to be utilized first. These circumstances adversely affected
profitability of the feed milling operation. Consequently, the overall share of joint venture profit declined,
from Rs. 192 Mn in the first six months of last year to a loss of Rs. 12 Mn for the corresponding period of
2025.

The company continues to focus on enhancing operational efficiency and maintaining product quality,
while proactively managing supply chain challenges. We remain confident that our strategic initiatives
and experience in the industry will enable us to sustain growth momentum and continue to deliver value
to our shareholders.

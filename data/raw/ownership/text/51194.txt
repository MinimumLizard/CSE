CEO’S MESSAGE

       Dear Stakeholder,

       Sarvodaya Development Finance PLC Records Strong Q4 Performance, Reinforcing its Role in Inclusive and
       Sustainable Finance
       Colombo, Sri Lanka: Sarvodaya Development Finance PLC (SDF) recorded a strong performance for the three
       months ended 31 March 2026, reflecting sustained growth in profitability, asset quality and portfolio expansion,
       while reaffirming its role as a purpose-driven development finance company serving underserved communities and
       productive sectors across Sri Lanka.

       For the quarter under review, SDF reported total income of LKR 1.87 billion, a year-on-year increase of 46.8%,
       supported by strong growth in interest income and disciplined portfolio management. Net Interest Income rose by
       40.6% to LKR 1.02 billion, while Total Operating Income increased by 43.0% to LKR 1.20 billion, demonstrating the
       Company’s ability to deliver consistent earnings growth in a dynamic financial environment.

       The Company recorded a net profit after tax of LKR 815.9 million (unaudited) for the financial year 2025/26,
       representing a significant growth of 73.4% compared to the previous financial year, while the net profit for the
       quarter reached LKR 266.6 million, doubling from LKR 131.0 million recorded in the corresponding period of the
       previous year, reflecting the strength of SDF’s business model, prudent credit expansion, and effective cost and risk
       management. Profit before tax for the quarter increased by 99.3% to LKR 437.8 million from LKR 219.7 million reported
       for same quarter of the corresponding period.

       As at 31 March 2026, SDF’s total assets stood at LKR 37.34 billion, representing a 65.6% year-on-year increase. Loans
       and receivables grew by 72.2% to LKR 21.22 billion, while lease rentals receivable increased by 25.0% to LKR 8.58
       billion, indicating broad-based portfolio growth across key customer segments. Shareholders’ funds improved by
       12.2% to LKR 4.42 billion, while Net Asset Value per Share rose to LKR 29.57.

       SDF’s performance is particularly significant given its continued focus on communities and sectors often underserved
       by mainstream financial institutions, including agriculture, micro-enterprises, SMEs and grassroots entrepreneurs. The
       Company’s lending approach places strong emphasis on future income potential, enterprise viability and long-term
       community value, rather than relying solely on conventional security-based credit assessment.

       Fourth quarter performance of SDF reflects the strength of a financial institution built around purpose, discipline and
       inclusion. SDF demonstrated that serving underserved communities and delivering commercially sound growth
       are not competing priorities. By supporting agriculture, micro and SME enterprises, women entrepreneurs and
       community-level businesses, SDF continues to create long-term social, economic and environmental value while
       maintaining strong financial fundamentals.

       SDF’s growth has also been supported by impact-minded local and international funding partners who recognize
       the Company’s ability to bridge financing gaps in areas where conventional capital is often limited. During the year,
       SDF further strengthened its leadership in sustainable finance through pioneering initiatives, including Sri Lanka’s first
       listed, rated, high-yield sustainable bond and its listing on the Luxembourg Stock Exchange.

       Looking ahead, SDF remains focused on expanding inclusive access to finance, strengthening asset quality, and
       mobilizing capital towards projects that generate meaningful and measurable development impact across Sri Lanka.




       Nilantha Jayanetti
       Chief Executive Officer

3. FINANCIAL REPORTING BY SEGMENT									
For management purposes, the Company is organised into business units based on its products and services and has three reportable
segments, as follows:													
							
Lease									
This segment includes Leasing products offered to the customers.									
														
Loan									
This segment includes SME, Micro, Personal and Gold loans offered to the customers.							
												
Other Business									
This segment includes all other business activities that the Company engaged other than above segments.					
												
The Management Committee monitors the operating results of its business units separately for the purpose of making decisions about
resource allocation and performance assessment. Segment performance is evaluated based on profit or loss and is measured consistently
with profit or loss in the financial statements. Also, the Company’s financing (including finance costs and finance income) and income taxes
are managed on a Company basis and are not allocated to operating segments.							
										
Transfer prices between operating segments are on an arm’s length basis in a manner similar to transactions with third parties.

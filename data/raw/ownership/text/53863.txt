Analysis on Statement of Financial Position

30,829Mn as at 31st March 2026. Total Non-Current Assets were Rs. 12,438Mn and Total Current Assets were Rs.

Equity Attributable to Owners of the Company

Equity attributable to owners of the Company has decreased by Rs. 413Mn to Rs. 9,247Mn as at 30th September

Net Assets Per Share (NAPS)

The NAPS has decreased to Rs. 48.75 as at 30th September 2026 compared with Rs. 50.93 per share recorded as at
31st March 2026.

Analysis on Statement of Profit or Loss and Other Comprehensive Income

Revenue & Expenses

Group Revenue has decreased by 24.13% to Rs. 3,800Mn for the quarter ended 30th September 2026 compared with
Rs. 5,008Mn in the corresponding quarter of the previous year. The composition of the Group Revenue for the quarter
is 44% from export revenue and 56% from local revenue.

Total admin and selling expenses have increased by 9% to Rs. 612Mn for the quarter ended 30th September 2026
compared with Rs. 560Mn in the comparable period in the previous year.

Profit / (Loss) After Tax

The Group reported loss of Rs.79Mn for the quarter ending 30th September 2026 compared with a profit of Rs.
1,156Mn in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

Earnings / (Loss) Per Share

Group earnings per share was Rs. 0.12 for the quarter ended 30th September 2026 compared with profit per share of
Rs.3.47 in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

Notes to the Interim Financial Statements

   01. Basis of preparation

       The Interim Financial Statements of Renuka Holdings PLC have been prepared in compliance with Sri Lanka
       Accounting Standard LKAS 34 -Interim Financial Reporting. These Interim Financial Statements are to be
       read in conjunction with the annual financial statements for the year ended 31st March 2026.

   02. Comparative figures

       The presentation and classification of figures for the corresponding period of the previous year have been
       rearranged where relevant, to be comparable with those for the current period.

       For management purposes, the Group is organized into business units based on their products and services
       and performance reported under three segments (i.e Agri Food Exports, Consumer Brands, Property and
       Investment) during the period under review.

   03. Stated Capital

       The stated capital of the company consists of 165,488,271 (31st March 2026 – 165,488,271) ordinary voting
       shares & 24,201,719 (31st March 2026 – 24,201,719) ordinary non-voting shares as at 30th September 2026.

   04. Earnings per share

       Earnings per share have been calculated by dividing profit attributable to equity holders (excluding non-
       controlling interest) by the weighted average number of ordinary shares outstanding during the period as
       given in the LKAS 33.

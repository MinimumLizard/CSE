Performance Review of Agarapatana Plantations PLC (APPLC) – Quarter Ended 30 June 2026
APPLC has recorded a profit before tax of Rs. 101 Mn for the first quarter of the financial year 2026/27 which is a
marginal increase when compared with the corresponding period of the last year. The Group performance for the 3
months was a profit before tax of Rs. 109 Mn which includes a profit before tax of Rs. 11 Mn achieved by its fully
owned subsidiary, Waverly Power (Pvt) Ltd.
The Net Sale Average of the Company increased by Rs. 94 per Kg when compared with last year. However, the
sales volume of the Company decreased by 0.4 Mn Kgs mainly due to geopolitical situation in the Middle East
which negatively impacted towards the revenue of the Company.
The net finance cost reduced significantly mainly due to the restructuring of borrowing facilities at lower interest
rates.
The Working Capital also reflected a favourable position with a current ratio of 1.34.
The Net Assets per share increased from Rs. 8.20 to Rs. 8.33 when compared with the end of the last financial year.
Future Outlook
The Company continues to be focused in maintaining a fair bottom line performance despite the challenges faced
by the industry.
To mitigate the recent increase in wages and the dearth of workers, the Company has already commenced
mechanisation including harvesting and field operations along with automation in the processing Centers. The
Company is also investing in renewable energy.
The Company is also committed to the development of Human Capital in terms of training & development,
providing opportunities to enhance worker earnings including compliance with Social Capital together with
Regulatory compliances & Corporate Governance.

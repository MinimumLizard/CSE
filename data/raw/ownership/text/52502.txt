NOTES TO THE FINANCIAL STATEMENTS

1. STATED CAPITAL
The stated capital consists of 665,000,000 shares issued as at 30th June 2026

2. BASIS OF PREPARATION
        2.1. The condensed interim financial statements of Acme Printing & Packaging PLC are for the period ended 30th June 2026. They have been prepared in accordance
        with LKAS 34, interim financial reporting. These interim financial statements have been prepared in accordance with the SLFRS standards and IFRIC interpretations
        issued and effective as at the time of preparing these statements. The condensed interim financial statements do not include all of the information required for full
        Annual financial statements and have not been audited.

        2.2. The same accounting policies and methods of computation have been followed in the interim financial statements as compared to the recent Annual financial
        statement published for the Year ended 31st March 2025.


3. COMPARATIVE INFORMATION

Comparative information has been restated / reclassified where necessary in line with the changes to the new Sri Lanka Accounting Standards (SLFRS / LKAS) which are
effective from 1st January 2012.


4. CONTINGENT LIABILITIES

        There are no material contingent liabilities as at the reporting date which would require adjustment or disclosure in financial statements other than following;



        D.C. Colombo Case No.DSP/0015/08

        Subsequent to the reporting date, on 10 July 2026, the High Court dismissed the Company's appeal and upheld the judgment of the District Court in favour of the
        Labour Union. The judgment confirms that a present obligation existed as at 30 June 2026. At the date these interim financial statements were authorized for issue,
        the Company had not completed the assessment required to determine the amount of the obligation. Consequently, no provision has been recognized because the
        amount cannot yet be estimated reliably. The Company is in the process of quantifying the financial impact.

5. EVENTS AFTER THE STATEMENT OF FINANCIAL POSITION DATE

There have been no material events occurring after the Statement of Financial Position date that require adjustments to or disclosure in the Interim Financial Statements
other than below.

        Subsequent to the reporting date, on 24 July 2026, the Company completed the sale of its land and factory for a consideration of Rs.630 million to Singer Sri Lanka
        PLC in accordance with the Sale and Purchase Agreement executed with the purchaser.


6. OTHER DISCOURSES

Right Issue of shares

Rights issue
      The Company made a Rights Issue of 570,000,000 Ordinary Shares at a price of Rs 2.50 per Share to the holders of the Issued Ordinary Shares of the Company as at
      the end of trading on 6th February 2026, in the proportion of six (6) new Ordinary Shares for every One (1) existing issued Ordinary Share held in the Capital of the
      Company. The Issue closed on 4th March 2026. The total number of shares subscribed for on the Right Issue was 570,000,000 shares and the total consideration
      received was Rs 1,425,000,000.00. The proceeds of the Rights Issue will be utilized for factory relocation and modernization, repayment of intercompany loan and
      bank borrowings, and working capital requirements.

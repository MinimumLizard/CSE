Notes To The Financial Statements

1. These Interim Financial Statements have been prepared in accordance with the requirements of Sri Lanka Accounting Standard -LKAS 34 on
'Interim Financial Reporting'.

2. During the period under review, the Company revised its depreciation policy applicable to specific biological asset categories, effective from
01 January 2025. Cinnamon rate was revised 5% to 2% and Pepper rate was revised 5% to 4%. This change has been applied prospectively from
the beginning of the financial year 2025. All other accounting policies, including depreciation rates for other asset categories, remain consistent
with those applied in the audited financial statements for the year ended 31 December 2024.

3. Stated Capital represents 202,792,332 number of issued & fully paid Ordinary Shares including one Golden Share held by the Treasury
which has special rights and 20,250,660 number of Non voting shares.

4. The above figures are provisional and subject to audit.
5. No circumstances have arisen since the Balance Sheet date, which would require adjustments or disclosures in the Financial statements.

6. The Company has been adopting Sri Lanka Accounting Standards (SLFRS/LKAS) with effect from 1st January 2012 .

7. Finance Cost incorporates a total of Rs. 85,353,549 attributable to FOREX loss, while Finance Income encompasses a total of Rs.
94,453,199 from FOREX gain for the nine months ended September 2025.

8. In the first ,second and third quarter of the previous year, interim Earnings Per Share (EPS) was presented on an annualised basis. From the
first quarter of the current year, EPS is reported on a non-annualised basis. Accordingly, comparative figures have been restated. Further, the
presentation and classification of the interim financial statements for the previous year have been amended, where relevant, to enhance
presentation and ensure comparability with the current period.
9. During the quarter ended 30 September 2025, the Company reviewed its internal reporting structure in line with the requirements of SLFRS
8 – Operating Segments. As a result, certain previously reported minor segments have been aggregated and presented under “Others,” since they
are no longer considered material for separate disclosure. This change reflects management’s revised assessment of reportable segments and has
been made in accordance with paragraphs 12–19 of SLFRS 8 and LKAS 34 – Interim Financial Reporting. Comparative information has been
reclassified to conform to the current period presentation to ensure comparability. The change in segment presentation has no impact on the
total revenue, profit before tax, or net assets previously reported for any period.

10. The interim financial statements for the quarter ended 30th Sep 2025 have been prepared on a standalone basis, in accordance with Sri
Lanka Accounting Standards (SLFRS) and the Listing Rules of the Colombo Stock Exchange. During the financial year ended 31 December
2024, the Company’s investment in Malwatte Spices (Pvt) Ltd was fully impaired and the subsidiary ceased all operational activities. As of the
reporting date, the subsidiary remains dormant with no active operations, no income-generating activity, and no ongoing transactions. The
management has assessed that the Group no longer constitutes a reporting group under SLFRS 10, as the economic activities of the subsidiary
are immaterial and non-consolidating in substance. Accordingly, consolidated financial statements have not been presented for the current
interim period. Comparative information presented for the prior periods included consolidated results. The change in presentation does not
affect the Company's financial position, performance, or cash flows for the current period.

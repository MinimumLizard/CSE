Dear Shareholders,

On behalf of the Board of Directors, I am pleased to present the financial results of the Company
for the twelve months ended 31 March 2026.

The year under review showed a significant improvement compared to previous years mainly due
to greater marketing focus and drive towards operational efficiency. The performance was
delivered despite external challenges faced during the latter part of the year.



Financial Performance

The Company recorded revenue of LKR 2.275 billion for the year ended 31 March 2026,
representing an increase of 24% compared to LKR 1.836 billion recorded in the previous financial
year. The growth in revenue reflects improved business volumes and sustained efforts to
strengthen market presence.

Gross profit improved significantly to LKR 330.1 million, an increase of 91% over the previous
year’s LKR 173.1 million, supported by improved operational efficiencies and stronger sales
performance.

Results from operating activities improved substantially to a profit of LKR 101.9 million,
compared to an operating loss of LKR 18.9 million in the preceding year. This improvement
reflects the Company’s continued focus on improved sales, cost management and operational
discipline.

Despite continued pressure from Net finance costs amounting to LKR 96.9 million, the Company
reported a profit before tax of LKR 5 million, a significant turnaround from the loss before tax of
LKR 139.6 million reported in the previous year.

The Company’s fourth quarter performance also demonstrated encouraging momentum, with
quarterly revenue increasing by 52% year-on-year to LKR 518.7 million. Gross profit for the
quarter increased by 55%, resulting in a significant improvement operating profit compared to
the corresponding period of the previous year.

While finance costs continued to impact profitability, the Company substantially reduced its
quarterly losses compared to the same period last year.

While macroeconomic uncertainties and cost pressures continue to pose challenges,
management remains focused on improving operational efficiencies, strengthening margins,
prudent cost management, and enhancing shareholder value.

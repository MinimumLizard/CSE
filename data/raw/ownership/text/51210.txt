3. EVENTS AFTER THE REPORTING PERIOD
     A final dividend of Rs.6 per share payable by cash has been recommended by the Board of Directors for the financial year ended
     31st March 2026 which requires approval of shareholders at the forthcoming Annual General Meeting.

     In accordance with LKA 10 "Events after the reporting period", this proposed final dividend has not been recognised as a liability
     for the year ended 31st March 2026.

     Other than the above, no events have occurred since the reporting date which would require adjustments to, or disclosures in,
     the financial statements.

4. CONTINGENCIES
     4.1 The Company has provided bank guarantees to the value of Rs. 164,477,194/- as at 31st March 2026.

     4.2 The tax assessments related to the company amounted to Rs. 96.44 Mn which have been duly appealed.

5.    CAPITAL EXPENDITURE
     Capital expenditure incurred during the period amount to Rs. 79.38 Mn and it is reflected in Property, Plant, and Equipement.

     The Board of Directors had approved to purchase capital assets totaling Rs. 77.40 million of which Rs. 61.04 million has been paid as an advance. The Company had no other capital
     commitments as at 31st March 2026.

7.    SEASONALITY OF OPERATIONS
     There is no seasonality / cyclical impact on the operations of the Company which need be disclosed.

8.   RECOGNITION OF A LOSS FROM THE IMPAIRMENT OF FINANCIAL ASSETS, PROPERTY, PLANT & EQUIPMENT AND INTANGIBLE ASSETS
     There were no material losses from the impairment of financial assets, property plant and equipment and Intangible assets.

9.   FINANCIAL ASSETS AT AMORTISED COST
     Financial Assets at amortized cost represents Treasury Bonds received during 2023/24 at an effective interest rate of 23.24% in lieu of outstanding receivable from the National
     Water Supply and Drainage Board of Sri Lanka for the value of Rs.189 Mn. The Financial Asset is measured at amortized Cost as at 31st March 2026 for the value of Rs. 194.7 Mn,
     which will mature on 15th September 2027

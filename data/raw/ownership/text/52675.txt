NOTES TO THE INTERIM FINANCIAL

01 Corporate Information
   Kahawatte Plantations PLC is a public limited liability company incorporated and domiciled in Sri Lanka. It is listed on the
   Colombo Stock Exchange. The registered office of the Company is located at No. 111, Negombo Road, Peliyagoda. As the
   principal activities, the Company is involved in the cultivation, manufacturing and sale of Tea, Rubber, Cinnamon, Coffee
   and other agricultural crops and to improve, manage, develop, make use of and in any manner whatsoever exploit land,
   other immovable property, resources of land, water resources, and solar power etc.


02 Basis of Preparation
   The interim condensed financial statements have been prepared in accordance with Sri Lanka Accounting Standards (SLAS)
   LKAS 34 – Interim Financial Reporting. These interim condensed financial statements should be read in conjunction with
   the annual financial statements for the year ended 31 March 2026.

   The presentation and classification of the financial statements of the previous period(s) have been amended, where relevant,
   for better presentation and to be comparable with those of the current period

   The interim condensed financial statements are presented in Sri Lankan Rupees and all values are rounded to the nearest
   thousand except when otherwise indicated.”


03 Approval of Financial Statements
   The Interim Financial Statements of the Company as at 30th June 2026 were authorized for issue by the Board of Directors
   on 14th August 2026.

For the six months ended 30 September 2026, Laxapana PLC recorded revenue of Rs. 1,913
million, in line with the corresponding period of the previous financial year. Profit Before Tax
(PBT) decreased by 17% to Rs. 179 million and Profit After Tax (PAT) by 23% to Rs. 124 million,
resulting in Earnings Per Share (EPS) of Rs. 1.87.

2. Operating Environment
The Sri Lankan economy expanded by 4.7% in the first half of 2026. However, an energy shock
stemming from tensions in the Middle East drove fuel prices higher, pushing headline inflation up
to 8.0% in August and September 2026. The Sri Lankan rupee recorded a year-to-date depreciation
of 6.3% as of September 2026.

These conditions had a direct impact on the Group. Higher living costs constrained household
purchasing power and weighed on consumer demand, while elevated material prices and tighter
financial conditions slowed construction activity. As a result, Group revenue was held in line with
the previous year, while the higher cost environment placed pressure on operating margins.

3. Segmental Performance

3.1 Energy and Lighting

The Energy and Lighting segment recorded steady revenue growth in the first six months of
FY2026/27. This was mainly led by stronger sales of LED-related products, while the growth of
the battery segment remained in line with the previous year.

The Group continues to strengthen its dealer programmes, with a focus on improving product
availability and widening market reach, to support continued sales growth in the second half of the
year.
                                              Page 1
3.2 Building Solutions

Performance in the Building Solutions segment, operated through the Group’s subsidiary Sterling
Steels (Private) Limited, declined year-on-year. Demand for building and roofing products
remained subdued amid elevated material prices and tighter financial conditions, which also
delayed the execution of industrial projects.

Management is closely monitoring the progress of these industrial projects, while sharpening its
focus on cost control, procurement efficiency, production planning and inventory management to
protect margins.


4. ESG Initiatives and Corporate Governance

The Group continued to strengthen its focus on environmental sustainability, responsible resource
management and sound corporate governance. Key priorities include improving operational
efficiency, reducing waste and environmental impacts, assessing sustainability-related risks and
opportunities, exploring low-carbon development and progressively digitalizing sustainability data
monitoring.

Underpinned by strong governance, regulatory compliance and effective internal controls, the
Group is progressively integrating ESG considerations into business planning and decision-
making to strengthen resilience and support sustainable long-term value creation.



5. Strategic Outlook

For the remainder of FY2026/27, the Group will focus on driving revenue growth, sustaining the
improvement in gross margins and tightening control over operating costs to restore earnings
momentum.

The Group remains cautious of uncertainties arising from geopolitical tensions and their potential
impact on fuel prices, inflation, exchange rates and the wider Sri Lankan economy. Management
will continue to monitor these developments closely and respond promptly to safeguard
performance and deliver long-term value to shareholders.

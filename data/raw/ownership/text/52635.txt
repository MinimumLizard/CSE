Interim Financial Statements for the Period Ended 30th June 2026

Analysis of Statement of Financial Position

Total Assets

Total Assets of the Group have increased by Rs. 605 Mn from Rs. 8,108 Mn as at 31st March 2026 to Rs.8,713
Mn as at 30th June 2026. Total Non-Current Assets were Rs. 4,395 Mn and Total Current Assets were Rs.4,318
Mn as at 30th June 2026.

Equity Attributable to Owners of the Company

Equity attributable to Owners of the Company has increased by Rs.239 Mn from Rs. 4,801 Mn as at 31 st March
2026 to Rs. 5,040 Mn as at 30th June 2026.

Net Asset Per Share (NAPS)

The NAPS for the group has increased to Rs. 6.31 as at 30th June 2026 compared with Rs. 6.01 per share recorded
as at 31st March 2026.

Analysis on Statement of Profit or Loss and Other Comprehensive Income

Revenue & Expenses

Group revenue has decreased by 10% to Rs. 1,823 Mn for the quarter ended 30 th June 2026 compared with Rs.
2,024 Mn in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

Total group admin and selling expenses have increased by 46% to Rs. 165 Mn for the quarter ended 30 th June
2026 compared with Rs. 113Mn in the corresponding quarter in the previous year.

Profit / (Loss) After Tax

The Group reported a profit amounting to Rs.239 Mn for the quarter ended 30th June 2026, compared with a
loss of Rs. 13 Mn reported in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

Earnings / (Loss) Per Share

Earnings per Share was Rs. 0.30 for the quarter ended 30th June 2026, compared with a loss of Rs. 0.02 per
share in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

1. Basis of Preparation
The Interim Financial Statements of Renuka Agri Foods PLC have been prepared in compliance with Sri Lanka
Accounting Standard LKAS 34 -Interim Financial Reporting and are subject to audit. These Interim Financial
Statements are to be read in conjunction with the annual financial statements for the year ended 31st March 2025.


2. Comparative figures
The presentation and classification of figures for the corresponding period of the previous year have been
rearranged where relevant, to be comparable with those for the current period.
The Group is organized into business units based on their products and services, and performance reported under
two segments (i.e. Manufacturing, Plantations) during the period under review.


3. Stated Capital
The stated capital of the company consists of 798,829,305 (31st March 2026 – 798,829,305) ordinary voting
shares as at 30th June 2026.



4. Earnings Per Share
Earnings per share have been calculated by dividing profit attributable to ordinary equity holders (excluding
Non-Controlling Interest) by the weighted average number of ordinary shares outstanding during the period
as given in the LKAS 33.

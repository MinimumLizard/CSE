CEO’S MESSAGE


Dear Stakeholder,

Sarvodaya Development Finance PLC (SDF) delivered a strong start to the 2026/27 financial year outpacing the increasing
complex operating environment due to challenging macro economy, regulatory pressure, and emerging external sector
vulnerabilities. The performances achieved during the first quarter of 2026/27 financial year reflects the resilience of
SDF’s business model, disciplined credit expansion, and proactive strategic retort while advancing meaningful social and
economic development across the communities we serve.

For the three months ending 30th June 2026, the Company recorded a net profit after tax of LKR 201.4 million, representing
a significant increase of 57.3% compared with the corresponding quarter of the previous year. Gross income increased
by 44.4%, while net interest income grew by 38.2%, supported by the expansion of the lending portfolio and the resulting
growth in interest income. Operating profit before taxes on financial services increased by 49.7%, and the annualized Return
on Equity improved to 17.8%, compared with 12.8% in the corresponding period of the previous year.

The Company’s financial position strengthened further during the quarter, with total assets increasing by 3.8% to LKR 38.8
billion from 2025/26 financial year end. The net lending portfolio expanded by 3.0 % to LKR 30.6 billion, while customer
deposits grew by 3.3% to LKR 11.0 billion. Bank and institutional borrowings increased by 6.5% to support portfolio growth
and maintain adequate liquidity. SDF continued to operate with a sound capital buffer, maintaining a Total Capital Ratio of
20.49%, significantly above the regulatory minimum of 12.5%, while remaining focus on prudent credit risk management
practices to achieve quality portfolio growth.

We remain committed to balancing financial strength with the inclusive development approach inherited by the
founding vision of the late Dr. A.T. Ariyaratne by supporting rural communities, entrepreneurs, women-led enterprises
and underserved segments. While thanking all my stakeholders, I ensure, with disciplined execution and a clear sense of
purpose, SDF remains well positioned to navigate the evolving environment and create sustainable, long-term value for all.

Sincerely,




Nilantha Jayanetti
Chief Executive Officer

3. FINANCIAL REPORTING BY SEGMENT									
For management purposes, the Company is organised into business units based on its products and services and has three reportable
segments, as follows:													
							
Lease									
This segment includes Leasing products offered to the customers.									
														
Loan									
This segment includes SME, Micro, Personal and Gold loans offered to the customers.							
												
Other Business									
This segment includes all other business activities that the Company engaged other than above segments.					
												
The Management Committee monitors the operating results of its business units separately for the purpose of making decisions about
resource allocation and performance assessment. Segment performance is evaluated based on profit or loss and is measured consistently
with profit or loss in the financial statements. Also, the Company’s financing (including finance costs and finance income) and income taxes
are managed on a Company basis and are not allocated to operating segments.							
		
Transfer prices between operating segments are on an arm’s length basis in a manner similar to transactions with third parties.

“the Group” referring to the Companies whose accounts have been consolidated therein.

August, 2026.

Reporting. These interim condensed financial statements should be read in conjunction with the annual financial statements for the year ended 31 March, 2025.

classification of the financial statements of the previous year have been amended, where relevant, for better presentation and to be comparable with those of the
current year.

     The Interim Financial Statements of the Company and the Group have been prepared on the basis of the same accounting policies and methods of computation applied for the year ended

     The Group accounts comprise the consolidation of accounts of the Nuwara Eliya Hotels Co. PLC (The company) and the subsidiary, Fair View Hotel (Pvt) Ltd. Intercompany balances,

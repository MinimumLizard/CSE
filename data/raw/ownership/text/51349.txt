FINANCIAL REVIEW




Cable Solutions PLC Group recorded satisfactory revenue growth during the quarter ended 31
March 2026 despite a challenging operating environment. Group revenue increased by 8% YoY
to LKR 833 Mn compared to LKR 772 Mn in the corresponding quarter of the previous year. The
growth was mainly supported by continued demand from export and deemed export customers
together with gradual improvements in customer orders.

However, profitability during the quarter was impacted by the increase in raw material prices
and higher operating costs. Gross profit declined to LKR 187 Mn from LKR 199 Mn recorded in
the corresponding quarter last year, while gross profit margins were reduced mainly due to
increased material costs and sales mix changes.

Group operating profit (EBIT) stood at LKR 68 Mn for the quarter compared to LKR 81 Mn in the
previous year. Administrative expenses increased moderately during the period due to ongoing
operational expansion and business development activities.

Accordingly, Group net profit for the quarter amounted to LKR 44 Mn compared to LKR 67 Mn
recorded in the corresponding quarter of FY2024/25. Despite the decline in profitability, the
Group continued to maintain stable operations and positive cash generation during the quarter.

The Group remains focused on strengthening export markets, improving operational efficiencies,
managing costs effectively, and expanding its customer base. Management expects business
momentum to improve gradually with continued focus on value-added products, operational
improvements, and strategic market expansion initiatives.

       review. The principal activity of the subsidiary is manufacturing cable assemblies in India.

01. BASIS OF PREPARATION
The Interim Financial Statements for the period ended 31 December 2025 have been prepared in accordance with the accounting policies
set out in the Audited Financial Statements for the year ended 31 March 2025, and also in compliance with Sri Lanka Accounting Standard
34 - Interim Financial Reporting.These interim condensed financial statements should be read in conjunction with the annual financial
statements for the year ended 31 March 2025.
The Accounting policies adopted in the preparing of Condensed Interim Financial Statements are consistent with those followed in the
preparartion of the Annual Financial Statement for the year ended 31 March 2025.
The Interim Condensed Financial Statements also provide information required by the Colombo Stock Exchange.

02. COMPARATIVE INFORMATION
The presentation and the classification of the financial statements of the previous period have been adjusted where relevant, for better
presentation and to be comparable with current reporting.

03. OTHER INCOME / OTHER EXPENSES
During the period, an insurance claim of Rs. 117 million relating to business interruption was recognised under Other Income, while the
related expenses of Rs. 27 million were recorded under Other Expenses. In the previouse year, a fire occurred at the Company’s Ekala
facility during the first quarter of the 2024/25 financial year, resulting in damage to property, plant and equipment and inventory. The
associated insurance claim and the corresponding impairment of the damaged assets were recognised in property, plant and equipment
and inventory in the previous financial year, 2024/25.

04. EARNINGS PER SHARE
Basic Earning Per Share is calculated by dividing the net profit for the period attributable to ordinary shareholders by the weighted average
number of shares outstanding during the period.

06.2 Contingent Liabilities
The Company is involved in a case of tax litigation amounting to Rs. 85,145,893 relating to Alco Industries (Pvt) Ltd, which was previously
amalgamated with Alumex PLC. To avoid further interest accumulation on this liability, the Company decided to pay the quantum under
dispute, which includes a penalty of Rs. 10,760,808 and accumulated interest of Rs. 20,580,045. After the case left the IRD, the company
initially lodged an appeal with the Tax Appeals Commission, which was dismissed by said Commission on 16th June 2025. Management has
subsequently filed an appeal with the Court of Appeal, and the matter is currently under review.

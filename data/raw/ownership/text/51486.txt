11)   Properties of Millennium Housing Limited and M.C. Urban Developers Limited
      M.C. Urban Developers Limited acquired ownership of a property located at Battaramulla a few years ago, which was pledged to Sampath Bank as security for credit facilities
      obtained by the company. Due to challenges in repaying the facility, the Bank exercised its rights under the Recovery of Loans by Banks (Special Provisions) Act No. 4 of
      1990, and following Board Resolutions and publication of notices in the Government Gazette and newspapers, proceeded to auction the property on December 28, 2023. As
      there were no third-party bidders at the Bank’s valuation-open market value of Rs. 539,500,000.00 and forced sale value of Rs. 458,500,000.00.The property was purchased
      by the Bank as permitted under the said Act. The Bank has since notified the company that interest will continue to accrue on the facility until it is fully settled. The asset
      has not been restated on the basis that, if the loan is repaid in full before the Bank resells the property to a third party, ownership of the property will revert to the company
      as though the sale had never taken place.

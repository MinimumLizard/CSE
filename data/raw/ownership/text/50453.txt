LEISURE
The tourism sector recorded 1.6 Mn
arrivals during the nine months ended
December 2025, reflecting a 16% YoY
increase and indicating a sustained
recovery from past disruptions. Arrivals
were led by India, supported by renewed
travel confidence and improved air
connectivity.

Pegasus Reef Hotel’s refurbishment
and capital enhancement programme,
which commenced in April 2025,
temporarily constrained room inventory
and function‑hall availability during the
period. As a result, the hotel recorded
an average occupancy of 55%, with
sector revenue declining by 18% YoY to
Rs. 678 Mn.

Despite these transitional impacts,
the Loss Before Tax narrowed to
Rs. 27.6 Mn from Rs. 45.1 Mn in the
previous year, reflecting effective cost
discipline alongside the ongoing capital
improvement initiatives.

REAL ESTATE
The commercial real estate sector
delivered a 10% YoY increase in revenue,
driven by a 4% YoY uplift in average
rental rates, and an increase in average
occupancy levels to 85% during the
reporting period. The sector recorded
revenue of Rs. 287 Mn and a Profit
Before Tax of Rs. 192 Mn for the nine
months ended December 2025

1     REPORTING ENTITY
Bukit Darah PLC is a limited liability company incorporated and domiciled in Sri Lanka. The ordinary shares of the Company have
a primary listing on the Colombo Stock Exchange. The interim consolidated financial statements as at and for the period ended
31st December 2025 comprise the company and its subsidiaries (together referred to as the “Group” and individually as “ Group
entities”). It is a conglomerate with diversified businesses ranging from oil palm plantations in Indonesia to palm oil refining in
Malaysia, breweries, investment holdings, portfolio & assets management, real estate and leisure in Sri Lanka, and has offices in
Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia, Mauritius and Sri Lanka.


There were no significant changes in the nature of the principal activities of the Company and the Group during the financial
period under review.

11    DISCLOSURE ON IMPLEMENTATION OF NEW LAWS IN INDONESIA FOR PALM OIL PLANTATIONS
Changes relating to spatial planning and forest area management laws in the recent past have introduced additional
requirements for plantation companies in Indonesia.


During the year, the Government of Indonesia introduced further new regulations establishing a Presidential Task Force for
the repossession of lands categorized as forest status. As per publicly available data, the new regulations have affected many
companies in the Palm Oil Plantation Sector in Indonesia covering over 4.1 million hectares.


Based on these new regulations, approximately 5,877 hectares of planted area and 6,275 hectares of unplanted area pertaining
to five subsidiaries of Goodhope Asia Holdings Ltd (“Goodhope”) are impacted. The Task Force has subsequently transferred the
repossessed lands to a newly established state-owned enterprise, which is presently engaged in discussions with Goodhope Asia
Holdings Ltd regarding the potential arrangements for the management of the plantations on these lands.


While discussions regarding the future management of these plantations are ongoing, there is no prohibition on plantation
operations in this repossessed area, the repossession itself triggered an impairment assessment. Accordingly, the Group has
recognized a provisional write-down of non-current assets value of USD 14.5 million, in relation to the above land area.


12    EVENTS AFTER THE END OF THE REPORTING PERIOD, 31 DECEMBER 2025
In January 2026, the Task Force has intimated to Goodhope a provisional administrative fine of approx. IDR 1,826 billion
(equivalent to approx. USD 108.4 million) which has been communicated as a temporary notification and subject to future
adjustment and finalisation following further review by the Task Force. Goodhope has sought detailed clarification from the
authorities regarding the basis and methodology underlying this provisional computation and is actively discussing the matter
with relevant authorities. No provision has been made at this time. Goodhope has adequate liquidity to meet the fine above and
future operational commitments.


Subsequent to the reporting period, no circumstances have arisen which would require adjustments to or disclosure in the
financial satement other than the above.

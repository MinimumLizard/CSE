FINANCIAL REVIEW

The company has reported a constant top line growth of 34% in Quarter 4 while ensuring a stable level of financial position
and profitability contributed by effective management and underwriting practices.

The Gross Written Premium for the twelve months ended period ended 31st December 2025 was Rs. 8,367.39 Mn
compared to Rs. 6,249.30 Mn in 2024.

The profit before tax is Rs. 472.72 Mn for the twelve months ended 31st December 2025 compared to Rs. 701.62 Mn
achieved in 2024 and the Profit after tax of the company for the period under review was recorded at Rs. 355.25 Mn against
the Rs. 429.42 Mn in the same period of the previous year.

The Motor segment was the largest contributor to the company’s GWP, accounting to 79% and reported a 42% growth
compared to the previous financial year. The Fire segment demonstrated strong growth, recording a 15% increase and
contributing 9% to the overall GWP.

Company’s claims cost increased by 1% to Rs. 3,164.89 Mn for the twelve months, while operating expenses increased by
19% to Rs. 1,959.58 Mn from Rs. 1,641.59 Mn in 2024, driven by rising salary cost due to expansion of direct sales unit,
increased staff welfare and shared cost.

Underwriting Loss of the Company increased by 23% to Rs. 633.88 Mn in 2025 compared to Rs. 513.36 Mn in 2024 mainly
due to the second year impact motor SRCC & TC 100% cession to NITF.
Investment and other income which comprises interest and dividend income, net fair value gains/ losses and other income,
decreased by 9% to Rs. 1,106.60 Mn in 2025 reflecting the impact of fluctuating market conditions and lower investment
returns.

The Company’s total asset base expanded by 23% to Rs. 15.67 Bn as of 31st December 2025, compared to Rs. 12.69 Bn in
the previous year while financial investments grew by 14%, reflecting the Company’s proactive approach to managing cash
flows and optimizing investment returns.

Total shareholders' equity increased to Rs. 5,970.13 Mn in 2025, reflecting a 6% growth compared to Rs. 5,637.95 Mn in
2024.

Cash equivalents decreased by Rs. 73.05 Mn in 2025 Compared to previous year.

The Company is committed in driving sustainable growth while maximizing long term stakeholder expectation.

 1   Basis of Preparation
     The interim Financial Statements have been prepared in accordance with Sri Lanka Accounting Standard for Small and
     Medium-sized Entities ("SLFRS for SMEs") and the same accounting policies and methods set out in the Audited
     Financials Statements for the year ended 31st March 2024 and are in compliance with LKAS 34 – Interim Financial
     Reporting. The presentation and classification of comparative information have been amended where relevant. The
     interim financial statements are not audited.
 2   Contingencies
     The Company does not have any other significant contingent liabilities outstanding as at the reporting date which require
     adjustments to or disclosure in the financial statements.
 3   Events occurring after the reporting date
     There were no material events occurring after the reporting date which require adjustments to or disclosures in the
     financial statements.

 5   Subdivision of Shares
     Shareholders of the Company at its Extraordinary General Meeting held on 16 January 2024 has approved that the
     number of ordinary shares of the Company in issue be increased by way of a sub-division of shares where by one (01)
     ordinary share was subdivided in to five (5) ordinary shares. The resulting number of shares after the sub-division is
     37,950,000. There was no change to the stated capital of the Company subsequent to the said sub-division of shares. The
     company has concluded the sub-division on 26 January 2024.

Performance overview
Senkadagala Finance PLC delivered a strong performance for the year ended 31 March 2026, with total assets
increasing by 39.8% to surpass the Rs. 50 Bn mark, driven by significant growth in lending activities and
investments. Net operating income recorded a healthy growth of 16.8%, while profit after tax increased by 13.9% to
Rs. 1.84 Bn for the year. Asset quality improved further, with the gross Stage 3 loans ratio declining to 5.43% from
7.24% in the previous year. The Company also maintained prudent provisioning buffers while strengthening its
capital base through a successful rights issue, during the year. Liquidity and capital adequacy levels remained
comfortably above the regulatory minimum requirements, reflecting the Company’s continued financial resilience
and stable growth momentum.

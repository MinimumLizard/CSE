6         Notes to the condensed interim financial statements

    6.1   Condensed Interim financial statements


          The Statement of Financial Position as at 30th September 2026, Statement of Profit or Loss, Statement of Other Comprehensive Income,
          Statement of Cash Flows and Statement of Changes in Equity of the Company for the period ended there of are drawn up from Unaudited
          financial statements of the Company and provide information as required by the Colombo Stock Exchange and LKAS 34. Further,
          provisions of the Companies Act No.7 of 2007 has been considered in preparing the said financial statements of the Company.

          The presentation and classification of the financial statements of the previous period have been amended, where relevant, for better
          presentation and to be comparable with those of the current period. The condensed interim financial statements are presented in Sri Lankan
          Rupees (LKR) and all values are rounded to the nearest thousand except when otherwise indicated.

    6.2   Basis of preparation

          The same Accounting Policies and methods of computation as mentioned in the audited financial statements for the year ended 31
          December 2025 have been adopted in the preparation of these condensed financial statements.

          The Company has adopted Amendments to SLFRS 4 in the preparation of financial statements. Application of SLFRS 9 is not considered,
          considering the temporary exemption permitted under Para 20B of SLFRS 4 as the Company's activities are predominantly connected with
          insurance.
          Application of Statement of Alternative Treatment (SOAT) – Q3 Interim Financial Reporting

          By considering insurance industry implementation readiness and practical implementation challenges affecting the insurance industry, CA
          Sri Lanka has extended insurers to continue applying SLFRS 4 Insurance Contracts upto the third interim reporting quarters of the year
          2026. The Company has elected to apply this relief for the quarter ended 30 September 2026.
          Accordingly, for this interim period:

          Insurance contracts continue to be recognised, measured , and presented in accordance with SLFRS 4 Insurance Contracts

          Progress of SLFRS 17 and SLFRS 9 Implementation

          The Company has made substantive progress upto the third quarter of the financial year towards full implementation of SLFRS 17,
          including:

          • Parallel processing and dry runs of SLFRS 17 numbers including opening balance reconciliations
          • Strengthening of governance, controls, and data validation processes
          • Development of system modules and integrations with acturial models.

          Expected Impact of SLFRS 17

          The adoption of IFRS 17 introduces a comprehensive and principle-based accounting framework for insurance contracts,
          replacing the requirements under IFRS 4, which permitted insurers to continue applying existing local accounting policies. For
          the company's general insurance contracts, which are predominantly short-term in nature, the Premium Allocation Approach
          (PAA) has been applied where the eligibility criteria prescribed by IFRS 17 are met. While the PAA provides a simplified
          measurement model, it incorporates significant changes to the recognition, measurement, presentation and disclosure of insurance
          contracts. Under IFRS 17, the Liability for Remaining Coverage (LRC) replaces the unearned premium reserve, insurance
          revenue is recognised as insurance services are provided rather than based on premium earnings, and the Liability for Incurred
          Claims (LIC) is measured using fulfilment cash flows, including an explicit Risk Adjustment for non-financial risk. In addition,
          the previous liability adequacy testing requirements under IFRS 4 have been replaced by an assessment of onerous contracts
          within the measurement framework. Accordingly, IFRS 17 enhances the consistency, transparency and comparability of the
          Group's financial reporting while maintaining a simplified measurement approach for eligible short-duration insurance contracts.


          In accordance with the SOAT, the entity will not present the quantitative disclosures with the third interim financial statements
          for the period ended 30th September 2026, as the implementation and validation of SLFRS 17 are still in progress. The company
           expects to meet implementation deadline as issued by CA Sri Lanka.

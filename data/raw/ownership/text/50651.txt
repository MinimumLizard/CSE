    The Company recorded a growth of 19% in Gross Written Premiums (GWP) for the year, reflecting strong momentum across both
    regular new business premiums and renewal premiums.

    The surplus generated from the life insurance business increased from Rs. 3.0 Bn to 3.4 Bn during the year, driven by strong
    business growth and enhanced operational efficiency, despite operating in a low-interest rate environment compared to the previous
    financial year.

    The YoY net investment income grew by 5% despite the decline in re-investment rates while the equity portfolio performed well
    during the year contributing to a 52% growth in net realized gains over the previous year. Despite total claims, benefits and expenses
    growing by 16%, against a 19% growth in revenue, as a result of total other revenue growing by 7%, the profit from operations
    showed a 2% growth over the previous year.

    On 18 September 2025, the Company divested its 22% stake in Fairfirst Insurance Limited to Fairfax Asia Limited. The reduction
    in profit contribution from the equity accounted investee compared to the corresponding year is due to accounting for only eight
    months of share of profits of Fairfirst as a consequence of the divestment. In addition, the Company incurred Rs. 311.5 Mn in capital
    gains tax on this divestment, which contributed to a 21% increase in income tax expense for the year.

    Excluding the share of profits from the equity accounted investee and the one-off capital gains tax, the YoY Profit after tax grew by
    2%.

    A key strategic milestone during the year was the Company’s entry into an exclusive bancassurance partnership with Sampath
    Bank PLC, significantly strengthening its bancassurance presence. The Company now operates one of the largest bancassurance
    networks in the country, a key pillar in its long-term value creation journey.

1     REPORTING ENTITY
      Union Assurance PLC (‘the Company’) is a public limited liability company incorporated and domiciled in Sri Lanka and the ordinary
      shares of the Company are listed on the Colombo Stock Exchange. The registered office and principal place of business of the
      Company is located at No. 20, St. Michael’s Road, Colombo 03.

      The Company’s controlling entity and ultimate Parent undertaking is John Keells Holdings PLC which is incorporated in Sri Lanka.

2     APPROVAL OF FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
      The interim financial statements for the year ended 31 December 2025 were authorised for issue by the Board of Directors on 27
      February 2026.

      The Company divested its stake in Fairfirst Insurance Limited, an equity accounted investee on 18 September 2025. Fairfirst
      Insurance Limited has been presented in the financial statements in accordance with Sri Lanka Accounting Stadnards, LKAS 28
      - Investment in Associate and Joint Venture up to the disposal date. Please refer Note 10 for details.

4     BASIS OF PREPARATION AND CHANGES TO THE COMPANY'S ACCOUNTING POLICIES
4.1   Basis of Preparation
      The interim financial statements have been prepared in compliance with Sri Lanka Accounting Standard LKAS 34 – Interim
      Financial Reporting. These interim financial statements should be read in conjunction with the annual financial statements for the
      year ended 31 December 2024. Further, provisions of the Companies Act No.7 of 2007 have been considered in preparing the
      interim financial statements of the Company. The same accounting policies have been followed in preparation of these interim
      financial statements as stated in the Audited Financial Statements for the year ended 31 December 2024.

      The interim condensed financial statements are presented in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR) and all values are rounded to the nearest
      thousand except when otherwise indicated.

4.2   Fair value measurement and related fair value disclosures
      The fair values of all the financial assets and financial liabilities recognised during the period were not materially different from the
      transaction prices at the date of initial recognition. All listed equity instruments were measured based on the market prices as at
      31 December 2025 and classified as level 1 as per the fair value hierarchy. There were no transfers between Level 1 and Level 2

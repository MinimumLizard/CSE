        Management Review
      Dear Shareholder,

      Watawala Plantations PLC (CSE: WATA)
      Watawala Plantations PLC posted revenue of LKR 7,267 million at group level for the nine
      months ending 31 st December 2025 (9MFY26), up 20.6% YoY, driven by a 25.8% YoY increase in
      revenue from the oil palm business, partly offset by an 8.1% YoY decrease in revenue from the
      dairy business.

      Profit after tax (PAT) at group level was LKR 2,184 million, up 48.3% YoY. The increase in net
      profit was mainly driven by revenue growth along with margin improvements in the oil palm
      business. Net assets per share at group level decreased to LKR 2.68 as of 31 st December 2025
      from LKR 3.51 as of 31 st March 2025.

      Oil Palm
      Revenue was LKR 6,425 million in 9MFY26, up 25.8% YoY, driven by an increase in both price
      and sales volume compared to last year. Sales volumes were above the corresponding period
      last year due to a higher volume of crop processed and improved oil extraction rates. Cost of
      sales increased 12.6% YoY, primarily due to the higher cost incurred in purchasing a larger vol-
      ume of crop.

      Dairy
      Revenue was LKR 843 million in 9MFY26, down 8.1% YoY due to declines in both volume and
      price. Cost of sales reduced 11.1% YoY, primarily due to lower feed costs. The increase in net other
      operating expenditure to LKR 147 million, due to reduced compost sales and higher effluent
      management costs, resulted in the Company recording a net loss of LKR 168 million.

      Outlook
      Global palm oil prices dipped in 4Q 2025 due to weak export demand and spillover effects from
      falling global crude oil prices. This dip is expected to continue as the increase in supply is
      anticipated to surpass demand. Meanwhile, domestic palm oil prices are expected to remain at
      similar levels during the next quarter.

      While we expect the gross profit margin in the dairy sector to improve in 4QFY26, the increased
      net other operating expenditure will continue due to the higher cost incurred for effluent
      management.

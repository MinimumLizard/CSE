FINANCIAL REVIEW


The company has maintained a strong financial position during the second quarter 2025. The significant growth in the top
line together with the effective management of the claims and prudent underwriting practices has contributed significantly
for the improved performance.

The company recorded a GWP of Rs. 3,752.80 Mn for the six months ended 30th June 2025 compared to Rs. 2,829.89 Mn
in 2024 indicating a growth of 33% for the six months of the year.

The profit before tax is Rs. 321.57 Mn for the six months ended 30th June 2025 compared to Rs. 429.15 Mn achieved in
2024 and the Profit after tax of the company for the period under review was recorded at Rs. 221.58 Mn against the Rs.
293.10 Mn in the same period of the previous year.

The Motor segment remained the largest contributor to the company’s GWP, accounting to 77% and reported a 40% growth
compared to the previous financial year. The Fire segment demonstrated strong growth, recording a 19% increase and
contributing 14% to the overall GWP.

Company’s claims cost decreased by 11% to Rs. 1,373.34 Mn for the six months, while operating expenses increased by
35% to Rs. 994.24 Mn from Rs. 734.37 Mn in 2024, driven by rising salary costs due to expansion of sales unit ,
technology maintenance and increased shared cost.

Underwriting Loss of the Company increased by 8% to Rs. 231.90 Mn in 2025 compared to Rs. 215.16 Mn in 2024 mainly
due to second year motor SRCC & TC 100% cession to NITF.
Investment and other income which comprises interest & dividend income, net fair value gains/ losses and other income,
decreased by 14% to Rs. 553.47 Mn in 2025 reflecting the impact of fluctuating market conditions and lower investment
returns.

The Company’s total asset base expanded by 6% to Rs. 13.44 Bn as of 30th June 2025, compared to Rs. 12.69 Bn in the
previous year while financial investments grew by 7%, reflecting the Company’s proactive approach to managing cash
flows and optimizing investment returns.

Total shareholders' equity increased to Rs. 5,841.83 Mn in 2025, reflecting a 4% growth compared to Rs. 5,637.95 Mn in
2024.

Cash equivalents decreased by Rs. 18.35 Mn in 2025 Compared to previous year.

The Company is committed in driving continues sustainable growth while fostering stakeholder expectations aligning with
strategic business objectives.

11. IMPACT OF DITWA CYCLONE ON LOTUS HYDRO POWER PLC OPERATIONS
 Lotus Hydro Power PLC Group was heavily affected by the “Ditwa” Cyclone in November 2025. Three power plants—Delta,
 Sanquhar, and Stellenberg—sustained significant damage due to the cyclone.

 The Stellenberg plant resumed operations on 12th December 2025 after completing the necessary repair works. However, the
 damage to the Delta and Sanquhar plants was substantial. Repair works at the Sanquhar plant are currently in progress, and the
 company expects to recommence its commercial operations in February 2026.

 Repairs to the Delta plant have not yet commenced, as professional assessments are still being awaited due to the severity of the
 damage caused by landslides. It is anticipated that a considerable amount of time will be required before operations at the Delta
 plant can be resumed.

 The company has formally informed the relevant authorities of the damages sustained by the affected power plants as a result of
 the Ditwa Cyclone and is currently following up on any relief or assistance that may be available

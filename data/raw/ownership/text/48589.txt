FINANCIAL REVIEW

Cable Solutions PLC commenced the financial year 2025/26 on a strong note, recording solid
revenue growth and improved profitability, driven by higher export demand and improved
operational efficiencies.

Group revenue for Q1 FY26 stood at LKR 892mn, representing a 16% increase YoY compared to
LKR 769mn in Q1 FY25. The topline growth was broad-based, supported by improved volumes
from both existing and newly onboarded clients. The company is seeing good demand from
exports and deemed export customers while there are also pockets of strength from the select
domestic customer base. Orders from two recently onboarded customers have started to gain
traction and the company is also increasingly focusing on the US market for new opportunities.

Gross profit increased to LKR 290mn, up 8% YoY, although gross margin compressed slightly to
32.6% from 35.1% in Q1 FY2025, primarily due to a less favorable sales mix in comparison to the
previous year.

Group operating profit (EBIT) rose to LKR 170mn, registering a 12% YoY growth, reflecting
improved cost discipline and better operating leverage.

Group net profit for the quarter was LKR 135mn, up 14% YoY, translating to a net profit margin
of 15.1%. This improvement reflects strong operational performance and a modest decline in net
interest costs. The Indian operation made a loss of about LKR 7mn for the quarter, but client
traction is improving, and performance should improve as the year progresses.

Inventory and trade receivables remained elevated in line with the growing scale of operations,
although the Company continues to emphasize working capital discipline.

The Group’s net debt-to-equity ratio remains stable at 11.3% in Q1 FY26 and is expected to
improve as internal cash generation strengthens over the coming quarters. Capex commitments
for the ERP rollout and machinery upgrades are being managed within the Company's liquidity
profile.

Going forward, the Company expects to maintain growth momentum through increased
contribution from new product lines (e.g., EV cables, FLRY variants), deeper penetration into the
U.S. market (via the newly formed subsidiary), optimized production through better planning
tools, and strategic investments in R&D, certifications, and digital tools.

The broader macro environment remains favorable with copper prices stabilizing and global
demand for renewable and electric mobility solutions continuing to grow. It also seems the US
tariff related noises should start to ease in coming months. CSLK is well positioned to capitalize
on these trends with its diversified customer base, growing international presence, and ongoing
investment in quality and automation.

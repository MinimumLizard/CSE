   Colombo 03, and the principal place of the business is situated at Elkaduwa, Kandy.

   The Statement of Financial position of the Company as at 30th September 2026, Statement of Profit or Loss & Comprehensive income, Statement
   of Changes in Equity and Statement of Cash Flow for the period then ended have been prepared in accordance with Sri Lanka Accounting
   Standards (SLFRSs/LKASs) and are in agreement with the books of accounts maintained by the Company and have provided the information
   required by the Colombo Stock Exchange.


   The Interim Condensed Financial Statements have been prepared in compliance with Sri Lanka Accounting Standard LKAS 34- Interim Financial

Notes To The Financial Statements

1. These Interim Financial Statements have been prepared in accordance with the requirements of Sri Lanka Accounting Standard -LKAS 34 on
'Interim Financial Reporting'.

2. The accounting policies adopted in the preparation of these Interim Condensed Financial Statements are consistent with those followed in the
preparation of the Company’s Annual Financial Statements for the year ended 31 Dec 2025.

3. Stated Capital represents 202,792,332 number of issued & fully paid Ordinary Shares including one Golden Share held by the Treasury which
has special rights and 20,250,660 number of Non voting shares.
4. The above figures are provisional and subject to audit.

5. No circumstances have arisen since the Balance Sheet date, which would require adjustments or disclosures in the Financial statements.
6. The Company has been adopting Sri Lanka Accounting Standards (SLRFS/LKAS) with effect from 1st January 2012 .

7. Finance Cost incorporates a total of Rs. 26,073,850 attributable to FOREX loss, while Finance Income encompasses a total of Rs. 35,057,919
from FOREX gain for the three months ending in 2026.


8. Starting from the quarter ended 30 September 2025, the Company reviewed its internal reporting structure in line with the requirements of
SLFRS 8 – Operating Segments. As a result, certain previously reported minor segments have been aggregated and presented under “Others,”
since they are no longer considered material for separate disclosure. This change reflects management’s revised assessment of reportable segments
and has been made in accordance with paragraphs 12–19 of SLFRS 8 and LKAS 34 – Interim Financial Reporting. Comparative information has
been reclassified to conform to the current period presentation to ensure comparability. The change in segment presentation has no impact on the
total revenue, profit before tax, or net assets previously reported for any period.

9. Effective 01 January 2026, the Ministry of Plantation and Community Infrastructure agreed with Regional Plantation Companies to increase
the daily wage of plantation workers from LKR 1,350 to LKR 1,750 under the 2026 Budget proposal. The LKR 400 increase comprises LKR 200
borne by the plantation companies and LKR 200 funded by the Government as a wage support incentive.
10. No Provision was made for Deferred Tax.

11. The Financial Statements were authorized for issue on 12th May 2026.

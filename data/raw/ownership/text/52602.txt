    The Stated Capital of the Company as at 30th June 2026 was Rs.838,032,311/66 comprising of 97,200,743 Ordinary Shares.

7.1 Share Repurchase
    On 23rd July 2026, the Company announced an offer to repurchase of 1 ordinary share for every 54 ordinary shares held.
    Accordingly, the Company would repurchase a maximum of 1,800,014 ordinary shares from the Shareholders at a price of Rs.203.33
    per share, and the total value of the share repurchase transaction amount would be Rs.366 Mn.

8   GOING CONCERN
    The Board of Directors is not aware of any material uncertainties that may cast significant doubt upon the Company’s ability to continue
    as a going concern. Accordingly, the interim financial statements are prepared on the going concern basis.

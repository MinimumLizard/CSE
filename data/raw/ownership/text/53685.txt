Performance Review of Agarapatana Plantations PLC (APPLC) – Period Ended 30 September 2026
APPLC has recorded a profit before tax of Rs. 57 Mn for the six months period of the financial year 2026/27 which
is a significant decrease when compared with the corresponding period of the last year. The Group performance for
the period was a profit before tax of Rs. 69 Mn which includes a profit before tax of Rs. 12 Mn achieved by its fully
owned subsidiary, Waverly Power (Pvt) Ltd.
The sales volume of the Company decreased by 266,000 Kgs mainly due to geopolitical situation in the Middle
East which negatively impacted towards the revenue of the Company. Further, the wage increase of 15% effected
from January 2026 also significantly contributed to the reduction in profitability.
The net finance cost reduced significantly mainly due to the restructuring of borrowing facilities at lower interest
rates.
The Working Capital also reflected a favourable position with a current ratio of 1.34.
The Net Assets per share increased marginally from Rs. 8.20 to Rs. 8.22 when compared with the end of the last
financial year.
Future Outlook
The Company continues to be focused in maintaining a fair bottom line performance despite the challenges faced
by the industry.
To mitigate the recent increase in wages and the dearth of workers, the Company has already commenced
mechanisation including harvesting and field operations along with automation in the processing Centers. The
Company is also investing in renewable energy.
The Company is also committed to the development of Human Capital in terms of training & development,
providing opportunities to enhance worker earnings including compliance with Social Capital together with
Regulatory compliances & Corporate Governance.

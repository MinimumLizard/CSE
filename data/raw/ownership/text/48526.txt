    During the 1st quarter of the comparative year (2024/25), a fire occurred in the Company's facility in Ekala, resulting in damages to property, plant, equipment

    inventory of Rs. 947 mn under other expenses during the year ended 31st March 2025. As of 30th June 2025, the Company received Rs. 1.00 Bn out of the
    insurance claim of Rs. 1.45 Bn.

06.2 Contingent Liabilities

    The Company is involved in a case of tax litigation amounting to Rs. 85,145,893 relating to Alco Industries (Pvt) Ltd, which was previously amalgamated with
    Alumex PLC. To avoid further interest accumulation on this liability, the Company decided to pay the quantum under dispute, which includes a penalty of Rs.
    10,760,808 and accumulated interest of Rs. 20,580,045. The Company appealed to the Tax Appeals Commission (TAC) against the IRD verdict, but in June
    2025, the TAC ruled in favour of the IRD. The company has now filed an appeal against the TAC ruling to the Court of Appeal and the matter is currently
    under review.

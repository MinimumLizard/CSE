NOTES TO THE FINANCIAL STATEMENTS

1. Corporate Information
Royal Ceramics Lanka PLC (“the Company”) is a public limited liability company incorporated and domiciled in Sri Lanka and listed on the
Colombo Stock Exchange. The registered office of the Company is located at No.20, R.A. De Mel Mawatha, Colombo 03.

The Consolidated Financial Statements of the company for the quarter ended 30th September 2026 comprise the Royal Ceramics Lanka PLC
(Parent Company) and its subsidiaries (together referred as the “Group”), namely Rocell Bathware Limited, Ever Paint and Chemical Industries
(Private) Limited, Biscuits and Chocolate Company Limited, Nilano Garments (Private) Limited, Rocell Properties Limited, CP Holding (Private)
Limited, Lanka Ceramic PLC, LC Plantation Project Limited, Lanka Walltiles PLC and its subsidiaries.

2.Figures for the period ended 30th September 2026 are provisional and unaudited.

3. Number of fully paid ordinary shares as at 30th September 2026 is 1,107,893,840 (30th September 2025- 1,107,893,840).

4.There has not been a significant change in the nature of the contingent liabilities which were disclosed in the Annual Report for the year ended
31st March 2026.

5. Fair value measurement and related fair value disclosures
The fair values of all the financial assets and financial liabilities recognized during the period were not materially different from the transaction
prices at the date of initial recognition. There were no transfers between Level 1 and Level 2 and no transfers into or out of Level 3 categories as per
the fair value hierarchy, during the reporting period. Further, carrying values of financial assets and financial liabilities as at the end of the reporting
period were assumed that the respective carrying amounts approximates their fair values.

6. Events Occurring After the Balance Sheet Date
Proposed Rights Issue by a Subsidiary-Lanka Walltiles PLC
Subsequent to the reporting date, the Board of Directors of Lanka Walltile PLC, a subsidiary of Royal Ceramic Lanka PLC, approved a proposal to
raise capital by way of a rights issue, subject to obtaining the necessary shareholder and regulatory approvals. The proposed rights issue comprises
eight (8) new ordinary voting shares for every seventeen (17) existing ordinary voting shares at an issue price of Rs. 39/- per share, with the
objective of raising approximately Rs. 5 billion.

Proposed Amalgamation of Vallibel Plantation Management Limited with Lanka Walltiles PLC
Further to the Board of Directors' resolution approving the proposed rights issue of shares by Lanka Walltiles PLC, the Board also resolved to
amalgamate Vallibel Plantation Management Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary of Lanka Walltiles PLC, with Lanka Walltiles PLC immediately
following the completion of the rights issue. The proposed amalgamation is subject to the Company satisfying the solvency test immediately after
the amalgamation becomes effective.

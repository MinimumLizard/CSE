Interim Financial Statements for the Period Ended 31st December 2025

Analysis of Statement of Financial Position

Total Assets

Total Assets of the Group has increased by Rs. 1,503Mn from Rs. 12,476 Mn as at 31st March 2025 to Rs.
13,979 Mn as at 31st December 2025. Total Non-Current Assets were Rs 6,870Mn and Total Current Assets
were Rs. 7,109Mn as at 31st December 2025.

Equity Attributable to Parent Company’s Shareholders
Equity attributable to Parent Company’s Shareholders has increased by Rs. 1,121Mn from Rs. 5,497 Mn as at
31st March 2025 to Rs. 6,619 Mn as at 31st December 2025.

Net Assets Per Share (NAPS)

The NAPS has increased to Rs. 35.33 as at 31st December 2025 compared with Rs. 29.35 per share recorded
as at 31st March 2025.

Analysis on Statement of Profit or Loss and Other Comprehensive Income

Revenue & Expenses
Group Revenue has increased by 35 % to Rs. 4,854 Mn for the quarter ended 31st December 2025 when
compared with Rs. 3,585 Mn in the corresponding quarter of the previous year. The composition of the Group
Revenue for the quarter is 54 % from exports sales and 46% from local sales.

Total group admin & selling expenses has increased by 14.5% to Rs. 522 Mn for the quarter ended 31st
December 2025 compared with Rs. 482 Mn in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

Other income comprises disposal of shares in group company which has resulted in a gain of Rs.441Mn.

Profit / (Loss) After Tax

The Group reported a profit amounting to Rs. 459Mn for the quarter ended 31st December 2025 compared to
loss of Rs 247 Mn reported in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

Earnings / (Loss) Per Share
Earnings per Share was Rs 2.45 for the quarter ended 31st December 2025, compared to Loss per Share of Rs.
1.05 in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

     Notes to the Interim Financial Statements

1.   Basis of Preparation
     The interim financial statements of Renuka Foods PLC have been prepared in compliance with Sri Lanka
     Accounting Standard LKAS 34 -Interim Financial Reporting. These interim financial statements are to be read
     in conjunction with the annual financial statements for the year ended 31st March 2025.
2.   Comparative figures
     The presentation and classification of figures for the corresponding period of the previous year have been
     rearranged where relevant, to be comparable with those for the current period.

     For management purposes, the Group is organized in to business units based on their products and services
     and performance reported under two segments (i.e. Agri Food Exports and Consumer Brands) during the
     period under review.

3.   Stated Capital
     The stated capital of the company consists of 180,005,317 (31st March 2025 – 180,005,317) ordinary voting
     shares and 7,306,800 (31st March 2025 – 7,306,800) ordinary non-voting shares as at 31st December 2025.

4.   Earnings per Share
     Earnings per share have been calculated by dividing profit attributable to ordinary equity holders (excluding
     non-controlling interest) by the number of ordinary shares as given in LKAS 33.

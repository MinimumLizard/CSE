FINANCIAL REVIEW

The company has reported a constant top line growth of 33% in Quarter 3 while ensuring a stable level of financial
position & profitability contributed by effective management and underwriting practices.

The Gross Written Premium for the nine months ended period ended 30th September 2025 was Rs. 6,252.84 Mn
compared to Rs. 4,694.25 Mn in 2024.

The profit before tax is Rs. 472.64 Mn for the nine months ended 30th September 2025 compared to Rs. 615.70 Mn
achieved in 2024 and the Profit after tax of the company for the period under review was recorded at Rs. 324.64 Mn
against the Rs. 398.79 Mn in the same period of the previous year.

The Motor segment was the largest contributor to the company’s GWP, accounting to 76% and reported a 43% growth
compared to the previous financial year. The Fire segment demonstrated strong growth, recording a 15% increase and
contributing 11% to the overall GWP.

Company’s claims cost decreased by 6% to Rs. 2,195.25 Mn for the nine months, while operating expenses increased by
28% to Rs. 1,512.04 Mn from Rs. 1,183.99 Mn in 2024, driven by rising salary costs due to expansion of direct sales unit
, technology maintenance and increased shared cost.

Underwriting Loss of the Company increased by 12% to Rs. 349.75 Mn in 2025 compared to Rs. 313.50 Mn in 2024
mainly due to the second year impact motor SRCC & TC 100% cession to NITF.
Investment and other income which comprises interest & dividend income, net fair value gains/ losses and other income,
decreased by 11% to Rs. 822.39 Mn in 2025 reflecting the impact of fluctuating market conditions and lower investment
returns.

The Company’s total asset base expanded by 10% to Rs. 13.95 Bn as of 30th September 2025, compared to Rs. 12.69 Bn
in the previous year while financial investments grew by 8%, reflecting the Company’s proactive approach to managing
cash flows and optimizing investment returns.

Total shareholders' equity increased to Rs. 5,944.89 Mn in 2025, reflecting a 5% growth compared to Rs. 5,637.95 Mn in
2024.

Cash equivalents increased by Rs. 36.58 Mn in 2025 Compared to previous year.

The Company is committed in driving sustainable growth while maximizing long term stakeholder expectation.

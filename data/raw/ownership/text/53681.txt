1.   Basis of preparation			

     The Financial Statements of the company and those consolidated with such for the interim period have been prepared on the same basis as
     the most recent audited financial statements and are in compliance with the Sri Lanka Accounting Standards - LKAS 34 - Interim Financial
     Reporting.			

     The accounting policies have been consistently applied by the Group and are consistent with those used in the previous financial year. The
     presentation and classification of the financial statements of the previous period have been adjusted where relevant, for better presentation and to
     be comparable with current reporting.



2.   Dividends paid

     The final ordinary dividend of Rs. 4.00 per share for the year ended 31st March 2026 was paid in accordance with the listing rules of the Colombo
     Stock Exchange after it was approved at the Annual General Meeting on the 30th June 2026. Total dividend paid was Rs. 1,623,984,180.



3.   Fair value measurement and related fair value disclosures

     As at reporting date there were no significant differences in the comparison made between the fair value and carrying value of financial assets
     and liabilities, from that which was disclosed in the annual report for the year ending 31st March 2026. There were no transfers between different
     levels of fair value hierarchy or changes to assets classes classified on recurring and non-recurring basis. Valuation techniques or significant
     unobservable inputs used for measuring level 2 and level 3 fair values remains unchanged from 31st March 2026.

     During the period under review there were no transfers in and out of level 3 financial instrument measurement and any change in fair values are
     captured in the statement of other comprehensive income.

     Unquoted equity securities that are subjected to level 3 of fair value measurement hierarchy, were revalued as at the reporting date. Fair value
     would not significantly vary if one or more unobservable inputs used for valuation were changed.



4.   Contingencies, capital and other commitments                                               			

     There were no liabilities for management fees or any other similar expenditure not provided for in the interim financial statements.

     During the period under review there were no material change in the nature of contingencies and other commitments, which were disclosed in the
     annual report for the year ended 31st March 2026.



5.   Issue of Debentures                                                                        			

     During the quarter ended 30th September 2026, Aitken Spence Hotel Holdings PLC, a subsidiary company of the Group issued 50 million Listed
     Rated Unsecured Senior Redeemable Debentures with a face value of LKR 100/- each, at par, aggregating to a total value of LKR 5 billion. The
     Debentures were allotted on the Colombo Stock Exchange on 24th September 2026.



6.   Events after the reporting period                                                          			

     Subsequent to the reporting date, on 03rd November 2026, the Company completed the disposal of its entire equity interest in a subsidiary
     operating within the Apparel segment of the Strategic Investments sector pursuant to a Share Purchase Agreement. Upon completion of the
     transaction, the entity ceased to be a subsidiary of the Group. The disposal was completed after the reporting date and has been treated as a non-
     adjusting event. Accordingly, no adjustments relating to the disposal have been made to these interim financial statements.

     There were no other material events that occurred after the reporting date that require adjustments to or disclosure in the financial statements.

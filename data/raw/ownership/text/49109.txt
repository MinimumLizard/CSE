CEO’S MESSAGE



Dear Esteemed Stakeholders,

Sarvodaya Development Finance PLC (SDF) is pleased to present its Interim Financial Statements for the six months ended
30 September 2025, reflecting a period of exceptional growth, enhanced operational efficiency, and strategic progress. This
performance reaffirms the strength of our business model, one that seamlessly integrates financial resilience with purpose-driven
impact, demonstrating our ability to generate sustainable returns while advancing meaningful social and economic development
across the communities we serve.

For the first half of the financial year 2025/26, SDF achieved a record-breaking financial performance, demonstrating strong
momentum that significantly outpaced both sectoral performances and the Company’s own performances reported in the
corresponding period of the previous year. Net profit surged to LKR 340.5 million, marking an impressive 57.8% increase compared
to the first half of 2024/25. This achievement represents 72% of the total profit recorded for the full financial year 2024/25 (LKR 470.5
million). This robust profitability was underpinned by a 43.6% growth in gross income and a 29.8% increase in net interest income,
reflecting the effectiveness of SDF’s prudent management practices and disciplined approach to operational excellence. The
Company’s continued emphasis on efficient capital deployment resulted in an annualized Return on Equity (ROE) of 16.9%, a notable
improvement from 12.3% reported in the same period of the previous year.

SDF’s financial position demonstrated notable strengthening, underscoring the Company’s capacity for continued, sustainable
growth. As of September 30, 2025, total assets stood at LKR 28.3 billion, representing a 25.6% increase over the position at the end
of the 2024/25 financial year. This expansion was driven by a 25.3% growth in the lending portfolio, reflecting a net increase of LKR
4,860.9 million. To support this business growth, the deposit base expanded by 2.7% to reach LKR 10.1 billion, while borrowings
amounted to LKR 12.9 billion. SDF continues to uphold strong institutional stability, with a Total Capital Ratio (Tier 2) of 16.5%,
comfortably exceeding the minimum regulatory requirement of 12.5%.

Our financial achievements are a direct reflection of the successful alignment between our strategic execution and the visionary
philosophy of the late Dr. A.T. Ariyaratne and the principles of the Sarvodaya Shramadana Movement. SDF remains committed to
leveraging its legacy as Sri Lanka’s oldest development finance institution that catalyzes rural-centric Agri-economic transformation.
With over 82% of our branches situated outside the Western Province, our core focus remains on financial inclusion, empowering
rural community, women, grassroots entrepreneurship in underserved rural economies. Our commitment to ethical, sustainable
finance continues to be recognized globally, exemplified by our full membership of the Global Alliance for Banking on Values (GABV).

Moreover, to further strengthen our capital base and support future growth, SDF successfully issued Sri Lanka’s first sustainable
bonds on October 8, 2025, a Rs. 2 billion Tier 2 listed, rated, unsecured, subordinated, redeemable, high yield, that was significantly
oversubscribed on the same day. This landmark issuance reflects the strong confidence investors place in SDF’s financial stability
and strategic direction, while underscoring our steadfast commitment to contributing to Sri Lanka’s sustainable development
roadmap.

Being thankful to all our stakeholders for the trust placed on us, we look ahead with unwavering focus on deepening our impact,
advancing our sustainable growth, and building enduring value for the communities we serve.

Sincerely,

3. FINANCIAL REPORTING BY SEGMENT									
For management purposes, the Company is organised into business units based on its products and services and has three reportable
segments, as follows:									
									
Lease									
This segment includes Leasing products offered to the customers.								
							
Loan									
This segment includes SME, Micro, Personal and Gold loans offered to the customers.							
							
Other Business									
This segment includes all other business activities that the Company engaged other than above segments.					
								
The Management Committee monitors the operating results of its business units separately for the purpose of making decisions about
resource allocation and performance assessment. Segment performance is evaluated based on profit or loss and is measured consistently
with profit or loss in the financial statements. Also, the Company’s financing (including finance costs and finance income) and income taxes
are managed on a Company basis and are not allocated to operating segments.							
										
Transfer prices between operating segments are on an arm’s length basis in a manner similar to transactions with third parties.

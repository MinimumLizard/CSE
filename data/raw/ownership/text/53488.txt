QUARTERLY REVIEW for the third quarter ended 30 September 2026
The Group’s revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was Rs. 8,506.9Mn, compared to Rs. 7,323.8Mn
during the same quarter of 2025, representing an increase of Rs. 1,183.1Mn or 16%. The Group's
revenue for the first nine months of 2026 was Rs. 24,432.8Mn, compared to Rs. 21,982.7Mn during the
first nine months of 2025, representing an increase of Rs. 2,450.1Mn or 11%.

The Group reported a profit after tax of Rs. 1,062.7Mn for the third quarter of 2026, compared to
Rs. 1,138.8Mn during the same quarter of 2025, representing a decrease of Rs. 76.1Mn or 7%. In
contrast, the Group reported a profit after tax of Rs. 3,273.8Mn for the first nine months of 2026,
compared to Rs. 2,801.6Mn during the first nine months of 2025, representing an increase of
Rs. 472.2Mn or 17%.


The Group reported a notable increase in revenue during the quarter under review, primarily driven by
higher demand for end product chicken meat amid limited availability of substitute products and
increased demand for Broiler Day Old Chicks (DOCs). Revenue from feed also improved, supported by
price revision; however, feed sales volume remained constrained by intense market competition,
particularly as more farmers shifted towards self-mixing feed to benefit from cost advantages.


Despite the growth in revenue, the Group's profitability for the quarter declined compared to the
corresponding quarter of the previous year, amid challenging geopolitical conditions that contributed
to rising raw material costs due to logistical and supply constraints. These pressures were further
intensified by delays in the issuance of maize import permits and an increase in the Special Commodity
Levy on maize from Rs. 25 to Rs. 50 per kilogram. Although feed prices were revised upwards, the
adjustment were insufficient to fully offset the increase in raw material costs. Consequently, the higher
feed costs adversely affected overall profitability, offsetting the improved performance of the chicken
and Broiler DOC segments.


The Company and the Group have adhered to the Sri Lanka Financial Reporting Standards in the
preparation of the interim financial statements for the period ended 30 September 2026.


There have been no other events subsequent to the interim period which require disclosure in the
interim financial statements

NOTES TO THE INTERIM FINANCIAL STATEMENTS

  1. CORPORATE INFORMATION
  Ceylon Grain Elevators PLC (the “Company”) is a “Quoted Public Company” with limited liability,
  incorporated and domiciled in Sri Lanka. The address of the Company’s registered office is No.15, Rock House
  Lane, Colombo - 15, Sri Lanka.

  The principal businesses of the Group are manufacturing and selling of poultry feed and other animal feed,
  importing and selling of poultry equipment, drugs and vaccines, operating of poultry breeder farms, raising
  grandparent and parent stock and hatcheries, hatching and selling of Day Old Chicks, operation of commercial
  farms, poultry processing and distribution and provision of warehouse facilities.

  The Company was listed in the Colombo Stock Exchange under Manufacturing Sector. Prima Limited,
  Singapore, holds 45.45% of the issued share capital of the Company.

  2. APPROVAL OF FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
  The interim financial statements of the Company and the Group for the nine months ended 30 September 2026
  are unaudited and authorised for issue by the Board of Directors on 10 November 2026.

  3. BASIS OF PREPARATION
  The interim financial statements of the Company and the Group have been prepared in compliance with Sri
  Lanka Accounting Standard (LKAS) 34 - Interim Financial Reporting.

  The interim financial statements have been prepared on a historical cost basis except for defined benefit
  obligation and biological assets.

  4. ACCOUNTING POLICIES

  The accounting policies adopted are consistent with those of the previous financial year.

  5. CONTINGENT LIABILITIES
  There has not been any significant change in the nature of the contingent liabilities, which were disclosed in
  the Annual Report for the year ended 31 December 2025.

  6. EVENTS AFTER THE REPORTING PERIOD

  Subsequent to 30 September 2026, there are no events which require adjustments to or disclosure in the
  financial statements


  7. COMPARATIVE INFORMATION

  The presentation and classification of the financial statements of the previous period have been adjusted
  where relevant, for better presentation and to be comparable with current reporting.

     Chief Executive Officer’s Review
     ___________________________________________________________________


     Dear Shareholders,

     We are pleased to present the financial results for the three months ending 30 September 2025.

     hSenid Business Solutions PLC (hBS) reported total revenue of LKR 516.9 million for the second
     quarter of FY2026, marking a strong 23 percent year-over-year growth in both LKR and USD constant
     currency terms. This performance also reflects robust 10 percent quarter-over-quarter growth (9 percent
     in USD constant currency terms), demonstrating sustained momentum in our business trajectory.
     Recurring revenues continued to strengthen, accounting for 72 percent of total revenue (up from 66
     percent in Q2 FY25), underscoring the quality and predictability of our revenue base.

     Exit Annualized Recurring Revenue (ARR) exceeded USD 4.9 million at the end of the quarter, as the
     Company approached the break-even point in terms of operational profitability on a normalized basis,
     adjusting for one-off staff restructuring expenses. The Company recorded a net loss of LKR 26 million
     for the quarter with a normalized EBITDA margin for the quarter at 11 percent, more than quadrupling
     the 2.5 percent margin achieved in Q1 FY26. Normalized EBIT margin approached the break-even
     point reflecting the fact that recurring revenues are now adequate to cover the fixed cost base; a key
     milestone in our journey toward sustained profitability. As a SaaS company, this means that a significant
     portion of every additional dollar of new sales generated from now on flows through to the bottom line.
     This gives us the ability to generate significant growth in profitability in the coming quarters,
     demonstrating the scalability of operations and non-linearity of financial gains.

     PeoplesHR Cloud remained the cornerstone of our business performance, generating LKR 368 million
     during the quarter, reflecting a 34 percent year-over-year growth in LKR terms (35 percent in USD
     constant currency terms) and a 19 percent quarter-over-quarter growth. Recurring subscription
     revenues accounted for 84 percent of PeoplesHR Cloud revenue, demonstrating the strength and
     stability of our cloud business model.

     New deal closures for the quarter totaled USD 381,931, with 76 percent attributable to PeoplesHR
     Cloud. This represents a strong 48 percent year-over-year increase, though we experienced a 14
     percent quarter-over-quarter decline. We remain focused on strengthening our sales pipeline,
     particularly in the Southeast Asian market, to drive consistent deal closure momentum in the quarters
     ahead.

     The Company continued to maintain positive free cash flow during the quarter on a normalized basis,
     adjusting for one-off staff restructuring expenses. The adjusted free cash flow to firm (FCFF) margin
     stood at 5 percent, reinforcing the health of our operations and financial discipline.

     Looking ahead, we are excited about several key product initiatives that are set to enhance our
     competitive positioning. PeoplesHR X (Version 10), the latest major release of our platform scheduled
     to launch shortly, comes with AI-powered features branded as Lexi, which includes an AI Navigation
     Agent to improve product discoverability and an AI Super-Agent that can execute and/or assist in
     performing tasks on the system. These innovations are designed to deliver enhanced user experiences
     and drive productivity gains for our customers by bringing Agentic AI capabilities to the product,
     strengthening our value proposition in an increasingly competitive market.

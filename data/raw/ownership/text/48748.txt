      Eastern Merchants PLC (the Company) is a Public Limited Liability Company incorporated and domiciled in
      Sri Lanka and listed on the Colombo Stock Exchange. The registered office and the principal place of the
      Business of the Company are located at 240, Torrington Avenue, Colombo 07.

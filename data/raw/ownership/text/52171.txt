QUARTERLY REVIEW for the second quarter ended 30 June 2026
The Group’s revenue for the second quarter of 2026 was Rs. 7,864.6Mn, compared to
Rs. 6,941.5Mn during the same quarter of 2025, representing an increase of Rs. 923.1Mn or 13%.
The Group's revenue for the first half of 2026 was Rs. 15,925.8Mn, compared to Rs. 14,658.9Mn
during the first half of 2025, representing an increase of Rs. 1,266.9Mn or 9%.


The Group reported a profit of Rs. 1,059.1Mn for the second quarter of 2026, compared to a profit
of Rs. 702.4Mn during the same quarter of 2025, representing an increase of Rs. 356.7Mn or 51%.
The Group reported a profit of Rs. 2,211.1Mn for the first half of 2026, compared to a profit of
Rs. 1,662.7Mn during the first half of 2025, representing an increase of Rs. 548.4Mn or 33%.

The increase in revenue was primarily driven by sustained demand for processed chicken and
improved demand for Broiler Day-Old Chicks (DOCs). However, revenue from feed segment
declined marginally, mainly due to farmers shifting from compounded feed to self-mixed feed,
driven by perceived cost advantage of the latter and increased availability of locally sourced raw
materials.

The Group reported improved profitability during the quarter under review, primarily attributed
to upward revision of average selling prices in response to cost escalations. However, profit
margins were challenged by prevailing geopolitical uncertainties, as a result of higher costs of
imported raw materials, freight, inward logistics and energy. Despite these challenges, the Group
successfully mitigated the cost pressures through various process optimization initiatives, resulting
in an overall improvement in profitability.


The Company and the Group have adhered to the Sri Lanka Financial Reporting Standards in the
preparation of the interim financial statements for the period ended 30 June 2026.


There have been no other events subsequent to the interim period which require disclosure in the
interim financial statements.

NOTES TO THE INTERIM FINANCIAL STATEMENTS

   1. CORPORATE INFORMATION
  Ceylon Grain Elevators PLC (the “Company”) is a “Quoted Public Company” with limited liability,
  incorporated and domiciled in Sri Lanka. The address of the Company’s registered office is No.15, Rock House
  Lane, Colombo - 15, Sri Lanka.

  The principal businesses of the Group are manufacturing and selling of poultry feed and other animal feed,
  importing and selling of poultry equipment, drugs and vaccines, operating of poultry breeder farms, raising
  grandparent and parent stock and hatcheries, hatching and selling of Day Old Chicks, operation of commercial
  farms, poultry processing and distribution and provision of warehouse facilities.

  The Company was listed in the Colombo Stock Exchange under Manufacturing Sector. Prima Limited,
  Singapore, holds 45.45% of the issued share capital of the Company.

   2. APPROVAL OF FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
  The interim financial statements of the Company and the Group for the six months ended 30 June 2026 are
  unaudited and authorised for issue by the Board of Directors on 31 July 2026.

   3. BASIS OF PREPARATION
  The interim financial statements of the Company and the Group have been prepared in compliance with Sri
  Lanka Accounting Standard (LKAS) 34 - Interim Financial Reporting.

  The interim financial statements have been prepared on a historical cost basis except for defined benefit
  obligation and biological assets.

   4. ACCOUNTING POLICIES

  The accounting policies adopted are consistent with those of the previous financial year.

   5. CONTINGENT LIABILITIES
  There has not been any significant change in the nature of the contingent liabilities, which were disclosed in
  the Annual Report for the year ended 31 December 2025.

   6. EVENTS AFTER THE REPORTING PERIOD

  Subsequent to 30 June 2026, there are no events which require adjustments to or disclosure in the financial
  statements.

   7. COMPARATIVE INFORMATION

  The presentation and classification of the financial statements of the previous period have been adjusted
  where relevant, for better presentation and to be comparable with current reporting.

   8. NM – Change in percentage term is Not Meaningful.

1. Corporate Information
Royal Ceramics Lanka PLC (“the Company”) is a public limited liability company incorporated and domiciled in Sri Lanka and listed on the
Colombo Stock Exchange. The registered office of the Company is located at No.20, R.A. De Mel Mawatha, Colombo 03.

Company) and its subsidiaries (together referred as the “Group”), namely Rocell Bathware Limited, Ever Paint and Chemical Industries (Private)
Limited, Biscuits and Chocolate Company Limited, Nilano Garments (Private) Limited, Rocell Properties Limited, CP Holding (Private) Limited,

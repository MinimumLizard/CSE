                                               EXPLANATORY NOTES

1)   Corporate Information

     Mahaweli Coconut Plantations PLC is a Listed Public Limited Liability Company, incorporated on 29th October
     1996 under the Companies Act No. 17 of 1982 and domiciled in Sri Lanka. The Company was re– registered
     under the Companies Act No. 07 of 2007 on 06th February 2008 and listed in Colombo Stock Exchange Dirisavi
     Board on 03rd June 2019 . (Registration No. (PB) 191 PQ). The registered office of the Company is located at
     No. 1/82, Kimbulapitiya Road, Bolawalana, Negombo. The Coconut Plantation is on lease land obtained from
     the Mahaweli Authority of Sri Lanka and is situated in Mahaweli System B Pimburettewa. Aralaganvila.

2)   Principal Business Activities and Nature of Operations

     During the period, the Principal Activity of the Company was Cultivation & Sale of Coconuts, Copra, Coco Peat,
     Coco Chips and Coir

3)   Approval of Interim Financial Statement

     The Interim Financial Statements of the Company for the nine months period ended 31st December 2025 were
     authorised to be published by the Board of Directors on 12.02.2026.

     Accounting Standards (SLFRS / LKAS).

QUARTERLY REVIEW for the second quarter ended 30 June 2026

The amalgamated Company’s revenue for the second quarter of 2026 was Rs. 1,862.1Mn compared
to Rs. 1,231.5Mn reported by the Group during the same quarter of 2025, representing an increase
of Rs. 630.6Mn or 51%. The amalgamated Company’s revenue for the first half of 2026 was
Rs. 3,520.6Mn compared to Rs. 2,703.3Mn reported by the Group during the first half of 2025,
representing an increase of Rs. 817.3Mn or 30%.


The amalgamated Company reported a profit of Rs. 511.0Mn for the second quarter of 2026
compared to Rs. 108.6Mn reported by the the Group during the same quarter of 2025, representing
an increase Rs. 402.4Mn or 370%. The amalgamated Company reported a profit of Rs. 925.1Mn for
the first half of 2026 compared to Rs. 458.8Mn reported by the Group during the first half of 2025,
representing an increase Rs. 466.3Mn or 102%.

The Group recorded strong revenue growth during the quarter under review compared with the
corresponding quarter of the previous year. This performance was primarily driven by higher
demand for Broiler Day-Old Chicks (DOCs), supported by consistent product quality and
favourable market prices, arising from strong demand for the end product processed chicken. The
Group's profitability improved significantly during the quarter, mainly due to both higher sales
volumes and average selling prices for Broiler DOCs, which outweighed the increased operating
costs arising from challenging macro-economic conditions including high inflation, exchange rate
depreciation, higher raw material prices and increased energy costs.

The Company have complied with the Sri Lanka Financial Reporting Standards in the preparation
of the Interim Financial Statements for the period ended 30 June 2026.


There have been no other events subsequent to the interim period which require disclosure in the
Interim Financial Statements.

NOTES TO THE INTERIM FINANCIAL STATEMENTS

1. CORPORATE INFORMATION
Three Acre Farms PLC (the “Company”) is a “Quoted Public Company” with limited liability,
incorporated and domiciled in Sri Lanka. The address of the Company’s registered office is No. 15, Rock
House Lane, Colombo 15, Sri Lanka.

The principal businesses of the Company is selective breeding, hatching, sale of Commercial Day Old
Chicks (DOCs), both Broiler (for chicken meat) and Layer (for the production of table eggs), grandparent
farm operation and commercial broiler farming.

The Company was listed in the Colombo Stock Exchange under the Food and Beverage Sector. The parent
company, Ceylon Grain Elevators PLC holds 57.21% of the issued share capital of the Company.

2. APPROVAL OF FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
The interim financial statements of the Company for the six months ended 30 June 2026 are unaudited and
authorised for issue by the Board of Directors on 31 July 2026.

3. BASIS OF PREPARATION
The interim financial statements of the Company has been prepared in compliance with Sri Lanka Accounting
Standard (LKAS) 34 - Interim Financial Reporting.

The interim financial statements have been prepared on a historical cost basis except for defined benefit
obligation and biological assets.

4. ACCOUNTING POLICIES
The accounting policies adopted are consistent with those of the previous financial year.


5. CONTINGENT LIABILITIES
There has not been any significant change in the nature of the contingent liabilities, which were disclosed in the
Annual Report for the year ended 31 December 2025.


6. EVENTS AFTER THE REPORTING PERIOD
Subsequent to 30 June 2026, there are no events which require adjustments to or disclosure in the financial
statements,


7. COMPARATIVE INFORMATION
The presentation and classification of the financial statements of the previous period have been adjusted, where
relevant, to improve comparability with the current period.

As approved by the Board of Directors at the meeting held on 12 November 2025, Millennium Multibreeder
Farms (Pvt) Limited and Ceylon Pioneer Poultry Breeders Limited were amalgamated with the Company (Three
Acre Farms PLC) with effect from 1 January 2026, and the Company continues as the amalgamated entity.
Accordingly, the current year reflects the results of the amalgamated Company, while the comparative period
presents the Group results for better comparability.


8. NM – Changes in percentage term is Not Meaningful.

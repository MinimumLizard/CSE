FINANCIAL REVIEW


Cable Solutions PLC commenced FY2026/27 with a strong revenue performance, supported by
sustained demand across its export-oriented customer base despite a challenging operating
environment marked by elevated raw material prices and increasing labour costs.
Group revenue for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 amounted to LKR 988 million, representing an
11% year-on-year increase compared to LKR 892 million in the corresponding quarter of the
previous year. Growth was mainly driven by higher export and deemed export sales, supported by
new customer acquisitions and increased order volumes from existing customers.

Despite the strong topline performance, profitability came under pressure as higher copper prices
and manufacturing costs outpaced revenue growth. Consequently, gross profit declined to LKR
276 million, while the gross profit margin reduced to 27.9% from 32.6% in Q1 FY2025/26.

Group EBIT declined to LKR 127 million from LKR 170 million, reflecting higher administrative
expenses arising from salary revisions, operational expansion, and inflationary cost pressures. Net
profit after tax amounted to LKR 99 million, representing a 26% year-on-year decline, although
the Group continued to maintain a healthy balance sheet and prudent capital structure.

The India operation continued to expand its customer base and production capabilities during the
quarter. While the subsidiary remains in its growth phase, management expects operational
efficiencies and improved capacity utilization to enhance future performance.

From macroeconomic perspective, global copper prices remained elevated during the quarter,
continuing to place pressure on manufacturing costs. However, demand across renewable energy,
industrial automation, telecommunications, and infrastructure sectors remained supportive of the
Group's long-term growth outlook.

Looking ahead, management remains focused on improving operational efficiencies, strengthening
export market penetration, and expanding its value-added product portfolio to deliver sustainable
long-term growth.

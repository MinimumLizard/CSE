       The following material event occurred after the reporting date of 30 September 2026 and has been disclosed in the financial statements.
       Case No. CA TAX 23/2022

       Reference is made to the above-mentioned case, as disclosed under the Note 30.1.1, Provisions, Commitments and Contingencies of the Annual Report for the financial year 2025/26.

       The Department of Inland Revenue had raised a PAYE tax assessment against the Company for the Year of Assessment 2011/12, amounting to LKR 634,686,195, together with penalties of
       LKR 317,343,097, in respect of shares gifted by three shareholders of the Company to employees and external parties.

       The Company challenged the determination of the Tax Appeals Commission dated 7 April 2022 before the Court of Appeal under Case No. CA TAX 23/2022. The Court of Appeal delivered

       958 share transferees.

       The Company intends to appeal the judgment of the Court of Appeal to the Supreme Court of Sri Lanka and pursue further legal proceedings in this regard. The Company is currently
       consulting with its legal advisers on the matter.

       Reference is made to the above-mentioned case, as disclosed under the Note 30.1.1, Provisions, Commitments and Contingencies of the Annual Report for the financial year 2025/26.
       In this case W.U. Seneviratne & Co. (Pvt) Ltd. and three other Plaintiffs filed an action against Access Engineering PLC (1st Defendant) and W.U.S. Logistics (Pvt) Ltd. (2nd Defendant),

       counterclaims, with the proceedings to be dismissed without costs. The necessary provision has been recognised in the Group’s financial statements for the period ended 30 September 2026.

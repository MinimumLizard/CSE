1.   BASIS OF PREPARATION OF INTERIM FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
     The Interim Financial Statements of Pegasus Hotels of Ceylon PLC (the "Company") have been prepared using the
     same accounting policies and methods as the audited financial statements for the year ended 31st March 2026,
     and are in compliance with Sri Lanka Accounting Standard LKAS 34 - "Interim Financial Reporting". The
     provisions of the Companies Act, No. 7 of 2007 have been considered in preparing these Interim Financial
     Statements.
2.   EVENTS AFTER THE REPORTING PERIOD
     Subsequent to the reporting period, no circumstances have arisen which would require adjustments to or
     disclosure in the financial statements, other than those disclosed in Note 7.1.
3.   COMMITMENTS AND CONTINGENCIES
3.1 Capital commitments
    The Company had capital commitments of Rs. 16,779,458/- as at the reporting date.
3.2 Contingent liabilities
    The Company does not have material contingent liabilities as at the reporting date.
4.   LIABILITIES FOR EXPENDITURE
     Significant expenses and support service fees for the period have been fully accounted for in the interim financial
     statements.

5.   COMPARATIVE FIGURES
     Previous period's figures and phrases are re-presented where necessary to conform to the current period's
     presentation.

6.   GOING CONCERN
     Based on currently available information, the directors are satisfied that the Company will continue to operate as
     a going concern for the foreseeable future.

7.   STATED CAPITAL
     The stated capital of the Company as at the reporting date was Rs. 751,548,321.50 consisting of 42,210,470
     Ordinary Shares.

7.1 Sub-Division of Existing Ordinary Shares
    As disclosed to the Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE) on 21st July 2026, the Board of Directors resolved to
    recommend to shareholders a Sub-Division of the Company's Ordinary Shares. Under the proposed Sub-Division,
    every existing Ordinary Share (01 Share) will be subdivided into two (02) Ordinary Shares. Accordingly, the
    number of issued Ordinary Shares will increase from 42,210,470 to 84,420,940. The Company's Stated Capital will
    remain unchanged at Rs. 751,548,321.50. The proposed Sub-Division of shares received the concurrence of the
    CSE on 12th August 2026 and is subject to shareholder approval at an Extraordinary General Meeting.

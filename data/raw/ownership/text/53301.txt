     Chief Executive Officer’s Review
     ___________________________________________________________________

     Dear Shareholders,

     We are pleased to present the financial results for the three months ended 30 September 2026.

     hSenid Business Solutions PLC (hBS) reported total revenue of LKR 598.6 million for the second quarter
     of FY2027, reflecting year-over-year growth of 16 percent in LKR terms. Revenue was modestly lower by
     1 percent on a sequential quarter-on-quarter basis, reflecting softer OnPrem and payroll outsourcing
     revenues, partially offset by higher sales of tracking devices.

     Gross profit increased 25 percent year-over-year to LKR 300.3 million, resulting in a gross profit margin of
     50 percent. The margin reflected a higher contribution from lower-margin tracking-device sales during the
     quarter. Operating performance remained solid, with EBITDA excluding foreign exchange losses reaching
     LKR 72.0 million, representing year-over-year growth of 141 percent and an EBITDA margin (excluding
     forex losses) of 12 percent. hBS recorded a foreign exchange loss of LKR 15.3 million owing to the
     appreciation of the LKR, resulting in a net loss of LKR 9.3 million for the quarter. Operating cash flow
     returned to positive levels, reaching LKR 70.6 million for the quarter.

     During the quarter, we continued to strengthen our go-to-market execution across our focus markets
     through targeted customer engagement and partner collaboration. We also advanced our Lexi AI
     capabilities, progressing towards our vision of a comprehensive Human Capital Operating System through
     more integrated AI functionality and improved workforce insights. Alongside these developments, we
     enhanced core platform functionality to streamline HR and payroll processes, improve operational
     efficiency, and deliver greater value to customers across our markets.

     During the period, hBS was honoured with the Best Investor Relations award at the CFA Society Sri Lanka
     Capital Market Awards 2026 in the category for listed companies with a market capitalisation of less than
     LKR 10.0 billion. This marks the third consecutive year hBS has received the award, reflecting our
     commitment to transparent and effective stakeholder communication.

     Looking ahead, we will continue to focus on driving business growth, deepening customer adoption of our
     AI capabilities, and strengthening operational efficiency. We remain attentive to the pace of new deal
     closures and to the mix of revenue between higher-margin cloud product and other revenue lines. Through
     disciplined execution and sustained investment in product development, we aim to build a stronger
     foundation for long-term growth, enhance customer value, and create sustainable returns for our
     shareholders.

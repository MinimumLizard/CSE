Chairman & Chief Executive Officer’s Review

Dear Valued Shareholders,

It is with great pride and confidence that I present the consolidated financial performance of Kapruka Holdings PLC for
the quarter ended 31 December 2025. The results clearly demonstrate the Group’s resilience, strategic execution, and
accelerating momentum toward sustainable, long-term growth.

In a challenging operating environment, Kapruka delivered a strong top-line performance, recording revenue of LKR
516.44 million, reflecting a solid 21% year-on-year growth. Gross profit increased by an impressive 44% to LKR
202.62 million, supported by improved operational efficiencies, disciplined cost management, and an increasingly
favourable sales mix.


This performance resulted in a 138% improvement in operating results, delivering an operating profit of LKR
12.25 million, compared to an operating loss of LKR 31.88 million in the corresponding period last year. Notably, the
Group achieved a 180% turnaround at the Earnings Before Taxation level, recording a profit of LKR 19.99 million,
compared to a loss of LKR 24.92 million in the prior year. This decisive return to profitability underscores the
scalability of our business model and the effectiveness of our strategic execution.

Strategic Drivers Three key engines drove this performance:

• Kapruka Partner Central (Scaling Efficiently): Our transition to a marketplace model is working. By onboarding
   third-party sellers, we are expanding our catalog into niche categories without tying up capital in inventory. This
   "asset-light" approach is the primary reason for our improved margins and return on capital.

• The Services Platform (New Market Entry): We have expanded beyond physical goods into the service economy.
   Inspired by models like TaskRabbit but built for Sri Lanka, this platform connects customers with verified providers
   for needs like IT support. This allows us to monetize our existing customer base in an entirely new way.


• Kapruka Cross Border (USD Revenue): Our role as an e-distributor for Sri Lankan brands on Amazon (US, UK,
   Canada) is now a mature, dollar-generating vertical. We are managing the end-to-end value chain - logistics,
   compliance, and listings - turning local brands into global exporters while securing vital hard currency revenue for
   the Group.

Outlook We enter 2026 with a stronger balance sheet, a diversified revenue mix, and a growing ecosystem of partners.
We are no longer just a retailer; we are an enabler of commerce - both local and global. Thank you for your continued
trust in this vision.

Sincerely,

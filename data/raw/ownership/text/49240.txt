NOTES TO THE INTERIM FINANCIAL STATEMENTS FOR THE SIX MONTHS ENDED 3OTH SEPTEMBER 2025



1. Principal Activities and Nature of Operations
    Jetwing Symphony PLC is the holding company that owns, directly and indirectly, investments in a number of companies
    that are involved in the business of hoteliering.



2. Approval of Financial Statements
    The interim financial statements of the Group and the Company for the six months ended 30th September 2025, were authorized
    for issue by the Board of Directors on 6th November 2025.


3. Basis of Preparation
    The interim financial statements have been prepared in accordance with the Sri Lanka Accounting Standard (LKAS) 34- Interim
    financial reporting.

4. Changes in Accounting Policies
     These interim financial statements have been prepared in accordance with the accounting policies set out in the Annual
     Report for the year ended 3l't March 2025.

5. Fair value measurement and related fair value disclosures
    The fair values of all the financial assets and financial liabilities recognized dunng the quarter were not materially different
    from the transaction prices at the date of initial recognition. There were no transfers between Level I and Level 2 and no
    transfers into or out of Level 3 categories as per the fair value hierarchy, during the period. The fair value changes on financial
    instruments in Level 3 category was properly recorded in the statement of other comprehensive income and there were no
    purchases and/or disposals during the period.


6. Contingent Liabilities
    There are no significant contingent liabilities as at 30fr September 2025, other than the following:


    Local authorities have claimed a fee amounting 1o lo/o of turnover for the issuance of trade license for the years 2021 , 2022,
    2023,2024 812025. The Group does not agree with such a claim. The estimated contingent liability as of 30th September 2025
    is Rs. 40 Mn. At the moment, three cases are pending at Magistrate's courts in Kandy, two cases at Tissamaharama, one case
    at Colombo & Dambulla respectively. Other than that, two cases are pending at Provincial High Courts in Kandy.



7. Information for the six months ended 30th September 2024 has been reclassified to conform with the current presentation where
   applicable.

8. There are no distinguishable components to be identified as segments for the Company.

9. Events after the reporting period

     No circumstances have arisen since the reporting date, which would require adjustments to or disclosure in the Financial
     Statements.
